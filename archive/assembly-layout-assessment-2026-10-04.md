Reviewed 4 October 2026. Scope: the current ten section sources, their displayed wording, the accepted v08 background, and the local assessment brief/rubric. This is an assessment and production recommendation, not approval of replacement copy, a new background or the final poster.

## Finding

The agreed 4/4/2 order tells a coherent marketing-plan story. The current standalone panel images are not ready to paste into that grid. The principal constraint is the shape and usable height of the cells, especially Sections 5, 7, 8, 9 and 10. Retain approved meaning and data, but compose their diagrams for those cells. Simply shrinking complete images will lose space and make important labels unreadable.

## A0 format and production route

An A0 landscape page is 1189 × 841 mm, whereas the background is approximately 16:9. At full A0 width and unchanged proportions, the image is approximately 669 mm high, leaving approximately 172 mm of page height. Stretching to fill A0 would distort the vehicle, flags and illustrations. Cropping to fill would cut the sides. Resolve the extra height in an A0 composition proof; retain the accepted background as its source rather than treating the 16:9 PNG as an A0 print master.

The intended sequence remains: **current Markdown → cell-sized infographic draft → production prompt/assets → controlled A0 composition → print → manual redraw**. The production prompt describes drawings and style; controlled copy supplies exact words, dates, numbers and charts. A single AI-generated poster image is not evidence that the assessed wording and data have survived correctly.

Background acceptance and standalone panel acceptance remain recorded. There is no final A0, print-distance proof or newly accepted replacement copy in this review. References stay internal under the student's settled citation-free-face instruction.
