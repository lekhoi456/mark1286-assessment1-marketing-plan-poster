## 5. Digital Marketing
<!-- budget: 210 -->

**Route:** Search + paid social → one local information page → Green SM app booking. The Denmark page advertises app booking. Its web page is not an evidenced checkout. <!-- E-188 -->

**PPC search:** Copenhagen taxi-intent terms in Danish and English, restricted to service area/hours after verification. Proposed media envelope: DKK108,000.

**SEO + content:** Local page maps coverage/hours, explains meter and app-fare terms, and gives the trip-reference help route. Production sits within creative/localisation: 120 h at assumed DKK600/h = DKK72,000.

**Paid social:** Frequency-capped fare/help explainers for Copenhagen adults in the verified service area. Proposed envelope: DKK90,000. National reach is not local prospect demand. <!-- E-042 -->

**Opt-in CRM/email:** After the first trip, send its reference, help route and one relevant pre-book reminder, with consent. Advance booking up to 30 days is advertised. <!-- E-016 --> The CRM/support handover line is 120 h × assumed DKK600/h = DKK72,000. The tools line is 12 × DKK2,500 = DKK30,000.

**Later awareness:** Digital Copenhagen screens only after Gate B and when 90-day repeat reaches ≥35%. Proposed 200,000 impressions × DKK191 CPM = DKK38,200, plus DKK4,995 setup, for DKK43,195. Rate-card cost excludes VAT and production. No screen rides are assumed. <!-- E-029 -->
