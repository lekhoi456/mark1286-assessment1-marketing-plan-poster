## 9. Ride Innovation
<!-- budget: 155 -->

### Verified in Copenhagen

App booking and pre-booking up to 30 days advertised. Licensed dispatch-office register: Green SM Denmark ApS. Performance untested.

### Proposed roadmap

**01 Target S1:** Repeat self-paying taxi riders, 25–44, service area. Big Data + marketing AI use consented first-party trip data for targeting and personalised reminders. Check purpose/quality/access. No bought/sensitive profiles.

**02 Dispatch AI:** Future Big Data + AI driver-task pilot, separately costed. Gate data/privacy, tech/ops checks, approval. Test booking-to-driver-match time, fulfilment, cancellations, fairness vs local baseline. Gain unproven.

**03 Connect care:** One trip reference across app, driver, car, support. Invite ratings. Human-review issues. Test chatbot FAQs with human hand-off.

**04 Test retention:** Measure frequency and contribution first. Cohort-test needs-matched prepaid/monthly bundles vs pay-per-trip. Track retention, margin. Stop if either falls.

**Feedback loop** Consent/trip data → check purpose/quality/access → target S1/care → measure match/rating/repeat → refine.

**Funding boundary:** DKK600,000 excludes app/platform build and vehicle/driver/frontline operations. AI dispatch/chatbot unpriced. Strategic subscription change. Group approval and separate costing needed.
