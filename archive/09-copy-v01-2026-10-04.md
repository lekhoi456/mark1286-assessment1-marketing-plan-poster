# Section 9 — candidate display copy

## 9. Ride Innovation
<!-- budget: 155 -->

### Proposed repeat reminder

**Illustrative message:** “Your usual Thursday ride. Pre-book it now.”

**Personalisation:** consent → rider’s own trip history → relevant reminder → intended repeat ride.

Uses the rider’s own trip history. No purchased profiles.

**Existing advertised feature:** pre-booking up to 30 days. <!-- E-016 -->

### Proposed joined journey

**One trip reference:** local info page → app → in-car help card → follow-up → help.

**Intended benefit:** less repetition when resolving a trip problem.

Both proposals need local implementation. Supports O3: repeat rides.

### Proposed usage model and limits

**Pay per trip:** usage-based charging.

**Subscription:** defer until frequent repeat is demonstrated.

**AI chatbot:** defer until a reliable human FAQ exists.

**Neuromarketing:** not selected. Ordinary surveys test clarity.
