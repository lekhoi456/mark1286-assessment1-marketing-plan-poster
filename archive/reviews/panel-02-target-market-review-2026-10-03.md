## Targeting judgement

The strongest challenge is that population size and operator testimony do not demonstrate which age group buys more taxi rides, converts better or repeats more. Unequal age-span widths also prevent treating the tallest column as proof of an optimal age target. The profile label and S1 behavioural filters keep the plan honest. This is a bounded targeting proposal for a 12-month entry campaign, not a proven market-performance result.

An optional stronger caption is **Large local age group · Existing taxi users · Repeat potential**. It is an agent recommendation, not a new adopted string. It connects the evidence and selection criteria to the business objective. The current approved caption already explains repeat opportunity and should not be silently replaced.
