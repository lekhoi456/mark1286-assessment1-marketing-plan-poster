## Precision of the proposed strengthening

The parent's proposed sentence supplies the right components. “Offers” and “backed by” need the visible proposed-position qualifier because accurate booking terms and local help are commitments in the plan rather than observed service outcomes. Retain the chosen audience's recurring local trips and confirmed-service-area condition. The worker's v02 says “proposed position” and separates the sourced operating facts from the plan inference.

The short implication “Direct fleet and driver control supports the proposed service promise” risks reading as a demonstrated causal result. The worker proposes an imperative under **Proposed delivery rationale**: “Use direct fleet and driver control to support clear booking terms and a route to local help.” This identifies an intended use of the model without claiming that ownership automatically delivers the promise.

The mechanism to defend in the pitch is one operator coordinating booking information, driver standards and local help. That is a plan rationale, not a finding in the authority PDF. It requires current app information, staffed assistance and coordination with drivers. The relevant approved resources and evaluation should be linked in the Budget, Success Dashboard and Business Goals panels and in pitch notes. Add no new KPI targets here and do not treat staffing or response performance as already observed.
