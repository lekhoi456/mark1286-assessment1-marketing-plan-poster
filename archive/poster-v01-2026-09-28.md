## Company/Business Introduction
<!-- budget: 125 -->

Green SM began in Vietnam, where its Xanh SM taxi service launched in Hanoi on 14 April 2023 (VietnamPlus, 2023a). <!-- E-002 --> Renamed Green SM in April 2026, it counted eight markets, including a Dutch pilot, as at 26 September 2026 (Green SM, 2026f; Green SM, 2026a). <!-- E-009 E-010 -->

Copenhagen, launched on 30 July 2026, is its first European market. Green SM owns the cars and employs the drivers (Green SM, 2026d; Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 174). <!-- E-008 E-019 -->

**Drivers of change:** Uber and Bolt run platform business models whose apps book cars run by other operators (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 119). <!-- E-184 --> Green SM runs its own service, and this plan markets that control.

## Target Market Analysis
<!-- budget: 180 -->

**STP model:** we segment Copenhagen taxi demand by who pays and why people ride, then use concentrated targeting on one segment.

**Priority segment:** residents of Green SM's service area who already take taxis for some regular trips and pay for them.

- **Demographic:** adults in a city of 670,389 people where 44.3% of households are one person (Statistics Denmark, 2026b; Statistics Denmark, 2026a). <!-- E-031 E-033 -->
- **Geographic:** the confirmed service area only. At launch it was Inner Copenhagen, 08:00 to 20:00 (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 175). <!-- E-182 -->
- **Behavioural:** 40-50% of recent taxi users have one taxi app and 30-40% have none. In a Capital Region subsample (n = 79), price (57%) and habit (22%) drove the last app choice (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 150, 194). <!-- E-023 E-024 -->
- **Psychographic (our hypothesis):** these riders want to know the area and fare before booking, and to get help if a trip goes wrong.

Illustrative persona, not a real person: lives alone, takes a taxi a few times a month, keeps one app out of habit and checks the price first.

## Positioning Strategy
<!-- budget: 115 -->

**Positioning statement:** for Copenhagen residents who already take taxis, Green SM is the locally run electric taxi that is easy to judge before you book and easy to get help from afterwards, because it owns its cars and employs its drivers.

**Benefit positioning, because price is level.** For a standard 10 km, 12-minute weekday trip, Green SM's published day meter gives DKK227 (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). TAXA 4x35 lists DKK230, at prices dated 1 December 2023 (TAXA 4x35, no date). <!-- E-015 E-028 -->

**Electric supports the claim but cannot lead it.** The European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association (ACEA) (2026) reports that 68.5% of new cars registered in Denmark in 2025 were battery-electric. <!-- E-039 -->

## Branding and Identity
<!-- budget: 95 -->

**Brand identity kept:** the Green SM name, the leaf-shaped logo in teal and yellow-gold, and the Danish core values "Safety - Discipline - Reliability - Professionalism - Local Respect" (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date c). <!-- E-012 -->

**Campaign line:** "Know your ride before you book." Clear area. Clear fare. A clear way to get help.

**Consistent brand messaging:** Danish first, English second. The same line and trip-reference format appear on ads, the landing page, app messages, in-car cards and street screens, so "Reliability" and "Local Respect" become a promise riders can check.

## Digital Marketing Tactics
<!-- budget: 160 -->

Each tactic serves one stage of the consumer decision-making process:

- **Need, pay-per-click:** Danish and English taxi searches in the service area and hours. DKK108,000.
- **Information, SEO and content marketing:** one local page showing the map, hours, fare rules and how to get help.
- **Evaluation, social media marketing:** short explainers on how the fare works and how help works. Social media user identities equal 78.9% of Denmark's population (Kemp, 2025a). <!-- E-042 --> DKK90,000.
- **Purchase:** DKK30 off a first ride, capped at 400 new riders.
- **Post-purchase, email marketing:** opt-in follow-up with the trip reference and a reminder to pre-book a regular trip up to 30 days ahead (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-016 -->
- **Awareness:** Digital Copenhagen street screens, 200,000 impressions at DKK191 per thousand (AFA Decaux, 2026), only after Gate B. <!-- E-029 -->

**Integrated digital marketing:** one message, one page, one trip reference, so channel results can be compared.

## Sales Strategies
<!-- budget: 130 -->

**Sales process:** for a taxi, the sale is a completed, paid trip.

1. **Lead generation:** search, social and screens bring residents to the local page.
2. **Prospecting:** installers who have not ridden get one opt-in message about the area and help route.
3. **Conversion:** the capped DKK30 voucher is the closing incentive, with no false urgency.
4. **After-sales service:** every trip has a reference, and problems reach a staffed help route.
5. **Relationship selling:** consented reminders for regular trips win the second and third ride.

**Value-based selling:** show fare rules and help, not bigger discounts. **Soft selling:** invite app-store reviews and never pay for them. Drivers are Green SM employees (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date a), <!-- E-011 --> so briefings and an in-car help card make each ride part of the sale.

## Creativity and Innovation
<!-- budget: 100 -->

**Personalisation:** with consent, the app reminds riders before a trip they take regularly and offers to pre-book it in Danish or English. It uses only the rider's own trip history.

**Omnichannel:** one trip reference links the page, app, in-car card, follow-up and help route, so no rider explains a problem twice.

**Deferred on purpose:** AI chatbots, because one wrong fare or refund answer would break the promise, and a subscription model, because the plan expects fewer than two rides per new rider in year one.

## Alignment with Business Objectives
<!-- budget: 95 -->

Green SM told Denmark's competition authority that it puts stable local operation before rapid expansion. The authority judged that becoming known would need substantial marketing investment (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 175, 179). <!-- E-020 E-021 -->

**Alignment with objectives:** spending grows only while service and repeat use hold, so marketing never outruns operations. **Long-term sustainable growth:** repeat trips, not one-off discounts, decide whether year two scales up. That serves Green SM's mission to "Provide a green, convenient, and sustainable mobility solution" (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date c). <!-- E-185 -->
