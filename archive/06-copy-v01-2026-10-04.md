## 6. From Clicks to Rides
<!-- budget: 185 -->

### Sales Strategy

### Lead

The proposed route runs from search/social to the local information page, then to an app install or account.

The website informs and directs riders to the app. Installs and accounts are leads, not sales.

### Prospect

Proposed prospecting sends one opted-in message to installers who have not ridden, with the verified service area and help route.

### First paid ride

Direct app booking → completed, paid trip = sale.

Proposed DKK30 voucher · once per new rider · cap 400.

### After-sales help

The plan includes a trip reference, staffed help route and one clarity question.

The in-car help card shows the trip reference and support route. It is never a booking card.

### Consented repeat

Relationship selling: with consent, a relevant reminder → another paid trip.

**Value-based selling:** Clear fare terms + available help.

**Soft selling:** Invite an app-store review after a resolved or well-rated trip. Never pay for it.

**Delivery check:** Brief employed drivers. Confirm help-route staffing + response standards before advertising.
