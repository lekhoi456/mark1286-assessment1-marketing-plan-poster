## 6. From Clicks to Rides
<!-- budget: 185 -->

### Sales Strategy

### Awareness

Copenhagen visibility. Installs are leads, not sales.

### Book

Book directly in the app.

Sale is a completed, paid trip.

DKK30 first-trip discount, capped at 400 riders (DKK12,000 reserve).

### Experience

Target: excellent app, driver, vehicle + support.

Verify staffed-help and operations standards first.

### Feedback

Ask post-ride feedback.

Unpaid app-store review after a resolved/well-rated trip.

### Repeat

With consent: app notifications.

Repeat voucher pilot.

Monthly bundle pilot: tailored, capped, time-limited.

Fund both separately only if observed rider frequency + unit economics fit.
