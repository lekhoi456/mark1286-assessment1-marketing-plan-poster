## Recommendation

Add at most a small **VinFast electric vehicles** label beside the vehicle if the student adopts it. This identifies the vehicle brand serving the transport business. Use plain handwriting; the Green SM logo remains the principal identity. A second manufacturer logo is unnecessary for this modest supporting detail.

Do not add Vingroup in the current composition. The introduction already supplies the business name, Vietnamese origin, launch history, international footprint, local service and fleet/driver model. The official C1 description asks for background, country of origin and core products/services; it does not expressly require a corporate parent, supplier diagram or multiple corporate logos. A VinFast label clarifies the product/service context, while a Vingroup label adds background of lower relevance to the Copenhagen rider plan. This is a content/hierarchy judgement, not a guaranteed marking effect.

If the student wants both names, a compact relationship explanation would distinguish Green SM as the service operator, VinFast as the vehicle brand and Vingroup backing as corporate context. **Vingroup-backed** is supported as the company's description. Do not turn that into an unverified current legal ownership chain, a 100% ownership claim or an assertion that all three are the same company. No such diagram or wording is selected.
