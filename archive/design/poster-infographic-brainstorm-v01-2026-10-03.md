## 2. Problem and acceptance conditions

The taxi reference is visually memorable but mainly repeats an icon-plus-bullet pattern. A coin icon does not explain allocation, and an upward arrow does not explain measurement. The Oura reference demonstrates more useful process arrows, comparisons and allocation graphics, but its crowded collage and small reference strip are not evidence of good marks or a readable A0 result.

The underlying problem is making the assessed marketing reasoning visible in the poster, while preserving the vehicle concept and making the result practical to redraw by hand.

Success means:

- A marker can locate all eleven elements and inspect the reasoning without relying on speech or hidden notes.
- Visuals communicate selection, comparison, sequence, allocation, measurement or a conditional decision.
- Verified facts, planning assumptions, proposed targets and illustrations remain distinguishable.
- Each factual visual retains the relevant source, date, denominator and scope.
- The print proof is genuinely A0 and readable at actual size; the handwritten version preserves the approved labels, figures and citations.

Confidence: **high** for the eleven-element structure and official weights; **moderate** for the recommended diagram families; **unknown** for exact physical fit before an actual-size proof.

## 4. Proposed composition and heading treatment

Use A0 landscape, 1189 × 841 mm. Keep a recognisable car silhouette around white analytical surfaces, rather than forcing text into literal windows, wheels or headlights. A simplified near-side view offers more writing space than dramatic perspective. The body is a graphic cutaway, so proportion can be stylised without turning it into a different vehicle model.

Outer composition:

1. Header: title, subtitle, tagline and clearly labelled Student/Business Details strip.
2. Background: restrained Copenhagen vignette, proposed canal-side coloured houses, water, bicycles and a Danish flag; no busy scenery behind the writing surfaces.
3. Main car: ten unequal panels. One possible reading structure is **3 + 3 + 2 + 2**: Company/Target/Positioning; Branding/Digital/Sales; Budget/Measurement; Innovation/Alignment. This row structure is a proposal, not a proven layout.
4. Reference provision: claim-level citations on the front; reserve physical and digital space for the complete list before fixing final panel dimensions.

Proposed title: **GREEN SM IN COPENHAGEN**.

Proposed subtitle: **A 12-month market-entry plan | Copenhagen, Denmark**.

Existing approved campaign line: **Know your ride before you book.** The poster explanation is in English; the proposed customer-facing campaign remains Danish-first. Use **Denmark** in the English poster.

The official brief requires precise information under eleven named elements; it does not explicitly say that the printed heading strings must be verbatim. The workspace separately requires those exact headings. A two-tier heading satisfies both the current workspace rule and the desired visual treatment: a short reader-facing title, followed by the exact canonical heading in a smaller, still legible line. ## 6. Worked visual example and honesty rules

Target Market could combine a three-segment selection diagram, a selected S1 profile and three horizontal reason bars. The existing evidence reports 57% price, 22% habit and an 11% safety-related motive among **79 Capital Region respondents who chose Uber/Bolt/TaxiToGo over a traditional dispatch office**. Preserve the question/sample qualification (E-024/E-187). These are not proportions of all Copenhagen residents and do not form a complete 100% pie. Population and household figures are context, not an observed count of S1.

The short decision caption must retain the challenge: price and habit are important; the proposed appeal of clarity/help remains a hypothesis. A graphic cannot silently convert the survey into proof that P1 will work.

Other rules:

- DKK227 and DKK230 are differently dated, standardised meter examples with a specified trip; they are not matched current app quotes. Do not make “cheaper” or price parity the dominant visual assertion.
- Do not invent positions on a competitor scatterplot, service superiority scores or customer quotations.
- Keep the budget's exclusion of cars, drivers, charging and frontline support visible. A proposed help route needs real operating capacity; marketing spend does not fund that entire capacity.
- The illustrative contribution case leaves approximately DKK554,000 uncovered. It is a conditional plan scenario, not positive ROI and not proof of company losses.
- The DKK524 acquisition target refers to search/social cost per new rider, not total programme cost per rider.
- Retention figures retain their cohort and observation-window definition. Gate B thresholds and the street-screen condition are not interchangeable with year-end targets.
- Gates show possible future branches. Unlit signals or labelled decisions are preferable to green checks suggesting that a gate has passed.
- Scheduled booking already appears in the saved service evidence. The proposed reminder mechanism must not portray advance booking itself as newly invented.

## 7. Text and references

Write complete assessable content first without an arbitrary word cap. Then identify which meaning moves into a diagram, number, label, comparison, source caption or short decision sentence. A poster can contain substantial information without paragraphs, but drawing an icon beside unchanged bullets is insufficient transformation.

Every essential argument and qualification must appear visibly in the final poster. Hidden Markdown comments, production notes, an AI prompt or the oral pitch cannot supply missing poster content. The full Markdown is the control document; the visible-label manifest is its assessed visual expression.

References architecture:

1. Keep author–year citations beside the supported labels/diagrams, with relevant page/date/sample information.
2. Include a complete Cite Them Right Harvard list. The handbook says the back of the chart; the separate brief says the end. The final physical/digital packaging remains Q-L3/Q-L4/Q-S6.
3. Proposed route: complete list on the reverse, with a corresponding digital reference page if accepted. Do not assume that front-only photography includes the reverse.
4. Reserve the list area in the front layout if the confirmed upload route requires front-only references. A separate handout or QR link may assist access but cannot silently replace the required list.
5. Generate references from the registry, recheck year suffixes for the final cited set and cite only registered, verified PDFs. Copy neither the Oura reference list nor references hallucinated by an image model.
6. Illustration provenance and any required AI disclosure are separate from market evidence; manual redrawing does not erase AI involvement.
