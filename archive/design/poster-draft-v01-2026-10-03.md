## 1. Product and assessment logic

The poster presents a coherent market-entry marketing plan through ten different information graphics inside a stylised cyan Limo Green, with the six-line identity cloud outside. Copenhagen provides the setting. The title, subtitle and tagline remain exactly as approved.

The brief's eleven elements are content requirements. The seven rubric criteria have their own weights; individual panel headings do not carry separate published marks. C1 company/context is 10%; C2 target/segmentation/branding 15%; C3 digital/sales 20%; C4 budget/resources 15%; C5 KPI alignment 10%; C6 creativity/emerging concepts 10%; C7 visual/presentation quality 20%. Positioning and alignment remain required even without standalone rubric weights.

For each panel below, “required content” means the brief/rubric's explicit ask. “Proposed content” and “visual treatment” are the agent's way of answering it. These working-document categories are not labels to repeat inside every poster panel. There is no uniform decision/evidence/action template.

## 2. Panel 1 — Meet Green SM

**Required content:** background, origin, operating context and core offer. Primary rubric: C1.

**Proposed content:** Vietnamese company/GSM identity; Hanoi taxi launch on 14 April 2023; Copenhagen entry on 30 July 2026 as the company's first European market; app-booked electric taxi rides; eight-market context dated 26 September 2026, including the Dutch pilot; owned fleet/employed-driver model.

**Visual treatment:** a short Vietnam → Copenhagen route timeline with two dated stops. A separate eight-market badge carries its date/pilot qualification. Beneath the route, a simple company → car + employed driver sketch explains how delivery is organised. This introduces the actual service, not vehicle sales or a family-car product.

**Meaning to retain:** Vietnam is the country of origin; Denmark is the plan's market. The brand was Xanh SM at the Hanoi launch. The Limo Green drawing is an illustrative vehicle choice, not a verified Copenhagen fleet inventory.

**Sources/concepts:** E-002, E-008, E-009, E-015, E-019, E-184, E-188. Module: `drivers-of-change`, `platform-business-model`, `customer-experience-differentiator`.

## 3. Panel 2 — Target Market

**Required content:** defined target; demographic, psychographic and behavioural characteristics; segmentation and prioritisation. Primary rubric: C2.

**Proposed content:** prioritise self-paying adult residents already using taxis for recurring occasions, within confirmed coverage. Organisations and short-stay visitors sit outside the campaign: local account capability is unverified, and visitors offer fewer opportunities for repeat rides. Show city context without converting it into a customer count. Label the rider persona and predictability/control preference as illustrative/hypothetical.

**Visual treatment:** three segment lanes, one selected, each with its selection reason. The chosen lane opens into a rider profile containing age/area, taxi behaviour and the psychographic hypothesis. Put separate horizontal bars alongside it: lower price 57%, habit 22%, driver/trip visibility and safety 11%. The bars share a 0–100% scale, but are not a pie or shares of an exhaustive whole.

**Meaning to retain:** the survey covers n=79 Capital Region platform-app choosers, with multiple answers. The 11% safety-related response concerns visibility, tracking and sharing; it does not establish that all S1 riders want Green SM or will pay extra. Population 670,389 and 44.3% one-person households are municipality context with different dates/denominators. The historic Inner Copenhagen/08:00–20:00 launch scope is not current coverage.

**Sources/concepts:** E-024, E-031, E-033, E-182, E-187. Module: `stp-model`, `market-segmentation`, `segmentation-bases`, `targeting-strategies`, `customer-personas`.

## 4. Panel 3 — Why Green SM?

Small subtitle: **Positioning Strategy**.

**Required content:** positioning statement, differentiation and relevant UVP. Related rubric: C2 and the practical-strategy argument.

**Proposed content:** serve existing resident taxi users with clear area/fare terms before booking and a route to help afterwards. Owned fleet/employed drivers provide a delivery basis. Switching appeal and support performance still require proof. Compare operating models and published fare examples without inventing rival service scores.

**Visual treatment:** the positioning statement leads. A small comparison shows Green SM's owned fleet/employed drivers versus Uber/Bolt booking other operators' rides. Two dated meter cards support the price discussion: Green SM DKK227 and TAXA 4x35 DKK230 for a 10 km/12-minute weekday trip. A slim before-booking → after-trip strip connects terms with help.

**Meaning to retain:** Green SM's tariff is dated 24 June 2026; TAXA's is dated 1 December 2023. These are published benchmarks, not contemporaneous matched app quotes. App maxima are separate. No unsupported “cheapest”, “safest” or “best support” ranking. The 68.5% Danish new-car BEV share describes new registrations, not taxi fleet share, and supports the inference that electric alone has limited differentiation.

**Sources/concepts:** E-015, E-019, E-028, E-039, E-184. Module: `positioning`, `positioning-statement`, `unique-value-proposition`, `positioning-strategies`.

## 5. Panel 4 — Brand & Promise

**Required content:** logo, colours, messaging and fit with target/positioning. Primary rubric: C2.

**Proposed content:** retain the authentic Green SM identity and observed teal/yellow-gold palette. Use the approved English poster line **Clear terms. Local care.** Explain how “Reliability” and “Local Respect” become visible area/fare information and a staffed route to help. Danish-first and English-second campaign delivery remains the basis; a Danish translation has not been chosen.

**Visual treatment:** authentic logo + two colour swatches; the tagline connects to an area map, fare explanation and trip-reference/help touchpoint. One short sentence explains audience fit: predictability/control for occasional resident riders is the intended emotional benefit.

**Meaning to retain:** the company identity is existing; the particular marketing expression is proposed. Retaining the palette supports recognition; no colour-psychology evidence or official colour codes are invented. Help is a promise requiring staffing, not a proved response record. Cyan body paint is the accepted poster illustration treatment.

**Sources/concepts:** E-010, E-012, E-185. Module: `brand-identity`, `brand-identity-components`, `brand-strategy-alignment`, `consistent-brand-messaging`.

## 6. Panel 5 — Digital Marketing

**Required content:** selected channels/tactics, customer engagement and practical examples. Primary rubric: C3.

**Proposed content:** PPC search catches an existing taxi need; the SEO/content page answers area, hours, price and help questions; social explains an unfamiliar service; opted-in email/CRM encourages the next paid ride. Retain DKK108,000 search and DKK90,000 social allocations. Later screens depend on Gate B and the stricter 35% repeat condition.

**Visual treatment:** two small example entry points, a Copenhagen taxi-intent search and a fare/help social explainer, flow into one local page. The page has labelled map/hours/fare/help tiles and points to the app. A separate post-trip lane shows email/CRM and a relevant reminder. A small street-screen sketch sits after a visible conditional gate.

**Meaning to retain:** the user-identity statistic is national reach context, not a unique-prospect count. DOOH is 200,000 impressions at the published DKK191 CPM plus DKK4,995 setup; impressions are not people or sales. No screen conversions are credited. Advance booking is already advertised; the relevant reminder is proposed.

**Sources/concepts:** E-016, E-029, E-042, E-188. Module: `pay-per-click`, `search-engine-optimisation`, `content-marketing`, `social-media-marketing`, `email-marketing`, `integrated-digital-marketing`, `consumer-decision-making-process`.

## 7. Panel 6 — From Clicks to Rides

Small subtitle: **Sales Strategy**.

**Required content:** sales channel, techniques, lead generation, prospecting and conversion. Primary rubric: C3.

**Proposed content:** direct app booking; an install/account is a lead; one opted-in message develops the prospect; a completed paid trip is conversion. Show the DKK30 first-ride voucher, once per new rider, capped at 400. Follow-up/help and consented reminders support repeat. Name value-based, incentive-based closing, soft and relationship selling through their actual uses.

**Visual treatment:** five equal-width stages: lead generation → prospecting → first paid ride → after-sales → repeat. A voucher callout belongs at conversion, and a trip-reference/help callout at after-sales. Equal widths indicate process, not unverified drop-off volumes. A narrowing funnel would require actual stage-volume evidence.

**Meaning to retain:** the website informs and directs to app booking, not a web checkout. Soft selling invites unpaid reviews. Drivers can be briefed because they are employed; card printing needs a quote/approved contingency. Frontline support is an operating prerequisite outside the marketing budget.

**Sources/concepts:** E-011, E-188. Module: `sales-process`, `sales-channels`, `selling-techniques`, `closing-techniques`, `soft-selling`, `after-sales-service`, `relationship-selling`.

## 9. Panel 8 — Success Dashboard

**Required content:** success metrics, tracking/analysis and objective alignment. Primary rubric: C5.

**Proposed content:** preserve all eleven v07 KPI/control rows, grouped under O1 awareness, O2 trial, O3 repeat and O4 service integrity, with spend control. Each retains its target/condition, tool and review rhythm. Retain the usable-offer baseline control and committed-plus-spent control omitted from v06.

**Visual treatment:** four objective-labelled dashboard groups, implemented as a readable table/row grid. Use prominent proposed targets, not filled achievement gauges. Under the grid, two compact decision branches carry Gate A and Gate B conditions and actions. Tools and review rhythms can occupy shared columns where their applicability is unambiguous.

**Meaning to retain:** consideration is +10 percentage points from an unmeasured M1–2 baseline, not +10%. Paid conversion uses all paid clicks; DKK524 is paid-channel cost per first rider, not full CAC. Repeat excludes immature cohorts. The help KPI counts referenced complaints, not an undefined expanded set of requests. No usable-offer threshold is invented before its baseline.

Gate A: M4, service and conversion floors; minimum observed volume must be agreed with operations; fail → hold/fix/re-test. Gate B: M6, 20% mature-cohort repeat and DKK1,829 cost ceiling to continue; 35% repeat to release screens; fail → stop scale-up. M12 → renew/revise/stop. Annual targets and lower gate floors are distinct planning assumptions.

**Sources/concepts:** approved plan §10 and v07 KPI table. Module: `kpis-and-metrics`, `campaign-tracking-and-analytics`, `sales-conversion-rate`, `customer-retention-rate`, `customer-acquisition-cost`.

## 10. Panel 9 — Ride Innovation

**Required content:** original business-specific application of emerging concepts. Primary rubric: C6.

**Proposed content:** consented own-history reminders support repeat; one trip reference across channels supports help continuity; pay-per-trip remains the revenue model; AI chatbot/subscription are deferred with reasons; ordinary surveys measure clarity instead of neuromarketing.

**Visual treatment:** one illustrative phone with “Your usual Thursday ride. Pre-book it now.” Beneath it, consent → own trip history → reminder → intended repeat. A shared-reference line connects page/app/card/follow-up/help. A compact treatment strip distinguishes adopted proposals from deferred/non-selected trends and gives their reasons.

**Meaning to retain:** pre-booking already exists. Reminder/support integration is proposed and needs local implementation. Personalisation has a specific mechanism, not merely a person's name inserted into an advert. Omnichannel connects the same journey/reference, not just multiple platform icons. Subscription requires repeat demand; an AI fare error would undermine the promise. No executed tests/results are claimed.

**Sources/concepts:** E-016; approved plan §11. Module: `personalisation`, `omnichannel`, `artificial-intelligence`, `subscription-model`, `subscription-challenges`, `usage-based-model`, `neuromarketing`.

## 11. Panel 10 — Business Goals & Growth

**Required content:** alignment with overall goals and long-term sustainability/growth.

**Proposed content:** company priority of stable local operation before expansion; O1/O2 build awareness/trial; O3 repeat governs growth; O4/gates protect delivery. Connect the electric offer to the stated mission without asserting measured environmental impact. Year-two renewal depends on service, repeat and financial evidence.

**Visual treatment:** three horizontal goal → objective/action links, ending in a year-two renew/revise/stop branch. This is a conditional growth plan; avoid an unqualified rising arrow. The detailed spending timeline belongs in Budget, and gate thresholds in Dashboard, reducing duplication while keeping the goal links visible here.

**Meaning to retain:** Green SM's stated operating priority and the authority's judgement about entry investment are different attributions. A sustainable-growth intention does not establish year-one breakeven or lifecycle emissions savings.

**Sources/concepts:** E-020, E-021, E-185. Module: `alignment-with-objectives`, `long-term-sustainable-growth`, `retention-over-acquisition`.
