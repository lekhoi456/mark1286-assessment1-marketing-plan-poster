## Precision review of the parent's proposed copy

The parent's sentence gives the target, electric-taxi frame, buyer benefits and source-backed delivery basis. Its phrases “offers” and “backed by” should stay under an explicit proposed-position qualifier because clear terms and local help are planned commitments, not tested current service outcomes. The refinement uses “proposed position” and keeps the delivery rationale separate from the sourced ownership/employment fact. It also retains recurring local trips and the confirmed-service-area condition.

“Direct fleet and driver control supports the proposed service promise” is a reasonable plan inference, but sounds like an established causal result if it is displayed without a qualifier. “Proposed delivery rationale: Use direct fleet and driver control to support clear booking terms and a route to local help” expresses the group's intended use of the operating structure. It does not claim unique care, measured clarity or superiority. The parent may prefer its shorter sentence once this distinction is preserved.

Operational delivery still requires accurate app information, staffed local help and coordination with drivers. Ownership/employment alone proves none of these outcomes. Their resources and evaluation belong in the approved Budget, Success Dashboard and Business Goals panels, not in invented new panel-3 metrics.
