## Exact executed prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: a hand-drawn decorative illustration sheet for the approved Target Market section of an MBA marketing-plan poster. Create real coloured-pencil and marker artwork with slightly irregular dark navy pen strokes, bright cyan, warm cream highlights and small golden-yellow accents, crisp shapes and modest paper-like drawing texture. Background must be genuinely transparent; the layout will supply cream paper. Landscape 3:2 composition with SEVEN clearly separated illustrated islands and generous transparent gutters. Centre column: one generic adult resident in casual neutral clothing, waist-up, holding a smartphone, friendly neutral expression, no occupation, income, family, luxury or demographic stereotypes. This is an illustrative proposed rider profile, never a real surveyed customer. Left column has three small thought-bubble symbols vertically separated: top an unnumbered price tag; middle a simple smartphone containing only a route line and dots; bottom a customer-support headset. Right column has three separate symbols vertically: top a cyan taxi silhouette with completely blank roof sign and no brand or letters; middle an undated calendar with two circular repeat arrows and no dates or writing; bottom a simple yellow personal wallet with no denomination. Make the central person larger than the six icon islands. Keep each island inside its own clear area so a layout tool can crop and position each independently. Nothing touches the edge and no connector reaches another island. No heading, city, location text, label, chart, bars, axis, legend, numbers, currency glyphs, letters, words, percentages, dates, logos, watermarks or simulated writing anywhere. No writing on the phone, taxi, calendar, price tag or wallet. Leave the top margin empty. All exact approved labels and the accurate population chart will be placed later by a layout tool, so generate decorative art only.
