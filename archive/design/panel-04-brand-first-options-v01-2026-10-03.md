# Panel 4: brand-first replacement proposal

Prepared 4 October 2026. The filename retains the assigned 3 October review-series date.

**Status: proposal only.** D-074 and production v05 remain unchanged. This document recommends one replacement direction; it does not select wording or authorise image production.

## Diagnosis and recommendation

Remove the four app-choice percentages from the visible Branding and Identity panel. They describe market behaviour, not the visual and verbal identity being developed. Their dominant grid makes v05 read as market evidence followed by a campaign response. It leaves little space to explain how the logo, colours and proposed wording form a coherent identity for the selected audience and position.

Section 2 already establishes the target and proposed rider profile. Section 3 already explains clear terms, local care and delivery arrangements. Digital Marketing Tactics and Sales Strategies cover the route to app booking. Panel 4 should contribute the brand expression connecting those choices: inherited visual identity, proposed local wording, an explicit fit rationale and consistent examples. Keep the tagline **Clear terms. Local care.**

Propose **4. Branding and Identity** as the displayed heading. It matches the brief directly and keeps the agreed marketing-section number. This heading change remains a proposal.

## Brief, module and rubric basis

The brief's element 5 requires the logo, colour scheme and brand messaging, and asks whether they align with the desired positioning and target market. The registered `brand-identity` concept defines visual and verbal identity, while `brand-identity-components` lists logo, colours, typography, tagline, voice and visual style. `consistent-brand-messaging` supports repeating the same tone and message across touchpoints. Sources: `00_brief_and_criteria/brief.md`, element 5; `03_course_materials/concept-register.md`, entries above and `brand-strategy-alignment` (Week 4 Lecture slide 8; Week 4 Tutorial slide 6; Week 3 Lecture slide 9).

Official C2 assesses target definition and creative logo/message alignment within its overall 15% criterion. Official C7 assesses visual organisation and presentation within its overall 20%. Part B's C2.3 and C7 tests are local operational checks, not official grade bands. This proposal makes the required alignment easier to assess; it cannot establish a numerical mark or audience validation.

## Proposed infographic structure

Use four compact zones in the existing warm-paper, navy, cyan and yellow style:

1. **Existing identity:** the shared hand-drawn rendition of Green SM's established lockup and the observed teal/cyan and yellow-gold swatches. Describe inherited assets accurately; do not present the rendition as a new corporate logo.
2. **Proposed Copenhagen expression:** make the retained tagline dominant. Place Danish-first/English-second and plain, respectful wording beneath the proposed label. These are local planning choices, not demonstrated Danish preferences or Green SM's official voice.
3. **Target and position fit:** name the selected self-paying, recurring Copenhagen taxi audience. Pair Clear terms with plain wording and Local care with respectful wording. This explains the verbal expression of the selected position without repeating section 3's service details. Trip control and fair treatment remain an appeal to test.
4. **Consistency:** show three equal specimen cards for an ad, app and in-car card. Repeat the same lockup, palette and tagline. These illustrate proposed brand applications, not a conversion funnel, an existing Danish app screen, new app features or a committed channel allocation.

The four app-choice cues remain in the research record and may support the pitch's market rationale. Do not insert them into accepted section 2 automatically. Typography remains the poster's hand-drawn presentation treatment; no official Green SM font specification is asserted.
