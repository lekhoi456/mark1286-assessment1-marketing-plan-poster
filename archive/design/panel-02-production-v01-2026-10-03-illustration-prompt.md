## Self-contained prompt

Create one decorative vector-like hand-drawn illustration asset for a university MBA marketing-plan poster section. Use a warm cream background, restrained bright cyan, dark navy pen outlines and small yellow accents. The visual style should resemble clean coloured-pencil/marker drawing with slight natural stroke variation, crisp recognisable shapes and generous empty space.

The composition centres one generic adult urban resident holding a smartphone. Show a neutral, friendly adult with casual clothing. The person is an illustrative customer profile, not a named individual, photograph, occupation, income class or household category. Avoid professional uniforms, luxury signals, family props and any assertion about real customer behaviour.

Use three simple thought symbols to the left of the head: an unnumbered price tag, a smartphone with a route line, and a customer-support headset. Use three simple behavioural symbols to the right and near the hands: a taxi silhouette without lettering, a calendar with circular repeat arrows but no dates, and a plain personal wallet. Their order is price tag / route phone / support headset, and taxi / repeat calendar / wallet. Connect the thought symbols loosely to the head and the behavioural symbols loosely to the phone/hands. Keep every symbol distinct and readable. Include a small blank location pin above the resident.

Produce the illustration zone only, with no vehicle shell, city skyline, heading, chart or text. Use a wide horizontal arrangement approximately 1,600 units across by 420 units high: three left symbol zones, an open central resident zone and three right symbol zones. Keep the edges spacious so a layout tool can place approved labels beside each symbol. Leave a clear area above the resident for the location/service-area marker and the profile qualifier. The layout tool will build the complete section.

Do not generate any letters, words, numerals, currency symbols, percentages, dates, logos, watermarks, chart columns, axes, legends or data. No writing on the phone, calendar, taxi or price tag. All chart geometry, population numbers, source/date, profile labels and the repeat-opportunity caption are supplied later as native vector/text objects by the layout tool.
