## Illustration prompt

Create the illustration layer for an A0 landscape marketing-plan poster, 1189 × 841 mm, intended to be printed as a drawing guide and redrawn by hand.

The scene is Copenhagen, Denmark. Use a restrained canal-side city vignette with recognisable colourful narrow houses, water, a few bicycles and a small Danish flag. Leave pale, uncluttered sky for the header and one broad white cloud at the upper right. The cloud needs a flat internal writing area sufficient for six straight lines, without a speech-bubble tail. Keep city detail around the poster perimeter so it does not interfere with writing surfaces.

The main subject is a large, stylised cyan VinFast Limo Green electric transport-service vehicle, near-side view with a slight view of the front. Follow the correct Limo Green proportions, front/rear styling and reference silhouette. Make its body a deliberate graphic cutaway with generous white analytical surfaces. Keep essential writing space outside tyres, lights and narrow body corners. Wheels may extend below the panel grid. The cyan body, dark wheel shapes and restrained highlights give the vehicle identity.

Use hand-drawn marker and coloured-pencil qualities: dark navy outlines with slight natural variation, flat cyan/teal fills, pale yellow-gold accents, and light pencil texture. Broad simple curves and flat perspective should remain practical to redraw. Use white or very pale panel interiors. Preserve a clear reading order and ample separation.

Arrange exactly ten blank marketing panels inside the vehicle body in four bands: three across the upper band, three across the next band, two across the deepest band, and two across the lower band. The deepest band is for budget and measurement; it needs more space than the branding/company sections. In the upper band, the middle target-market panel is wider than the company panel. In the next band, the middle digital and right sales panels are wider than the left branding panel. Use mostly rectangular internal writing areas with softly drawn borders; the outer car shell supplies the organic shape.

The upper-left sky reserves space for three title groups: city/flag, invitation and an authentic brand logo to be placed afterwards. Reserve two straight lines below for subtitle and tagline. Reserve reference space according to the supplied layout draft. All reserved surfaces stay blank.

Output the illustration without written words, letters, numbers, logos, prices, names, citations, chart data or dashboard readings. Do not improvise company branding or reproduce the text from reference posters. No generated marketing text belongs in this layer. The Danish flag is a visual symbol, not a word label.

## Controlled layout overlay

After illustration generation, a layout tool places the approved title composition, subtitle, tagline and six cloud lines from the display master. Place an authentic Green SM logo from a verified company source, maintaining its original proportions and colours.

Use the exact approved ten headings. Only Why Green SM? receives Positioning Strategy as a small subtitle, and only From Clicks to Rides receives Sales Strategy. Do not add a second canonical heading line elsewhere.

Build the information graphics as editable vector/text objects using the complete content master and panel mapping: timeline, segment comparison/profile/motive bars, operating-model and meter comparison, brand touchpoints, digital-channel flow, sales process, budget receipt/resources/economics, KPI grid/gates, reminder/reference mechanism and goal links. Decorative icons may support these objects but cannot substitute for their content.

Use exact checked figures. Label planned targets, assumptions and illustrative examples. Keep n=79/Capital Region/multiple-response qualifications with the motive bars, dates and trip specification with fare benchmarks, and the 30% contribution/fare-proxy assumptions with the financial illustration. Preserve the difference between 20% repeat to continue and 35% repeat for screens. Place citations beside their claims and the complete references in the selected readable package.

Export actual A0 dimensions through the layout tool. Obtain the printer's resolution/profile/bleed requirements rather than treating the image model's raster output as automatically print-ready. Inspect text fidelity, figure fidelity, spelling, clipping, complete references and physical legibility before accepting the final proof.
