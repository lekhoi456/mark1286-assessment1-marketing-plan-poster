## Approved targeting scope

Priority: Copenhagen residents aged **25–44**, within the usable service area, who already choose taxis on recurring local occasions and pay personally. This combines the newly approved age range with the existing S1 geographic, behavioural and payer conditions. It does not impose an age restriction on the taxi service itself.

Selected target description, retained alongside the approved profile labels:

**Copenhagen residents aged 25–44**  
**Self-paying · Existing taxi users · Recurring local trips**
