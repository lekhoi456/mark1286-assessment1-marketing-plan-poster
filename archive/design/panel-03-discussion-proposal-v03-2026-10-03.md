## Source-based challenge to the strategy

Authority Table 5.12 reports price 57% and habitual app use 22% among a Capital Region platform-app-choice subsample (n=79, multiple answers; pp. 150–151, E-024). E-187's 11% concerns driver information/trip visibility and safety, not local support or willingness to pay. These are context, not age-25–44 survey findings.

The selected P1 therefore faces a substantive commercial challenge: clearer terms/help may not outweigh price or app habit. The plan tests clarity, service response and repeat use through its existing Dashboard/gates. This is a defensible proposed marketing strategy, not demonstrated demand. Keep these analytical boundaries behind the displayed factual claims; the Proposed position qualifier handles plan status on the poster. No unapproved survey chart is introduced.
