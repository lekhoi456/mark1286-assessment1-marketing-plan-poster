## Behind-the-scenes production treatment

The full surface uses handwriting, thin irregular pen lines and translucent visible marker passes on warm near-white paper. The former thick cyan border, computer sans lettering, solid bars, ruler grid and smooth UI capsules are replaced. The 40.2% pill is still an enclosed hand-drawn shape above 25–44, with 269,277 separate. Its all-city denominator is preserved.

Headings use `/System/Library/Fonts/MarkerFelt.ttc`, collection index 0 (Thin). Every other text object uses `/System/Library/Fonts/Noteworthy.ttc`, collection index 1 (Bold). The final portable SVG renders paths directly from these local glyphs, so it cannot fall back to a clean sans. The native-text companion is convenient for editing on this Mac; its appearance elsewhere depends on those fonts. Text remains exact in the manifest and all letters are controlled by layout, not by the image model. Font files are neither copied nor redistributed.

Four population bars use the unchanged calculation scale 253.8 ÷ 350,000 × 1.1 units per resident and zero baseline 655. Their true tops/heights are saved in the manifest. Slightly irregular perimeter paths and visible horizontal marker strokes decorate those quantities; fills are clipped to the true rectangles. Equal category spacing/width does not imply equal age spans. Population size does not prove taxi demand or optimal age targeting.

The native-generated decorative drawing shows one simple adult holding a phone, an unnumbered price tag, route phone, support headset, taxi, repeat calendar and wallet. A drawn customer-support figure is only a support pictogram. The resident's appearance is illustrative, not a gender, occupation, income or household filter. Profile wording remains Proposed rider profile. The original asset bytes and alpha are preserved; native viewports and one resident-only clip position the islands and exclude neighbouring fragments.
