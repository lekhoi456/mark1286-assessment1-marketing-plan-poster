### Why Green SM?

**Proposed position**

For self-paying Copenhagen residents aged 25–44 taking recurring local taxi trips within the service area, Green SM offers app-booked electric taxi rides with clear booking terms and a route to local help, through its own fleet and employed drivers.

**Clear terms**

Service area · Operating hours · Fare terms

**Local care**

Trip reference · A clear route to local help

**App-booked electric rides**

**Green SM: Owned fleet · Employed drivers**

**Uber / Bolt: Taxi partners operate rides**

(Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 119, 174). <!-- E-019 E-184 -->

**Delivery plan**

Use direct fleet and driver control to support clear terms and local help.
