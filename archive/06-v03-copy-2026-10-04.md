## Display copy

### 6. From Clicks to Rides
<!-- budget: 185 -->

### Sales Strategy

### Awareness

Build visible Copenhagen awareness. Installs/accounts are leads, not sales.

### App booking

Book directly in the app. A sale counts only when the trip is completed and paid. The plan offers a DKK30 discount on one first trip per new rider, capped at 400 riders.

### Experience to earn

Earn an excellent experience through the app, driver, car + customer care.

### Feedback

Ask one trip-clarity question. After a resolved or well-rated trip, invite an unpaid app-store review.

### Repeat

Send app notifications only with consent. A repeat voucher needs separate approval and funding. Pilot a tailored, capped, time-limited monthly promo bundle only if observed rider frequency and unit economics fit.

### Service gate

Verify operations and staffed-help standards before making any quality claim.

### Pilot boundary

No repeat-voucher budget or Copenhagen package launch is approved. No retention or revenue lift is forecast.

## Layout mapping

- Exact numbered title and small unnumbered subtitle sit at upper left; the provided Green SM hand-drawn logo is embedded unchanged at upper right.
- Five wavy cards create the visual sequence Awareness → App booking → Experience to earn → Feedback → Repeat. Hand-drawn city, voucher, car/app, feedback and notification/package icons carry most of the visual weight.
- A curved return arrow connects Repeat back to App booking, closing the repeat-purchase loop.
- Two bottom callouts separate the service-readiness condition from the offer/pilot boundary.
- This is a sales journey, not a media allocation or channel plan. Section 5 owns media choices and release gates; Section 9 retains the innovation decisions.
