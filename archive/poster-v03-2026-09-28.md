**Drivers of change (evolving business models):** 📌 Uber and Bolt apps book cars run by other operators, and dispatch offices rely on self-employed operators. Green SM owns its cars and employs its drivers (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 119, 174). <!-- E-019 E-184 E-186 -->

## Target Market Analysis
<!-- budget: 200 -->

**Decision: concentrate on self-paying Copenhagen residents who already ride taxis.**

**STP model, three segments compared:**

- ✏️ Organisations: not targeted, as accounts are not evidenced in Denmark.
- ✏️ Short-stay visitors: not targeted, as trips seldom repeat.
- ✏️ Self-paying residents: concentrated marketing.

**Segmentation bases for residents:**

- 📌 Demographic: 670,389 residents in Copenhagen Municipality, where 44.3% of households are one person (Statistics Denmark, 2026b; Statistics Denmark, 2026a). <!-- E-031 E-033 -->
- 📌 Geographic: the confirmed service area only, Inner Copenhagen from 08:00 to 20:00 at launch (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 175). <!-- E-182 -->
- 📌 Behavioural: nationally, 40-50% of recent taxi users have one taxi app and 30-40% have none. Price (57%) and habit (22%) led 79 Capital Region riders who chose Uber, Bolt or TaxiToGo over a traditional taxi firm (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 150-151, 194). <!-- E-023 E-024 -->
- 📌 Psychographic: 11% of those riders felt safer seeing the driver, tracking the trip and sharing it (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 151). <!-- E-187 --> ✏️ With meter prices level, we expect them to switch only if area and fare are easy to check and help is easy to get.

✏️ Illustrative persona, not a real person: lives alone, rides a taxi a few times a month, keeps one app and checks the price first.

## Positioning Strategy
<!-- budget: 140 -->

**Decision: win on clear terms and help, not on price or electricity.**

**Positioning statement and unique value proposition:** for Copenhagen residents who already take taxis, Green SM is the local electric taxi that is easy to judge before booking and easy to get help from afterwards, because it owns its cars and employs its drivers.

**Benefit positioning, two meters at one price level** (standard 10 km, 12-minute weekday trip):

- 📌 Green SM day meter: DKK227 (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-015 -->
- 📌 TAXA 4x35 door price: DKK230, dated 1 December 2023 (TAXA 4x35, no date). <!-- E-028 -->

📌 App fares have a higher published maximum, so ✏️ we promise clear terms, not a lower price.

📌 Electric cannot lead: 68.5% of new cars registered in Denmark in 2025 were battery-electric, calculated from European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association (ACEA) (2026) figures. <!-- E-039 -->

**Decision: keep the identity and add one checkable promise.**

📌 **Brand identity kept**, because the April 2026 rebrand unified it across markets (Green SM, 2026f): the name, the leaf-shaped logo in teal and yellow-gold, and the Danish core values "Safety - Discipline - Reliability - Professionalism - Local Respect" (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date c). <!-- E-010 E-012 -->

## Digital Marketing Tactics
<!-- budget: 150 -->

**Decision: one tactic per stage of the consumer decision-making process, all pointing to one local page.**

1. ✏️ Need recognition, pay-per-click: Danish and English taxi searches in the service area. DKK108,000.
2. ✏️ Information search, SEO and content marketing: one local page with map, hours, fare rules and help.
3. ✏️ Evaluation, social media marketing: short explainers on fares and help. DKK90,000. 📌 Social media user identities equal 78.9% of Denmark's population (Kemp, 2025a). <!-- E-042 -->
4. ✏️ Purchase: DKK30 off a first ride, capped at 400 new riders.
5. ✏️ Post-purchase, email marketing: opt-in follow-up with the trip reference and a reminder to pre-book, which the app already allows up to 30 days ahead (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-016 -->

✏️ Awareness only after Gate B: 📌 Digital Copenhagen street screens, 200,000 impressions at DKK191 per thousand (AFA Decaux, 2026). <!-- E-029 -->

**Integrated digital marketing:** one message, one page, one trip reference, so channel results can be compared.

## Sales Strategies
<!-- budget: 145 -->

**Decision: the sale is a completed, paid trip, sold direct through the app.**

**Sales channel:** direct to consumer, through Green SM's own app and website.

**Sales process as one ride:**

1. ✏️ Lead generation: search, social and screens bring residents to the page.
2. ✏️ Prospecting: app users who have not ridden get one opt-in message.
3. ✏️ Conversion: the capped DKK30 voucher closes the sale without false urgency.
4. ✏️ After-sales service: every trip has a reference, and problems go to a help route staffed outside this budget.
5. ✏️ Relationship selling: consented reminders win the second and third ride.

**Value-based selling:** show fare rules and help, not discounts. **Soft selling:** invite app-store reviews and never pay for them. 📌 Drivers are employees in the initial phase (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date a), <!-- E-011 --> so briefings and an in-car help card make each ride part of the sale.

## Creativity and Innovation
<!-- budget: 120 -->

**Decision: personalise with the rider's own data and link every channel through one trip reference.**

### Phone mock-up, illustrative

"Your usual Thursday ride. Pre-book it now."

- ✏️ **Personalisation:** with consent, the app reminds riders before a regular trip and offers pre-booking, using only their own trip history.
- ✏️ **Omnichannel:** one trip reference links page, app, in-car card, follow-up and help, so no rider explains a problem twice.
- ✏️ **Revenue model:** pay per trip, a usage-based model. A subscription model waits, because the plan expects fewer than two rides per new rider within 90 days.
- ✏️ **AI chatbots** wait until a human-run FAQ shows recurring questions, because one wrong fare answer breaks the promise. **Neuromarketing** is not used.

## Alignment with Business Objectives
<!-- budget: 105 -->

**Decision: marketing grows only as fast as the service keeps its promise.**

📌 Green SM told Denmark's competition authority that it puts stable local operation before rapid expansion. The authority judged that becoming known would need substantial marketing investment (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 175, 179). <!-- E-020 E-021 -->

✏️ **Alignment with objectives:** O1 to O4 lead to the finish line, stable operation first and growth second. ✏️ **Long-term sustainable growth:** repeat trips, not one-off discounts, decide whether year two scales up. 📌 This serves the mission to "Provide a green, convenient, and sustainable mobility solution" (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date c). <!-- E-185 -->
