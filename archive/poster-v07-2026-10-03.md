## Company/Business Introduction

**Vietnamese origin:** GSM Green and Smart Mobility Joint Stock Company.

**14 April 2023 → 30 July 2026:** Xanh SM taxis launch in Hanoi → Green SM enters Copenhagen, its first European market (VietnamPlus, 2023a; Green SM, 2026d). <!-- E-002 E-008 -->

**Eight markets**, as at 26 September 2026, including the Dutch pilot (Green SM, 2026a). <!-- E-009 -->

**Local offer:** app-booked electric taxi rides, Green SM Car (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-015 E-188 -->

**Business model:** owned fleet and employed drivers give Green SM direct control of delivery. Uber and Bolt book rides supplied by other operators (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 119, 174). <!-- E-019 E-184 -->

## Target Market Analysis
<!-- budget: 215 -->

**STP: concentrated marketing to self-paying residents who already take taxis.**

| Segment | Priority and reason |
|---|---|
| Adult residents paying themselves | Selected: existing taxi occasions and opportunities to repeat |
| Organisations | Outside the campaign: local account capability unverified |
| Short-stay visitors | Outside the campaign: fewer opportunities to repeat |

**Proposed rider profile:** adults 18+ living within confirmed Copenhagen coverage, already paying for some recurring taxi trips.

**City context:** 670,389 residents on 1 July 2026. One-person households: 44.3% on 1 January 2026, calculated from household counts (Statistics Denmark, 2026b; Statistics Denmark, 2026a). These describe the municipality, not the number of target riders. <!-- E-031 E-033 -->

**Reported app-choice motives:** lower price 57%, habit 22%, driver/trip visibility and safety 11%. Capital Region platform-app choosers, n=79, multiple answers (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 150-151). <!-- E-024 E-187 -->

**Psychographic hypothesis:** predictability and trip control matter. Switching for clearer terms/help remains unproven.

**Illustrative persona:** lives alone, rides a few times monthly, checks prices, usually keeps one app.

**Geographic limit:** confirm area, hours and usable offers before advertising. Launch coverage was Inner Copenhagen, 08:00–20:00 (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 175). <!-- E-182 -->

## Positioning Strategy
<!-- budget: 180 -->

**Proposed benefit positioning:** for Copenhagen residents who already take taxis, Green SM is a locally run electric taxi with clear area and fare terms before booking, and a clear route to help afterwards. Its owned fleet and employed drivers provide a basis for delivering this promise (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 174). <!-- E-019 -->

**Operating-model comparison:** Green SM runs its fleet. Uber and Bolt use other operators. This establishes a delivery difference, not superior service (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 119, 174). <!-- E-019 E-184 -->

**Published weekday benchmarks: 10 km / 12 minutes**

- Green SM day meter: DKK227, calculated. Tariff updated 24 June 2026 (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-015 -->
- TAXA 4x35: DKK230. Tariff dated 1 December 2023 (TAXA 4x35, no date). <!-- E-028 -->

Different dates prevent a matched app-price comparison. App maxima are separate. The plan does not promise cheaper rides.

**Country context:** battery-electric cars were 68.5% of Danish new registrations in 2025, calculated from European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association (ACEA) (2026). Electric alone offers limited differentiation. <!-- E-039 -->

## Sales Strategies
<!-- budget: 185 -->

**Sales channel:** direct app booking. The website informs riders and directs them to the app (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). A completed, paid trip is the sale. <!-- E-188 -->

**Proposed sales process**

1. **Lead generation:** search/social → local page → app install or account.
2. **Prospecting:** one opted-in message to installers who have not ridden, explaining area and help.
3. **Conversion:** app booking → completed first paid ride. DKK30 voucher, once per new rider, capped at 400.
4. **After-sales service:** trip reference, staffed help route and a clarity question.
5. **Relationship selling:** consented reminders → another paid ride.

**Selling techniques:** value-based selling explains fare terms and available help. The capped incentive supports closing. Soft selling invites app-store reviews without paying for them.

**Delivery:** brief employed drivers and design an in-car help card (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date a). Printing needs a quote and approved contingency release. <!-- E-011 -->

**Resource dependency:** frontline support sits outside this marketing budget. Confirm staffing and response standards before advertising the help promise. Installs are leads, not sales.

## Creativity and Innovation
<!-- budget: 155 -->

**Proposed reminder, illustrative:** “Your usual Thursday ride. Pre-book it now.”

**Personalisation:** consent → the rider's own trip history → relevant reminder → intended repeat ride. Pre-booking already exists (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). The reminder mechanism is proposed. <!-- E-016 -->

**Omnichannel:** one trip reference across page, app, in-car card, follow-up and help. Intended benefit: less repetition when resolving a trip problem.

| Trend | Treatment and reason |
|---|---|
| Revenue model | Pay per trip: usage-based charging |
| Subscription | Deferred until frequent repeat demand is demonstrated |
| AI chatbot | Deferred until a human-run FAQ is reliable. Wrong fare answers undermine P1 |
| Neuromarketing | Not selected. Ordinary surveys test clarity |

**Originality:** connect personalised repeat use with a consistent route to help. Use consented first-party trip history, not purchased profiles. These features need local implementation. No delivered result is claimed.
