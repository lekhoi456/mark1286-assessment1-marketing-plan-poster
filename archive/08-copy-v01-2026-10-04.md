**Counting:** installs excluded from paid riders, refunds removed. Repeat uses only riders with a full 90-day window. DKK524 covers paid search/social acquisition, not total acquisition cost. Advertise only verified coverage with usable offers.

**Gate A / M4:** completion ≥95%, service cancellations ≤2%, referenced complaints answered within 24h ≥90%, search conversion ≥1.5%, social ≥0.8%. Agree the minimum observed volume with operations. Fail → hold media, fix and re-test. Pass → release M5–6.

**Gate B / M6:** mature-cohort 90-day repeat ≥20% and paid cost per first rider ≤DKK1,829 → continue. Repeat ≥35% → release street screens. Fail → stop scale-up.

**M12:** evaluate O1 to O4, then renew, revise or stop for year two. Floors and targets are planning assumptions. <!-- E-020 E-021 -->
