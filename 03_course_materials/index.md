## Source rules (carried over from P0)

- The Study Hub catalogue (`../study-hub/content/materials.json`) was used only to find original files. The Study Hub's lesson prose (`../study-hub/content/**`) is a study aid: it is never quoted or cited, and nothing in the register comes from it.
- Every extract carries a warning that its text is machine-extracted. A quotation is used only after the wording has been checked against the original slide or page; automatic OCR does not verify the source's accuracy or the truth of a claim.
- Slides, cases and the handbook are cited, if at all, from the original course files (D-005). Slide bibliographies are unverified leads, not references.
- Statistics printed on slides and in cases are the module's claims, not verified evidence; none may be used as a plan figure or a fact without checking the original source.
