## Slide 10 — Email Marketing how it can be implemented?

- Mini Case Study: Tesco UK – Email Marketing Strategy
- Frequent Campaigns: Regular promotions, discounts, and loyalty rewards.
- Personalization: Tailored emails based on purchase history and Clubcard status.
- Target Audience: Families, bargain shoppers, and loyalty members.
- Content Focus: Discounts, product recommendations, and exclusive deals.
- CTA (Call to Action) Optimization: Clear "Shop Now," "Get Offer," "Claim Discount" buttons.
- Success Metrics: High open rates, click-through rates, and customer retention.
- Automation Tools: Uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Klaviyo.
- Example Campaign: Clubcard Points Boost – Extra points on selected products to drive engagement.
- Tesco’s email marketing ensures personalized engagement, brand loyalty, and consistent sales.
- Source: Yesiloglu & Costello (2021)
