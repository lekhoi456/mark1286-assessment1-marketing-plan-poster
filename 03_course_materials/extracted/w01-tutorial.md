## Slide 21 — Shifting consumer behaviour

- [Image alt text: omnichannel statistics and attributions]
- Digital-first mindset - UK: 90% of consumers begin their buying journey online.
- Experience economy: From transactions to experiences - Pakistan: Foodpanda and Daraz have invested in immersive digital touchpoints to retain app users through gamification and loyalty points.
- Demand for ethical brands: UK: 73% of consumers prefer sustainable and socially responsible brands.
- Omnichannel expectations: India: Reliance Retail and Tata Neu integrate offline and online retail ecosystems to meet hybrid consumer demand.
- Source: Corsaro & Maggioni (2021)

## Slide 22 — Evolving business models

- [Image alt text: SaaS Freemium Conversion Rates: 2025 Report – First Page Sage]
- Subscription Economy (e.g. Netflix, Dollar Shave Club) - UK consumers spend an average of £552 per year on subscriptions, a 20% increase since 2020.
- Platform-based Models (Airbnb, Uber to interact with users) - Airbnb listings in South Asia grew by 30% in 2023, with India leading the regional market.Uber operates in over 70+ countries, offering tailored services like Uber Auto in India and UberBoat in Bangladesh.
- Freemium to Premium Conversions - Spotify reports 45% of global users convert to premium subscriptions, driven by personalised playlists and ad-free experiences.
- Gig and Creator Economies - Gig work platforms like Fiverr, Upwork, and Toptal see rising freelance participation from South Asia’s youth demographic.
- Source: Smith & Jones (2023)
