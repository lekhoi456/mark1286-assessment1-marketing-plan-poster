                                          Mini Case Study 1
    ASOS UK vs. Zara India – Content Marketing Strategies in Different Economies

Introduction

ASOS UK and Zara India operate in distinct economic environments—developed and developing
economies, respectively. Their content marketing strategies reflect their unique target audiences,
cultural influences, and consumer expectations.

ASOS UK: Digital-First Engagement in a Developed Economy

    •   Tone of Voice: Friendly, inclusive, and trend-focused, promoting individuality and self-
        expression.

    •   Campaign Example: #AsSeenOnMe—encouraging customers to share their outfits,
        leveraging user-generated content.

    •   Community Engagement: Active customer                       participation through social media
        interaction and influencer collaborations.

    •   Use of Video: Instagram Stories, YouTube tutorials, and behind-the-scenes fashion
        content enhance engagement.

    •   Results: Increased brand engagement and viral marketing success, driving strong online
        sales.

    •   Target Audience: Young adults and trendsetters seeking diverse, affordable fashion
        choices.

Zara India: Luxury Appeal in a Developing Economy

    •   Tone of Voice: Elegant and aspirational, focusing on timeless style and exclusivity.

    •   Campaign Example: Summer Collection Launch—high-fashion elements with luxury
        appeal.

    •   Community Engagement: Showcasing high-end collections through social media
        exclusivity.

    •   Use of Video: Product video ads, influencer collaborations, and runway clips elevate
        brand prestige.

    •   Results: Increased foot traffic during seasonal promotions and fashion week events.

    •   Target Audience: Fashion-conscious professionals, middle-class customers, and urban
        families seeking stylish yet affordable options.

Conclusion

ASOS UK thrives in a digital-first developed economy, leveraging social media for engagement
and brand loyalty. In contrast, Zara India tailors its strategy to a developing economy, prioritizing
exclusivity, aspirational branding, and seasonal promotions. Both brands successfully align their
content marketing approaches with their respective economic contexts.

                                            Mini Case Study 2

                          Amazon's PPC Strategy in a Developed Economy

Overview

Amazon leverages Pay-Per-Click (PPC) advertising to increase product visibility, drive targeted traffic,
and boost sales across multiple categories.

Key Strategies

    •   PPC Goals: Focus on search visibility and higher conversions for product-specific queries.

    •   Ad Types: Sponsored Product Ads, Brand Ads, and Display Ads targeting relevant keywords.

    •   Target Audience: Online shoppers searching for electronics, books, clothing, and more.

    •   Ad Placement: Search results and product detail pages on Amazon’s website & app.

    •   Ad Copy & Design: Clear, product-focused messaging with compelling visuals.

    •   Bidding Strategy: Automatic & manual bidding for optimal keyword targeting.

    •   Success Metrics: CTR*, conversion rate, CPC*, ROAS*, and total sales from PPC ads.

Example Campaign

Prime Day Deals PPC Campaign – Amazon sponsors ads targeting keywords like “Prime Day discounts”
and “best deals on electronics”, ensuring top search rankings during the event.

Amazon’s PPC strategy enhances brand visibility, ensures product discoverability, and drives strong
customer engagement.

*CTR (Click-Through Rate): Measures the percentage of people who click on an ad after seeing it.
*CPC (Cost Per Click): The amount paid for each click on an ad.
*ROAS (Return on Ad Spend): Evaluates revenue generated per dollar spent on ads.

                                              Mini Case Study 3

           Customer Engagement Strategies of Virgin Telecom (UK) & Reliance Jio (India)

Overview

Virgin Telecom (UK) and Reliance Jio (India) adopt distinct customer engagement approaches tailored to
    their respective markets. While Virgin Telecom emphasizes personalized service and loyalty programs,
    Jio focuses on affordability and accessibility to build strong customer relationships.

Key Strategies

       •     Customer Engagement:

                 o   Virgin Telecom: Proactive communication, personalized support, loyalty incentives.

                 o   Reliance Jio: Affordable, widely accessible customer service, prioritizing strong
                     relationships.

       •     Communication Channels:

                 o   Virgin Telecom: Live Chat, Social Media (Twitter, Facebook), Email, Phone Support,
                     Community Forums, Virgin Media App.

                 o   Reliance Jio: Live Chat, Social Media (Facebook, Twitter, Instagram), Phone Support,
                     Jio App, Retail Outlets.

       •     Customer Support:

                 o   Both brands offer 24/7 assistance, but Jio integrates retail outlets for in-person service,
                     enhancing accessibility.

       •     Customer Feedback & Loyalty:

                 o   Virgin Telecom: Surveys, online feedback, social interactions, and Virgin Red rewards
                     program offering exclusive deals.

                 o   Reliance Jio: Customer surveys, app feedback system, cashback, data packs, and
                     exclusive content.

       •     AI & Automation:

                 o   Both brands leverage chatbots and AI-powered automation for basic customer queries
                     and support efficiency.

Conclusion

Virgin Telecom excels in personalized engagement and loyalty-driven retention, while Reliance Jio focuses
    on mass-market accessibility and affordability. Their distinct strategies highlight how telecom
    companies adapt to market needs for customer satisfaction and brand loyalty.
