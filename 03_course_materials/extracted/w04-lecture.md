## Slide 7 — Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning (STP) Marketing Model

- The Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning (STP) model is a critical framework in marketing that helps businesses to focus on the right customers and effectively communicate their brand's value. Here's a breakdown of the model:
- 1. Segmentation (S)
- Segmentation is the process of dividing a broad consumer or business market, typically consisting of existing and potential customers, into sub-groups of consumers based on some type of shared characteristics. The goal is to identify segments that are most likely to respond similarly to marketing strategies.
- 2. Targeting (T)
- Once a company has segmented the market, the next step is targeting. Targeting involves selecting which segments the company will serve. It is essential to evaluate the potential of each segment and choose the ones that align best with the company's resources, capabilities, and objectives.
- 3. Positioning (P)
- Positioning refers to how a brand or product is perceived in the minds of consumers in relation to its competitors. Effective positioning helps a brand to stand out and deliver a unique value proposition (UVP) that resonates with the target audience.

- Source: Ries & Trout (2001)

## Slide 9 — 4. Sales Strategies and Building Relationships (effective communication)

- Building relationships:
- One of the core components of sales is relationship-building. Successful salespeople invest time in creating long-term relationships with customers, ensuring trust and satisfaction.
- Modern selling techniques use tools like CRM software, automated follow-ups, and personalized communication to nurture relationships and build customer loyalty.
- In sales fundamentals, persuasive communication techniques, such as active listening, problem-solving, and addressing objections, are key.
- Modern techniques may include digital tools like email marketing, live chat, social selling (via platforms like LinkedIn), and webinars to engage customers in a more personalized and convenient way.
- Sales Channels and Techniques:
- What channels will you use to reach customers? - Online stores, direct sales, social media, partnerships.
- Sales Process:
- Lead Generation - Identifying potential customers.
- Prospecting - Building relationships with prospects.
- Conversion - Turning prospects into customers.
- Source: Churchill & Peter (2015)

## Slide 11 — 6. Sale Closing Techniques

- Sales fundamentals emphasize the importance of closing techniques, including asking for the sale, creating a sense of urgency, and offering incentives.
- Modern selling includes closing strategies like ‘soft’ selling, which might involve allowing the customer to make decisions at their own pace, using customer testimonials, case studies, and leveraging online reviews to influence their choice.
