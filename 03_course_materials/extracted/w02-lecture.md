## Slide 6 — Social Media Marketing (SMM)

- [Image alt text: nike | Social Media Revolver]
- Using platforms like Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Twitter to reach customers and promote brand engagement.
- Social media marketing involves creating engaging content, running paid ads, and building community connections to strengthen brand presence and customer loyalty.
- Key Strategies:
  - Organic reach via posts and community building.
  - Paid ads (sponsored posts, stories, etc.) for targeted visibility.
  - Influencer collaborations and user-generated content.
- Nike uses Instagram to share motivational fitness content and connect with followers by showcasing customer stories and brand partnerships.
- Pros:
  - Broad reach and high engagement potential.
  - Real-time feedback from customers.
  - Highly targeted based on demographics, behavior, and interests.
- Cons:
  - Requires consistent content production.
  - Paid ads can be expensive with no guaranteed ROI.
  - Can be difficult to stand out in a crowded space.
- Source: Nazir et al., (2025)

## Slide 7 — Content Marketing

- Creating valuable, relevant content to attract and engage target audiences, positioning your brand as a thought leader.
- Content marketing focuses on producing educational, entertaining, or informative content to drive traffic and build relationships with customers.
- Key Components:
- Blogs, videos, podcasts, eBooks, infographics, and white papers.
- Content that addresses customer pain points and interests.
- HubSpot publishes blogs, ebooks, and webinars to educate their audience about inbound marketing, which attracts potential clients and builds trust.
- Pros:
- Builds long-term trust and loyalty.
- Improves SEO and drives organic traffic.
- Enhances brand authority.
- Cons:
- Time-consuming to create high-quality content.
- Results take time to show (long-term investment).
- Requires continual updating and optimization of content.
- [Image alt text: Blogging | HubSpot Marketing]
- Source: Krowinska et al., (2023)

## Slide 8 — Influencer Marketing

- [Image alt text: roger federer's rolex watch | Roger federer rolex, Roger federer, Rolex]
- Collaborating with digital influencers to promote products or services and drive consumer behavior.
- Influencer marketing leverages the credibility and trust of individuals who have a large following on social media to endorse brands or products.
- Types of Influencers:
- Macro-Influencers: Celebrities or public figures with a large following.
- Micro-Influencers: Niche influencers with smaller, highly engaged audiences.
- Nano-Influencers: Extremely small, but often hyper-targeted audiences.
- Roger Federer has been a key figure in Rolex's influencer marketing strategy, embodying the brand's values of elegance, precision, and excellence.
- Pros:
- High credibility and trust among followers.
- Can drive immediate consumer purchasing decisions.
- Highly engaging, especially for younger audiences.
- Cons:
- Can be costly, especially with top influencers.
- Risk of "over-saturation" and lack of authenticity.
- Difficulty in measuring ROI in some cases.
- Source: Yesiloglu & Costello (2021)

## Slide 9 — Email Marketing

- [Image alt text: Argos Marketing Strategy: How Argos Nailed Omnichannel | ContactPigeon]
- Email marketing involves sending targeted messages via email to nurture leads, retain customers, promote products/services, and build relationships.
- Despite being one of the oldest digital marketing channels, email remains highly effective for direct communication. It supports personalization, automation, segmentation, and performance tracking.
- Argos uses email marketing to promote limited-time offers, showcase new product ranges, and remind customers of items left in their online baskets—often tailored to their browsing and purchase history.
- Pros:
- Cost-effective with high ROI.
- Personalized and targeted messaging.
- Easy to automate and measure.
- Ideal for nurturing long-term customer relationships.
- Cons:
- Requires a clean, opted-in email list.
- Overuse can lead to unsubscribes or spam flagging.
- Design and content must stand out in cluttered inboxes.
- Source: Chaffey & Ellis-Chadwick (2019)

## Slide 10 — Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

- Optimizing your website and content to rank higher in search engine results pages (SERPs) for targeted keywords.
- SEO is a long-term strategy aimed at improving a site’s organic visibility and traffic. It involves technical SEO, content optimization, and backlink strategies.
- Google provides guidelines and tools, such as and the SEO Starter Guide, to help website owners improve their site's visibility. These resources cover topics like keyword optimization, mobile-friendliness, and creating high-quality content.
- Pros:
  - Long-term, sustainable traffic.
  - Increases organic visibility without paid ads.
  - High credibility for users.
- Cons:
  - Takes time to see results.
  - Requires ongoing optimization and updates.
  - Highly competitive and constantly evolving.
- [Image alt text: Advanced SEO (Search Engine Optimization) - iShack]
- Source: Rushing (2017)

## Slide 11 — Pay-Per-Click (PPC)

- [Image alt text: The Importance Of PPC Advertising For Private Label Products On Amazon ...]
- Running paid advertisements on search engines or social media platforms targeting specific keywords and audience segments.
- PPC offers immediate visibility for businesses willing to pay for ad space, and it targets consumers who are actively searching for products or services.
- Amazon uses PPC ads on Google to drive traffic to their site for various product searches, ensuring they capture customers' attention at the point of purchase intent.
- Pros:
  - Immediate visibility and results.
  - Highly targeted ads.
  - Measurable ROI.
- Cons:
  - Expensive, especially for competitive keywords.
  - Requires continuous investment.
  - Ad fatigue can set in quickly, reducing effectiveness.
- Source: Chaffey & Ellis-Chadwick (2019)

## Slide 12 — Customer Engagement and Support

- Strategies to proactively engage customers and provide support across digital channels, creating positive experiences and building brand loyalty.
- Customer engagement is all about fostering meaningful interactions through digital platforms, while support focuses on resolving issues and providing assistance in real-time.
- Key Strategies:
- Chatbots and AI-powered customer service.
- Real-time customer support through live chat, social media, and email.
- Community-building and customer forums.
- Zappos is known for its excellent customer service, offering a 365-day return policy and 24/7 live chat support.
- Pros:
- Builds stronger relationships and loyalty with customers.
- Reduces churn by addressing concerns promptly.
- Enhances customer satisfaction.
- Cons:
- Can require significant investment in technology and staff.
- Maintaining high levels of customer engagement can be challenging.
- Risk of automation feeling impersonal if not well-implemented.
- [Image alt text: Good Customer Service 101: Definition, Tips & 2024 Examples]
- Source: Rushing (2017)

## Slide 13 — Cloud-Based Automation Tools

- Tools that automate repetitive digital marketing tasks such as email campaigns, social media posting, and lead generation to improve efficiency.
- Marketing automation saves time, improves targeting, and ensures consistent messaging while allowing marketers to focus on more strategic initiatives.
- Key Strategies:
- Email marketing automation (e.g., welcome emails, cart abandonment).
- Social media scheduling and management tools.
- Lead nurturing and CRM systems.
- Salesforce provides robust cloud-based marketing automation solutions, including tools for email marketing, customer segmentation, and CRM. It allows businesses to automate customer journeys, send personalized email campaigns, and track customer behavior across multiple channels to improve engagement and conversions.
- Pros:
- Highly customizable automation workflows.
- Strong segmentation and targeting capabilities.
- Integrates with numerous third-party applications.
- Cons:
- Requires a learning curve for new users.
- Can be expensive for small businesses.
- May require ongoing optimization and adjustments to maintain effectiveness.
- [Image alt text: Salesforce Crm]
- Source: Yesiloglu & Costello (2021)

## Slide 14 — Integration of Digital Marketing Strategies

- Combining different digital marketing strategies to create a seamless and cohesive brand experience across multiple touchpoints.
- Integration ensures that businesses provide consistent messaging and engage customers across all platforms, from social media to email marketing to content creation.
- Coca-Cola integrates social media campaigns, video ads, influencer marketing, and content creation, providing a cohesive experience across platforms like YouTube, Instagram, and their website.
- Pros:
- Cohesive brand messaging.
- Improved customer experience and engagement.
- Better tracking of cross-channel performance.
- Cons:
- Can be resource-intensive.
- Requires synchronization across teams and platforms.
- Potential to dilute brand identity if not aligned properly.
