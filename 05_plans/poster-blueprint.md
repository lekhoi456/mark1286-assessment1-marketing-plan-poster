# Poster blueprint — P5 (updated for poster v02, 28 September 2026)

Purpose: turn the approved Copenhagen plan (`../02_research/marketing-plan/integrated-plan-copenhagen.md`, D-033) into eleven readable A0 panels, with word budgets and visuals fixed before layout. Current copy: `../07_drafts/poster-v02-2026-09-28.md` (v01 reviewed; fixes in `../07_drafts/reviews/poster-v01-review.md`). Layout details (orientation, tool, colours) are confirmed at P7; nothing here is artwork.

## 1. Format and reading route

- **Paper:** A0, 841 × 1189 mm (D-007). Proposed orientation: portrait, three columns under a title band, with a footer strip for names and the contribution statement. The group confirms at P7.
- **Reading route (brief order kept):** column 1 — Student/Business Details, Company/Business Introduction, Target Market Analysis; column 2 — Positioning Strategy, Branding and Identity, Digital Marketing Tactics, Sales Strategies; column 3 — Budget and Resource Allocation, Measurement and Evaluation, Creativity and Innovation, Alignment with Business Objectives. Headings use the brief's exact names.
- **Production (D-021, D-022):** AI-made hand-drawn style. A layout tool places the approved text, numbers and citations. An image model may draw decorative sketches only; it never writes letters, figures or the logo.
- **References:** kept as a full list under `## References` in the Markdown. Whether they go on the back, at the end or both is still Q-L3/Q-L4.

## 4. Density and legibility

v02 is 1,581 counted words including two tables. That is dense for A0 but carries the analysis D-022 requires. A rough fit check: three portrait columns of about 270 mm, at about 10 words per line and 90 lines per column at a 28 pt body, hold about 2,700 words before visuals; reserving 40% for visuals leaves about 1,600. This is an estimate, not a proof. At P7, check the actual-size proof: if body text falls below comfortable reading size, cut description first (company history, repeated labels), never the reason for a choice or a required element. Q-I3 closes only on an actual A0 proof.
