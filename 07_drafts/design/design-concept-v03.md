## 2. Honesty rules for visuals

| Visual | Rule |
|---|---|
| Timeline | Only the three dated facts in ②, including "As at 26 September 2026" |
| Two meters | Both dates shown as in the copy; no "cheaper" wording |
| Taxi receipt | Lines, amounts and shares exactly as the budget table; total DKK600,000 = 100%. No separate donut, so no new labels are needed |
| Staged-release bar | DKK177,000 at Gate A (month 4) and DKK265,000 in total at Gate B (month 6), as in the copy |
| Gate lights | Drawn **unlit** on the column 3 bar. Legend uses only words from the copy: "release", "hold", "stop" (Measurement decision line). A lit light would suggest a result that does not exist |
| KPI dashboard | Target marks only; no needle or bar may look like an achieved result. The ✏️ marker sits in the dashboard heading |
| Phone mock-up | Tagged "illustrative"; not an altered screenshot of the real app |
| 📌 / ✏️ markers | Teal pin = sourced fact; grey pencil = assumption or proposal; placed exactly where the copy places them |

## 3. Type and fit

| Element | Size |
|---|---|
| Title | about 96 pt |
| Campaign line (in ⑤) | about 48 pt, the most visible phrase after the title |
| Panel headings with stop numbers | about 40 pt |
| Decision lines | about 30 pt bold |
| Body | **22 pt** (floor 20 pt) |
| Tables | 18 pt (floor 16 pt) |
| In-text citations | at least 14 pt |

**Fit estimate [INFERENCE: average glyph width 0.48 em, leading 1.2, estimated visual allowances].** Against about 600 mm of body height, v05's columns need about 535, 685, 680 and 625 mm at 22 pt body and 18 pt tables, so **columns 2, 3 and 4 overflow at 22 pt**. At 20 pt and 16 pt they need about 460, 635, 600 and 550 mm: **only column 2 still overflows**, by about 30 mm (three lines). Details needs about 105 mm in the title band and Alignment about 80 mm in the finish strip at either size. At P7, proof columns 2, 3 and 4 at 100% first. If they overflow: (1) shrink visual frames in those columns (meters, swatches, journey strip), keeping the receipt and dashboard as the tables themselves; (2) use the 20 pt floor; (3) only then cut copy **in columns 2–3**, with group approval: the ACEA electric-car sentence (about 20 words) or the social-media statistic (about 13 words) in column 2, the soft-selling line (about 7 words) in column 3. Body text of 20–22 pt is below the common 24 pt poster guideline; 24 pt would need roughly 1,100 words [INFERENCE: text height scales with the square of the type size].

Fonts: one legible handwriting-style display font for headings and decision lines; one clean sans-serif for body, tables and citations. Colour: teal and yellow-gold sampled from the official logo file (no invented colour codes), near-black on off-white; every colour meaning also has a label or icon.
