# Design concept v01 — "One taxi ride through Copenhagen" (draft, before copy approval)

Status: draft direction for poster copy v03 (`../poster-v03-2026-09-28.md`), following D-034. This is not the final design brief: that follows group approval of the copy (skill step 8, master plan P7). Every string on the poster comes from the approved Markdown; AI images carry no letters, numbers or logos.

## 1. Format and grid

- **A0 landscape, 1189 × 841 mm.** Margins 25 mm.
- **Title band** (top, about 120 mm): title, campaign line "Know your ride before you book." as the second-largest text, Green SM logo (official file from greensm.com, not redrawn), and **① Student/Business Details** at the right end: names, numbers, business, the 📌/✏️ key.
- **Body** (about 620 mm): four columns of about 270 mm. A hand-drawn road starts under the title band and runs down each column, looping from the foot of one column to the head of the next. Each panel is a numbered stop sign on the road.
- **Footer** (about 50 mm): contribution statement (Q-S5), "References on the reverse" line if the lecturer confirms that route (Q-L3/Q-L4).

| Column | Stops (brief order kept) | Main visual |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ② Company/Business Introduction; ③ Target Market Analysis | Road timeline with dated pins; three "boarding gates", two crossed out, one open; persona sketch tagged "illustrative" |
| 2 | ④ Positioning Strategy; ⑤ Branding and Identity; ⑥ Digital Marketing Tactics | Two taxi meters side by side (DKK227 / DKK230, each with its date); logo and two colour swatches; five-stage journey strip with channel icons |
| 3 | ⑦ Sales Strategies; ⑧ Budget and Resource Allocation | Ride-shaped funnel (install → first ride → repeat); **budget as a printed taxi receipt** in app-screen style, beside a donut chart; two traffic lights for Gates A and B |
| 4 | ⑨ Measurement and Evaluation; ⑩ Creativity and Innovation; ⑪ Alignment with Business Objectives | **KPI dashboard** in app-screen style; **phone mock-up** with the reminder "Your usual Thursday ride. Pre-book it now."; chequered finish flag where O1–O4 arrows meet the company goal |

## 2. How each visual stays honest

| Visual | Rule |
|---|---|
| Timeline pins | Only the three dated facts in panel ②; "as at 26 September 2026" on the eight-market pin |
| Two meters | Show both dates: Green SM prices updated 24 June 2026; TAXA prices dated 1 December 2023. No "cheaper" wording |
| Taxi receipt | Lines, amounts and shares exactly as the budget table; total DKK600,000 = 100% |
| Donut | Six groups from the blueprint: professional time 46.00%, paid search and social 33.00%, street screens 7.20%, tools 5.00%, vouchers 2.00%, contingency 6.80% |
| Traffic lights | Amber = Gate A, month 4, DKK177,000; green = Gate B, month 6, DKK265,000 |
| KPI dashboard | Gauges mark **targets only**; no needle may suggest an achieved result. Each tile carries the ✏️ marker |
| Phone mock-up | Tagged "concept mock-up"; no real app screenshot altered to look like an existing feature |
| Fact/assumption markers | 📌 teal pin for sourced facts, ✏️ grey pencil for assumptions and proposals; the key sits with ① |

## 3. Type, colour and legibility

- **Hierarchy:** title about 110 pt; campaign line about 72 pt; panel headings about 44 pt with the stop number; **Decision lines about 34 pt bold**; body 26–28 pt; table text at least 22 pt; in-text citations at least 16 pt.
- **Fonts:** one legible handwriting-style display font for headings and decision lines, one clean sans-serif for body, tables and citations. Final choice at P7.
- **Colour:** teal and yellow-gold sampled from the official logo file (no invented colour codes); near-black text on off-white; amber and green only for the gates. Every colour meaning also has a label or icon.
- **Checks at P7:** actual-size proof section at 100%; whole poster viewed on a phone photo; contrast check; word-for-word comparison of the export with the approved Markdown.

## 4. AI-drawn decorative assets (no text, no logos)

| Asset | Prompt constraints |

| Copenhagen skyline strip (canal houses, bicycles, harbour) | Background band behind the title; generic, no landmark lettering |

Keep one ink-and-marker style across all assets so the AI-made hand-drawn look is consistent (D-021). Record the tool and prompts used for the AI-use disclosure (Q-L7).
