# Design concept v02 — "One taxi ride through Copenhagen" (draft, before copy approval)

Status: draft direction for poster copy v04 (`../poster-v04-2026-09-28.md`), following D-034 and the v03 reviews (`../reviews/poster-v03-review.md`). Replaces v01, which did not fit its own grid. Not the final design brief: that follows group approval of the copy. Every string on the poster comes from the approved Markdown; AI images carry no letters, numbers or logos.

## 2. Honesty rules for visuals

| Visual | Rule |
|---|---|
| Timeline | Only the three dated facts in ②, including "As at 26 September 2026" |
| Two meters | Both dates shown as in the copy; no "cheaper" wording |
| Taxi receipt | Lines, amounts and shares exactly as the budget table; total DKK600,000 = 100%. No separate donut, so no new labels are needed |
| Staged-release bar | DKK177,000 at Gate A (month 4) and DKK265,000 in total at Gate B (month 6), as in the copy |
| Gate lights | Drawn **unlit**, with a legend: green = release, amber = hold and fix, red = stop scale-up. A lit light would suggest a result that does not exist |
| KPI dashboard | Target marks only; no needle or bar may look like an achieved result. The ✏️ marker sits in the dashboard heading |
| Phone mock-up | Tagged "illustrative"; not an altered screenshot of the real app |
| 📌 / ✏️ markers | Teal pin = sourced fact; grey pencil = assumption or proposal; placed exactly where the copy places them |

## 3. Type and fit

| Element | Size |
|---|---|
| Title | about 96 pt |
| Campaign line (in ⑤) | about 48 pt, the most visible phrase after the title |
| Panel headings with stop numbers | about 40 pt |
| Decision lines | about 30 pt bold |
| Body | **22 pt** (floor 20 pt) |
| Tables | 18 pt (floor 16 pt) |
| In-text citations | at least 14 pt |

**Fit estimate [INFERENCE: average glyph width 0.48 em, leading 1.2, estimated visual allowances].** At 22 pt body and 18 pt tables, v04's columns need about 515, 670, 660 and 615 mm against about 580 mm available; at 20 pt and 16 pt, about 460, 635, 590 and 540 mm. v03 needed 650–990 mm per column at the v01 concept's larger sizes. Columns 2 and 3 are therefore at the limit even at 20 pt. At P7, proof columns 2 and 3 at 100% first. If they overflow: (1) shrink visual frames, keeping the receipt and dashboard as the tables themselves; (2) use the 20 pt floor; (3) only then cut copy, starting with the persona sentence, the social-media statistic and the soft-selling line (about 40 words), with group approval.

Fonts: one legible handwriting-style display font for headings and decision lines; one clean sans-serif for body, tables and citations. Colour: teal and yellow-gold sampled from the official logo file (no invented colour codes), near-black on off-white; every colour meaning also has a label or icon.
