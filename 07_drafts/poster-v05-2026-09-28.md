## Target Market Analysis
<!-- budget: 180 -->

**Decision: self-paying residents who already ride taxis.**

**STP model, three segments compared:**

- ✏️ Organisations: not targeted, as accounts are not evidenced in Denmark.
- ✏️ Short-stay visitors: not targeted, as trips seldom repeat.
- ✏️ Self-paying residents: concentrated marketing.

**Segmentation bases:**

- ✏️ Demographic: adults 18+. 📌 Copenhagen Municipality has 670,389 residents, and 44.3% of households are one person (Statistics Denmark, 2026b; Statistics Denmark, 2026a, our calculation). <!-- E-031 E-033 -->
- ✏️ Geographic: the confirmed service area. 📌 At launch, Inner Copenhagen from 08:00 to 20:00 (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 175). <!-- E-182 -->
- 📌 Behavioural: price (57%) and habit (22%) topped the reasons of 79 Capital Region riders who chose Uber, Bolt or TaxiToGo over a traditional taxi firm (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 150-151). <!-- E-024 -->
- 📌 Psychographic: 11% of them felt safer seeing driver details and tracking or sharing the trip (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 151). <!-- E-187 --> ✏️ Our reading: they want control of the trip. With meters level with TAXA 4x35, clear terms and help must win the switch.

✏️ Illustrative persona, not a real person: lives alone, rides a few times a month, keeps one app, checks the price first.

## Positioning Strategy
<!-- budget: 135 -->

**Decision: win on clear terms and help, not price.**

✏️ **Positioning statement and unique value proposition:** for Copenhagen residents who take taxis, Green SM is the locally run electric taxi that is easy to judge before booking and easy to get help from afterwards, because it employs its own drivers.

**Benefit positioning, two meters at one price level** (10 km, 12-minute weekday trip):

- 📌 Green SM day meter, prices updated 24 June 2026: DKK227 (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b, our calculation). <!-- E-015 -->
- 📌 TAXA 4x35 door price: DKK230, dated 1 December 2023 (TAXA 4x35, no date). <!-- E-028 -->

📌 App fares have a higher published maximum, so ✏️ we promise clear terms, not a lower price.

✏️ Electric cannot lead: 📌 68.5% of Denmark's new cars in 2025 were battery-electric, calculated from European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association (ACEA) (2026) figures. <!-- E-039 -->

## Digital Marketing Tactics
<!-- budget: 125 -->

**Decision: one tactic per decision stage, one local page.**

1. ✏️ Need recognition and information search, pay-per-click and SEO: service-area taxi searches lead to one page with map, hours, fares and help (content marketing). DKK108,000.
2. ✏️ Evaluation, social media marketing: fare and help explainers. DKK90,000. 📌 Social media user identities equal 78.9% of Denmark's population (Kemp, 2025a). <!-- E-042 -->
3. ✏️ Purchase: DKK30 off a first ride, for up to 400 riders.
4. ✏️ Post-purchase, email marketing: opt-in follow-up with the trip reference and a pre-book reminder. 📌 The app already books up to 30 days ahead (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-016 -->

✏️ Awareness after Gate B: 200,000 Digital Copenhagen impressions at 📌 DKK191 per thousand (AFA Decaux, 2026). <!-- E-029 -->

**Integrated digital marketing:** one message and one trip reference, so channels can be compared.

## Sales Strategies
<!-- budget: 140 -->

**Decision: the sale is a completed, paid trip, sold direct.**

**Sales channel:** direct to consumer, through Green SM's app and website.

**Sales process as one ride:**

1. ✏️ Lead generation: the digital tactics bring residents to the page.
2. ✏️ Prospecting: one opt-in message to people who installed but have not ridden.
3. ✏️ Conversion: the capped voucher closes the sale without false urgency.
4. ✏️ After-sales service: a trip reference and a help route, staffed outside this budget and promoted only once it meets its standard.
5. ✏️ Relationship selling: consented reminders win the second and third ride.

**Value-based selling** on fare rules and help, rather than competing on discounts. **Soft selling:** invite reviews, never pay for them. 📌 Drivers are employees in the initial phase (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date a), <!-- E-011 --> ✏️ so briefings and an in-car help card make each ride part of the sale.

## Creativity and Innovation
<!-- budget: 105 -->

**Decision: a promise riders can check, built from their own data.**

### Phone mock-up, illustrative

"Your usual Thursday ride. Pre-book it now."

- ✏️ **Personalisation:** with consent, reminders before regular trips, using only the rider's own history.
- ✏️ **Omnichannel:** one trip reference across page, app, in-car card, follow-up and help, so no rider explains a problem twice.
- ✏️ **Revenue model:** pay per trip, a usage-based model. A subscription model waits, as the plan expects under two rides per new rider within 90 days.
- ✏️ **AI chatbots** wait for a human-run FAQ, because one wrong fare answer breaks the promise. **Neuromarketing** is not used, and surveys measure clarity instead.
