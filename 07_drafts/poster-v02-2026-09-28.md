## Company/Business Introduction
<!-- budget: 135 -->

Green SM began in Vietnam, where its Xanh SM taxi service launched in Hanoi on 14 April 2023 (VietnamPlus, 2023a). <!-- E-002 --> It counted eight markets, including a Dutch pilot, as at 26 September 2026 (Green SM, 2026a). <!-- E-009 -->

Copenhagen, launched on 30 July 2026, is its first European market. The core product there is Green SM Car, app-booked electric taxi rides (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). Green SM owns the cars and employs the drivers (Green SM, 2026d; Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 174). <!-- E-008 E-015 E-019 -->

**Drivers of change (evolving business models):** Uber and Bolt apps book cars run by other operators, and existing dispatch offices rely on self-employed operators (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 119, 174). <!-- E-184 E-186 --> Green SM runs its own service, and this plan markets that control.

## Target Market Analysis
<!-- budget: 205 -->

**STP model:** of three segments (self-paying residents, organisations, short-stay visitors), concentrated targeting picks residents, because organisation accounts are not evidenced in Denmark and visitors seldom repeat (our judgement).

**Priority segment:** residents of Green SM's service area who already take taxis for some regular trips and pay for them.

- **Demographic:** adults in Copenhagen Municipality, which has 670,389 residents and where 44.3% of households are one person (Statistics Denmark, 2026b; Statistics Denmark, 2026a). <!-- E-031 E-033 -->
- **Geographic:** the confirmed service area only. At launch it was Inner Copenhagen, 08:00 to 20:00 (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, p. 175). <!-- E-182 -->
- **Behavioural:** nationally, 40-50% of recent taxi users have one taxi app and 30-40% have none. Price (57%) and habit (22%) led the choices of 79 Capital Region riders who picked Uber, Bolt or TaxiToGo over a traditional taxi firm (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 150-151, 194). <!-- E-023 E-024 -->
- **Psychographic (our hypothesis):** because meter prices are level, we expect these riders to switch only if the area and fare are easy to check and help is easy to get.

Illustrative persona, not a real person: lives alone, rides a taxi a few times a month, keeps one app and checks the price first.

## Positioning Strategy
<!-- budget: 140 -->

**Positioning statement and unique value proposition:** for Copenhagen residents who already take taxis, Green SM is the locally run electric taxi that is easy to judge before you book and easy to get help from afterwards, because it owns its cars and employs its drivers.

**Benefit positioning, because meter prices are level.** For a standard 10 km, 12-minute weekday trip, Green SM's day meter gives DKK227 (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). TAXA 4x35 lists a DKK230 door price, dated 1 December 2023 (TAXA 4x35, no date). <!-- E-015 E-028 --> App fares have a separate published maximum, so we promise clear fare terms, not a lower price.

**Electric supports the claim but cannot lead it:** 68.5% of new cars registered in Denmark in 2025 were battery-electric, calculated from European Automobile Manufacturers’ Association (ACEA) (2026) figures. <!-- E-039 -->

## Branding and Identity
<!-- budget: 100 -->

**Brand identity kept**, because the April 2026 rebrand unified it across markets (Green SM, 2026f): the Green SM name, the leaf-shaped logo in teal and yellow-gold, and the Danish core values "Safety - Discipline - Reliability - Professionalism - Local Respect" (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date c). <!-- E-010 E-012 -->

**Campaign line:** "Know your ride before you book." Clear area. Clear fare. A clear way to get help.

**Consistent brand messaging:** Danish first, English second. The same line and trip reference appear on ads, the page, app messages, in-car cards and screens, so "Reliability" and "Local Respect" become a promise riders can check.

## Digital Marketing Tactics
<!-- budget: 165 -->

Each tactic serves one stage of the consumer decision-making process:

- **Need recognition, pay-per-click:** Danish and English taxi searches in the service area and hours. DKK108,000.
- **Information search, SEO and content marketing:** one local page showing the map, hours, fare rules and how to get help.
- **Evaluation, social media marketing:** short explainers on how the fare works and how help works. Social media user identities equal 78.9% of Denmark's population (Kemp, 2025a). <!-- E-042 --> DKK90,000.
- **Purchase:** DKK30 off a first ride, capped at 400 new riders.
- **Post-purchase, email marketing:** opt-in follow-up with the trip reference and a reminder to pre-book a regular trip up to 30 days ahead (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date b). <!-- E-016 -->

Street screens add awareness only after Gate B: 200,000 impressions on Digital Copenhagen at DKK191 per thousand (AFA Decaux, 2026). <!-- E-029 -->

**Integrated digital marketing:** one message, one page, one trip reference, so channel results can be compared.

## Sales Strategies
<!-- budget: 150 -->

**Sales channel:** direct to consumer, through Green SM's own app and website. **Sales process:** the sale is a completed, paid trip.

1. **Lead generation:** search, social and screens bring residents to the local page.
2. **Prospecting:** app users who have not ridden get one opt-in message about area and help.
3. **Conversion:** the capped DKK30 voucher is the closing incentive, with no false urgency.
4. **After-sales service:** every trip has a reference. Problems go to a help route, staffed outside this budget and promoted only once it meets its standard.
5. **Relationship selling:** consented reminders for regular trips win the second and third ride.

**Value-based selling:** show fare rules and help, not discounts. **Soft selling:** invite app-store reviews and never pay for them. Drivers are Green SM employees in the initial phase (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date a), <!-- E-011 --> so briefings and an in-car help card make each ride part of the sale.

## Creativity and Innovation
<!-- budget: 130 -->

**Personalisation:** with consent, the app reminds riders before a trip they take regularly and offers to pre-book it in Danish or English. It uses only the rider's own trip history.

**Omnichannel:** one trip reference links the page, app, in-car card, follow-up and help route, so no rider explains a problem twice.

**Revenue model:** pay per trip, a usage-based model. A subscription model waits, because the plan expects fewer than two rides per new rider within 90 days of the first ride.

**AI chatbots** are tested only after a human-run FAQ shows which questions recur, because one wrong fare or refund answer would break the promise. **Neuromarketing** is not used, and short surveys measure clarity instead.

## Alignment with Business Objectives
<!-- budget: 95 -->

Green SM told Denmark's competition authority that it puts stable local operation before rapid expansion. The authority judged that becoming known would need substantial marketing investment (Konkurrence- og Forbrugerstyrelsen, 2026b, pp. 175, 179). <!-- E-020 E-021 -->

**Alignment with objectives:** spending grows only while service and repeat use hold, so marketing never outruns operations. **Long-term sustainable growth:** repeat trips, not one-off discounts, decide whether year two scales up. That serves Green SM's mission to "Provide a green, convenient, and sustainable mobility solution" (Green SM Denmark ApS, no date c). <!-- E-185 -->
