## 4. New findings

### Blocking

**N1 (C7). The copy does not fit the concept at its own type sizes.** Concept: "four columns of about 270 mm", body "about 620 mm", "body 26–28 pt; table text at least 22 pt". My line-fill estimate (glyph 0.46–0.5 em, leading 1.15–1.25) puts text and tables alone at 525–708, 660–816, 674–847 and 804–945 mm for columns 1–4, against 620 mm. With body at 22 pt, column 4 still needs 698–772 mm before the phone and flag. The 110 pt title alone spans about 940–1,150 of 1,139 mm, leaving no room for ① "at the right end". Risk: shrunken type or squeezed visuals. Revise: proof column 4 at actual size before layout, then cut and rebalance, because smaller type alone cannot close the gap; start with the title "Green SM in Copenhagen", as the campaign line repeats the rest.

### Significant

**N2 (C3, C5). The help promise lost its release condition.** v02's "promoted only once it meets its standard" became "a help route staffed outside this budget." Plan §10: "P1 cannot be advertised until met", yet paid media starts in month 3, before Gate A. Revise: restore the v02 clause.

**N3 (C4, C5). Two numbers lost their derivation.** "about 676 rides recover DKK46,000" dropped v02's "378 new riders"; Gate B's "at most DKK1,829" sits beside the target "DKK524 or less" without plan §10's downside label. Risk: C4.2's "reproducible calculation" fails, and a floor 3.5 times the target looks lax. Revise: "378 new riders taking about 676 rides"; "at most DKK1,829 (downside case)".

**N4 (C2). The segment-to-position link lacks a comparator.** "11% of those riders felt safer seeing the driver … ✏️ With meter prices level, we expect them to switch only if…". Those riders left "a traditional taxi firm", 57% for "en lavere pris" (p. 150); parity is shown only with TAXA 4x35, and plan §5 lists Uber and Bolt prices as unknown. P. 151 says "chaufførens info". Risk: "level" reads as level with the platforms, and the 11% never reaches the promise. Revise (plan §6 wording): "…seeing the driver's details, tracking and sharing the trip felt safer (…). ✏️ We read this as wanting control of the trip. With our meter level with TAXA 4x35's door price, we expect…".

**N5 (C2, C4). Five markers are misplaced or missing.** "📌 Electric cannot lead:" (a judgement); "📌 Geographic: the confirmed service area only" (a rule; plan §14 calls the area a gap); "📌 … 200,000 impressions" (AFA p. 3 gives rates, not quantity); the unmarked positioning statement (D-032: "a hypothesis requiring proof"); "📌 This serves the mission" (our link). Risk: one 📌 on an assumption discounts every 📌. Revise with the poster's own split, e.g. "✏️ Electric cannot lead: 📌 68.5%…"; "✏️ 200,000 impressions at 📌 DKK191 per thousand"; "✏️ Positioning statement…".

**N6 (C4, C7). The traffic lights encode gates, not outcomes.** "Amber, Gate A"; "Green, Gate B"; "A failed gate stops spending". Plan §10: Gate A fail = "hold media, fix the cause, re-test"; Gate B fail = "stop scale-up"; DKK265,000 is cumulative. Revise: each light shows green continue, amber hold and fix, red stop scale-up; "DKK265,000 committed in total by month 6".
