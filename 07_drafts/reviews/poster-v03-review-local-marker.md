## 4. New findings

### Blocking

None in the copy.

### Significant

**N1. 📌 on judgements (C2–C4, X2).** "📌 Electric cannot lead"; "📌 **Brand identity kept**"; "📌 … 200,000 impressions" (budget: assumption); "📌 This serves the mission"; "📌 Geographic: the confirmed service area only"; "📌 Drivers are employees…, so briefings…". DKK227 and 44.3% are calculations (E-015, E-033) with no label. Risk: one wrong marker discredits the whole key. Revision: split the lines, e.g. "✏️ Electric cannot lead: 📌 68.5%…"; "at 📌 DKK191 per thousand"; "day meter, calculated from its published rates: DKK227".

**N2. v02 safeguards deleted (C3.3, C1, C2.1, C4.2, C6.2).** "A clear way to get help" runs from launch, but plan §10 says "P1 cannot be advertised until met". Risk: help is promised before it is proven. Revision: restore "promoted only once it meets its standard", "as at 26 September 2026" (D-019; the release is dated 25 September), "adults in Copenhagen Municipality", "and short surveys measure clarity instead" and "378 new riders … of DKK600,000" (+20 words).

**N3. Concept overflows its grid (C7.1–C7.2).** Column 4 holds 444 words plus a nine-row table, a phone and a flag in 270 × 620 mm. [INFERENCE: estimated metrics] At 26 pt and 1.2 leading, a bare column holds about 500 words. The 22 pt table alone needs about 300 mm, so column 4 needs 1.3–1.5 times its height. Risk: at P7, body text drops below 26 pt or the visuals shrink. Revision: cut to about 1,400 words starting from column 4 (Sales step 1 repeats Digital's channels; drop the "finish line" sentence; merge "Tracking tool" and "Review rhythm"), then proof column 4 first.

**N4. Gate outcomes and lights (C4, C5.3, C7).** "✏️ Amber, Gate A…"; "✏️ Green, Gate B…"; "✏️ A failed gate stops spending."; concept: "Amber = Gate A … green = Gate B". Plan §10: a failed Gate A means "hold media, fix the cause, re-test"; a failed Gate B means "stop scale-up". Risk: a lit green light reads as "passed" (against the concept's own rule), and the copy contradicts the approved rule. Revision: "Gate A, month 4 (DKK177,000 committed): fail holds media while the cause is fixed and re-tested. Gate B, month 6 (DKK265,000): fail stops scale-up." Draw the lights unlit, with a legend: green release, amber hold, red stop.

### Minor

- M1 (C3). "not discounts" sits beside the DKK30 voucher, which reads as a contradiction. Revise to: "rather than compete on discounts; one capped voucher lowers first-try risk" (plan §7).
- M2 (C5.2). "Paid media cost per new rider": a marker who includes the screens gets DKK638, not DKK524. Rename it "Search and social cost per new rider" (plan §10).
- M3 (C3). "Need recognition, pay-per-click": a search happens after recognition (w03-lecture s5), so the concept is misapplied. Use the plan's stage "Need / search".
- M4 (C5.1). "O1 to O4 lead to the finish line" is empty without the drawing. Map it as plan §12 does: O4 and the gates keep operation stable; O1 and O2 make Green SM known; O3 repeat trips test growth.
- M5 (X6). Concept strings not in the Markdown ("Green SM prices updated 24 June 2026", "as at 26 September 2026", donut names, "install") break "Every string on the poster comes from the approved Markdown". Add or drop them.
- M6 (C2.2). "the local electric taxi" overclaims; the plan says "locally run".
- M7 (C7). "boarding gates" for segments blurs the meaning of Gates A/B.

### Defensible challenges

- E-187 cuts both ways: platform apps already offer tracking, and p. 150 quotes Uber's "prisestimat før bestilling". Pre-booking clarity is parity, so lead with help from employed drivers.
- An equal 11% called their choice random (p. 151).
- The segment is unsized: demographics cover the municipality, but service covered Inner Copenhagen (v01).
- The app-screen receipt, dashboard and phone look least hand-drawn (C7.4; D-034).
