# Draft review checklist

Copy into the relevant version review under `07_drafts/reviews/`; tick only after observing the evidence. Source requirements: brief, rubric Part A, requirements matrix. Rubric Part B is an operational interpretation, not an official band grid.

## Inputs and mechanical checks

- [ ] Correct business, offer, country, period and group-approved plan used; no decision assumed from silence.
- [ ] Poster contains all eleven exact element headings and all three names/numbers.
- [ ] Panel budgets established and counted; no imported diary word limit.
- [ ] Budget lines reconcile to total and 100%; shares correspond to amounts; currency and period explicit.
- [ ] Every budget basis resolves to a source or explicitly approved assumption; cost × quantity calculations checked.
- [ ] KPI table links objective, meaningful target, tracking tool and review rhythm; units/periods/baselines handled accurately.
- [ ] Reference registry/build/check-draft run on the actual version; every cited work has a readable verified PDF.
- [ ] Claim-level PDF passages and locators manually checked; projections, assumptions and results distinguished.
- [ ] Concept register/list checked; outside concepts flagged and PDF-backed; interpretation not passed off as quotation.
- [ ] Presentation gate and anti-slop detector ran; findings resolved or narrow genre exceptions recorded with reasons.
- [ ] Any OCR/audio quotation was checked against the original, not trusted solely because text matched extraction.

## Assessed marketing content

- [ ] Company/country context explains the marketing decision, not just corporate history (C1).
- [ ] Segments use demographic, psychographic and behavioural evidence; priority and rejected alternatives explained (C2).
- [ ] Positioning and UVP distinguish the offer credibly; branding aligns with segment/position (C2).
- [ ] Digital tactics and sales techniques are concrete, feasible and connected to the customer journey and revenue (C3).
- [ ] Budget covers actual implementation resources, not company revenue relabelled as spend (C4).
- [ ] KPIs test business objectives rather than only vanity activity (C5).
- [ ] Emerging concept has an applied mechanism, benefit, constraint and simpler-alternative comparison (C6).
- [ ] All elements form one coherent plan; long-term sustainability/growth considered without unsupported claims.

## Poster and presentation

- [ ] The poster itself carries required analysis; no essential criterion exists only in the speech.
- [ ] Contribution statement describes actual confirmed work, not agent-assigned future tasks.
- [ ] Script has all three speakers, handovers and allocations; spoken language is natural rather than essay prose.
- [ ] Timing estimate run; real rehearsal status and observed duration separately recorded.
- [ ] Q&A owners confirmed; answers acknowledge evidence and assumption limits.
- [ ] When design exists: inspect actual A0-scale proof and submitted-format export, not just Markdown.
- [ ] Figures/tables use checked data; captions/citations readable; no image-model-generated lettering or numbers.
- [ ] Reference-side/upload handling and AI/production permissions accurately recorded.

## Independent review and approval

- [ ] Two independent reviews use the actual rubric, not a remembered marking framework.
- [ ] Each reports criterion score/max, weak-point quotation, evidence, uncertainty and concrete fixes.
- [ ] Parent records disagreements and resolution; serious defects fixed in a new version.
- [ ] All affected artefacts updated after any fact/number change; requirements status evidence recorded.
- [ ] Group approval recorded for content and, later, actual final design. No guaranteed-grade claim.
