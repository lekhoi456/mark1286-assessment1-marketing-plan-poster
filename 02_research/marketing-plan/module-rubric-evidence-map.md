## 4. Source boundaries that must survive compression

1. **Separate populations.** DK-08's percentages come from 79 Capital Region respondents answering a particular app-choice question, not all Danish or Copenhagen residents. DK-09 is a national taxi-user result. DK-13 is national climate opinion. Amsterdam O&S city attitudes are not taxi-user willingness to pay. Vietnam surveys have multi-city/national and usage-based sampling limits. Do not combine these into an observed named persona.
2. **Separate price categories.** `04_references/green-sm-denmark-aps-ndb-green-sm-car.pdf` p. 3 contains the company's published meter tariffs and separate app maxima, dated 24 June 2026; the PDF was captured in Comet on 27 September. These are neither a government cap nor a real matched app quote. DK-29 is the national regulatory cap; DK-31 is TAXA's displayed tariff; DK-33 is a time-limited promotion. The Dutch government maximum applies to street-hail/rank trips in the saved guidance, while TCA's Schiphol offer is a published fixed-price proposition. None establishes a general lowest-price claim.
3. **Recognise existing service.** Both root product PDFs (`green-sm-denmark-aps-ndb-green-sm-car.pdf` and `green-and-smart-mobility-netherlands-b-v-ndb-green-sm-car.pdf`) p. 3 advertise scheduled booking up to 30 days and cancellation before arrival. Proposed marketing may explain or test this existing claim; it must not describe it as a newly invented Green SM feature. The Dutch product PDF pp. 2–3 contains fare conditions; no numeric tariff appears in the extracted text inspected here. Do not generalise that observation into “the company publishes no prices”.
4. **Separate supply from availability.** Registered/shipped/planned vehicles are not a staffed operating fleet. DK-20 and DK-27 need date/scope qualifications; Dutch pilot coverage cannot be expanded by inference. A pre-booking claim does not guarantee airport permission, vehicle availability or successful fulfilment.
5. **Separate rivals from unsupported superiority.** Uber Comfort Elektrisch is a current Amsterdam counterexample to electric comfort as a unique proposition. Viggo is now Bolt in Denmark. Employed drivers are a company-model fact, not proven unique or safer. The Dutch 65% Uber estimate in a TCA-affiliated column is not an official market share and is unnecessary to the favoured option.
6. **Separate law from plans and controls.** The NL pack preserves a 2023 zero-emission proposal and later contrary implementation evidence; do not present the old plan as current taxi law. Privacy, direct-marketing, subscriptions and environmental-claims compliance require local legal verification; proposed data minimisation is not proof of compliance. E-013's direct-tailpipe language is not lifecycle carbon neutrality.
7. **Separate story from rank.** Green SM's own first European entry is supported. “First Asian taxi company in Europe” is not. K1–K6 market-selection evidence and any weighted comparison do not predict C1–C7 marks.

## 5. From options to an assessable argument

The causal chain must be visible in the eventual approved work: **evidenced local problem → selected segment and unmet need hypothesis → position and proof → practical acquisition/sales journey → funded resources → measurable outcome and stop/change rule → business objective**. Every arrow must explain a decision. A label such as STP, AI or sustainability without that connection is insufficient under D-022.

For the Copenhagen S1 option, DK-09/DK-36 explain the challenge of trying a new app; DK-37 makes stable local repeat use a defensible proposed objective; DK-08 warns that price and habit may defeat a service-clarity proposition. Options §3 therefore specifies evidence that could reject it. For Amsterdam, verified pilot geography limits targeting before campaign scale; existing electric competitors require proof beyond drivetrain. These are different conditional arguments, not the same plan with a country name changed.

Before poster copy: log the group's country, service, segment, position, brand and period choices; select from the integrated evidence while retaining qualifications; approve justified costs/targets and implementation responsibilities. The source index/ledger already supplies canonical keys and E IDs, but no approved citation set exists. Before final submission: inspect actual design, references, upload packaging, rehearsal and genuine contributions under C7/X1–X6.
