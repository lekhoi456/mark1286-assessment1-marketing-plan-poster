## Prompt (copy everything inside the box)

```text
You are a marketing analyst. Document how Green SM (brand of GSM Green and Smart Mobility JSC, Vietnam; called Xanh SM until an April 2026 rebrand; all-electric ride-hailing, taxi, motorbike and delivery services using VinFast vehicles) markets and sells its services, from its founding in 2023 to September 2026. Cover Vietnam in most depth and note differences in Laos, Indonesia, the Philippines, India, Kazakhstan, Denmark and the Netherlands.

Report, with a source (full URL and publication date) next to every claim:

1. Brand: positioning statements, slogans and taglines used by the company; the April 2026 rebrand from Xanh SM to Green SM (reasons given by the company, what changed: name, logo, colours, app); brand colours; any published brand rankings or awards.
2. Products and pricing: all service lines (for example Green SM Car, Mini, Premium, Bike, Express delivery, Airport, Tour, Platform, business transport), how fares compare with competitors according to independent sources, and any membership or subscription programme (name, price, benefits, launch date).
3. Digital marketing: owned app features (AI, personalisation, loyalty), social media accounts and notable campaigns (TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, Zalo, Instagram), influencer or celebrity partnerships, content marketing, search advertising, and any published results of these campaigns.
4. Sales and partnerships: business-to-business corporate accounts, airport and hotel partnerships, the Green SM Platform model for independent drivers and vehicle owners (revenue share, conditions), the Green Alliance Frontier partner programme, links with Vingroup companies (VinFast, Vinhomes, VinClub) and any cross-selling.
5. Performance: any published figures on trips, users, market share, revenue, profit or losses, funding and valuation, with the date, and who reported them. Mark company claims separately from independent estimates.
6. Customer and driver perception: independent surveys, app-store ratings (with date), media coverage of service quality, safety incidents, driver protests or labour disputes, and regulatory problems in any market.
7. Criticism and risks reported by independent analysts: losses, dependence on VinFast and Vingroup, competition from Grab and others, sustainability claims.

Then:
A. Summarise in a table: marketing activity | market(s) | date | evidence of result | source.
B. List conflicting figures between sources.
C. List what is NOT publicly disclosed (for example marketing budget, customer acquisition cost, retention) so we know which numbers must be treated as assumptions.

Rules:
- Use primary and reputable sources: company and Vingroup releases, regulators, audited reports, reputable Vietnamese and international media, industry reports, peer-reviewed research. Avoid unsourced blogs.
- Do not estimate or extrapolate numbers that are not published; write "not found".
- Always state the date a figure refers to. Keep company claims and independent figures separate.
```
