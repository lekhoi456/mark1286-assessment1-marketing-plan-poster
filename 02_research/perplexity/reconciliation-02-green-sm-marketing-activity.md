## 2. Status definitions and evidence routing

- **Verified, bounded:** an inspected PDF supports the narrow statement; retain source date, market and attribution. Verification of an advertised mechanism is not verification of its result.
- **Corrected/contradicted:** a saved source gives a different fact/date or directly rules out the asserted scope. A newer offer can supersede an older one without making the older offer false.
- **Qualified/disputed:** evidence exists but needs a material limit, including company self-report, incomplete method or counterevidence.
- **Not yet verified:** no supporting original PDF was inspected for that precise lead. This does **not** mean false or non-existent.
- **Partly inaccessible:** only an accessible portion of a paywalled source is saved; no reconstruction of the hidden remainder.

Root PDFs and canonical metadata are in `04_references/`; the [dossier source map](../business-dossier/green-sm-company-dossier.md#11-canonical-source-map-and-acquisition-provenance) resolves the old `comet-` labels. Staging links remain provenance, not the bibliography authority. Corporate author identities follow actual legal footers; bilingual exceptions disclose the shared business ID and hashes. Existing country sources are reused: BusinessLine is `bl-mumbai-bureau-2026`; Philippines is `vingroup-2025a`; VinClub is `vingroup-2025b`. ## 7. Acquisition gaps and handover boundaries

1. **Integration complete, 28 September 2026.** The 13 company-source candidates and 34 page-matched quotations are now promoted/registered in the 97-source collection and the canonical `../business-selection/market-source-index.json`; `../evidence-log.md` gives durable evidence IDs. BusinessLine, Q&Me and VinClub reuse their existing entries; no duplicate source is created. Duplicate captures and generic home/news pages remain excluded from the selected set. The dossier source map resolves old Comet capture labels to canonical keys.
2. **Prioritise the chosen focus-market offer.** Actual local app quotes and full local passenger/driver terms matter more than verifying every historical Vietnam promotion. Denmark's printed tariff already exists; app maxima are not transaction prices. Netherlands/India pricing incompleteness needs visual/app verification, not a claim of non-publication.
3. **Keep current practice separate from optional transfer.** Vietnam prepaid voucher packs, household utility, SME sign-up and Vietjet distribution are verified examples. Their introduction/adaptation in Europe would be a proposal, not an existing local practice, and the existing European advance-booking facility cannot be sold as a new invention.
4. **Retain outcome gaps.** No reliable local baselines for acquisition cost, conversion, repeat rate, membership redemption, partner revenue, utilisation, cancellation, arrival reliability, customer lifetime value, country profitability or marketing spend have been established by this reconciliation. These become explicitly proposed assumptions only after the group approves a plan; they do not become facts because Perplexity lists them as ‘not publicly disclosed’.
5. **Retain counterevidence.** Use the full Rakuten population limit and competitor strengths; preserve the Indian complaints/paywall caveat; acknowledge hybrid models and existing electric competitors. No mark prediction follows from source availability.
6. **Quarantine 02a/02b.** Their existence is recorded, their cut-offs are not run times, and their unrelated subject matter is excluded. No source acquisition, financial copying or alternative-business work follows from those imports.

The reconciliation verifies selected mechanisms and corrects specific source/date/scope errors. It does not establish campaign success, choose a market, approve a segment or create primary research.
