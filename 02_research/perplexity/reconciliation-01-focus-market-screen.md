# Reconciliation 01: Green SM focus-market screen

Prepared 28 September 2026. **Research synthesis and claim-level reconciliation, not poster copy, a numerical market recommendation or a group decision.** This file reconciles the material market-selection leads in `prompt-01-result.md` and the market-related parts of `prompt-02-result.md` against the four country evidence packs, the canonical [market source index](../business-selection/market-source-index.json) and current reference registry, with temporary manifests checked for mapping differences. The original outputs remain unchanged. Perplexity is a discovery tool, never the cited source.

## 1. Scope, method and source gate

The [frozen selection method](../business-selection/focus-market-selection.md), §§3–4, distinguishes an **eight-market preliminary screen** from **four-finalist evidence collection**. The screen used leads where verified evidence was unavailable; it was not eight completed country audits. Denmark and the Netherlands were retained under D-023's European preference; Vietnam and India were retained as the stronger non-European challengers identified by that preliminary screen. Indonesia, the Philippines, Kazakhstan and Laos did not advance to equivalent evidence-pack collection. That is a scope decision, not proof that their data do not exist or their markets are inferior.

The four finalist packs are [Denmark](../market-analysis/denmark-evidence-pack.md), [the Netherlands](../market-analysis/netherlands-evidence-pack.md), [India](../market-analysis/india-evidence-pack.md) and [Vietnam](../market-analysis/vietnam-evidence-pack.md). Their original introductions describe staging-era status and proposed keys. For citation routing, the **current `04_references/references.json` takes precedence** over those old key proposals. The inspected registry contained 84 entries: 17 earlier sources and 67 selected country-source additions. This count establishes inventory, not 84 independent confirmations or 84 cleared claims.

### Classification

- **Confirmed:** the saved source supports the precise, dated lead. Company statements remain company statements.
- **Qualified:** only a narrower claim is supported, or measurement, date, source dependence, dispute or legal currency requires a material limit. A historical offer can be confirmed historically and qualified for current use.
- **Contradicted:** retrieved evidence conflicts with the asserted fact or scope. A failed search alone does not qualify.
- **Unretrieved:** the precise lead has no inspected original PDF in this reconciliation; this includes an uncollected original replaced by a better source. It does not mean false.

Every row below supplies its anchor and permitted-use boundary. Pages mean physical PDF pages. A bare source key identifies its file through the current registry. **Pack-only** means the named pack row and its staging PDF, not a claim that the source was selected or registered. Confirmed/qualified here does not replace source-identity verification and the evidence-log/claim-scope gate before drafting. The parent reports completion of registry identity verification, including explicitly documented manual exceptions; that is not blanket verification of every Perplexity lead. No project-wide verifier, external audit or online Green SM access was run by this reconciliation task.

## 7. Cross-source convergence, dependence and unresolved contradictions

| Theme | Evidence assessment and synthesis | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|
| European customer evidence exists | **Strong for existence; bounded for targeting.** Denmark's authority survey, national travel survey (`christiansen-anderson-2025`, pp. 2, 6), statistics and Eurobarometer converge on a usable context. | Price evidence is a hierarchy, not a single number | **Strong classification convergence.** Legal maxima, own posted meter/app schedules, historical discounts and one-off transactions are different evidence types across all four countries (DK-R8–R11; NL-R6–R7; VN-R11; IN-R7–R8). | Actual average fares, contribution margin, CAC or promotional profitability. |
| Apparent market leadership contradictions are often denominator problems | **Strong conceptual resolution, variable empirical reliability.** Usage/MAU can overlap; GMV differs from trips; supply differs from demand; forecasts differ from observations (VN-R1–R7; IN-R3–R4; NL-R2–R3; DK-R4). | Validation of opaque consultancy estimates merely because the metrics can be reconciled. Mordor's dispute remains. |
| Announcements are not outcomes | **Strong source-boundary convergence.** Launch offers, fleet ambitions, memberships, driver pay and pilot terms establish advertised actions, not incremental sales or delivery quality (DK-R6–R7; NL-R7–R8; VN-R11; IN-R6–R8). | Campaign effectiveness, realised earnings, current operating fleet, completed divestment or delivered expansion. |

**Independence is claim-level.** Government/statistical bodies and an independent empirical survey add different evidence from Green SM/Vingroup releases. However, an official decision can quote a company statement, a competitor's observation or confidential submissions; retain that attribution. A VOV/Vietnam News/EQS/Media Outreach reproduction does not become independent corroboration merely because its host differs. Reuters reporting may contain independent analysis alongside company plans. The Decision Lab landing page and blog share one study. A TCA-affiliated column is independent of Green SM but interested in the competitive dispute. Industry-association counts, regulator data, commercial panel surveys and company reports should not be assigned one undifferentiated quality label.

The principal contradictions are therefore resolved differently: **correct** enacted-policy and expired/current errors; **separate** measurement bases and event dates; **qualify** interested-source estimates; **retain unresolved** charging-baseline definitions, consultancy validity and incomplete current-law checks. No averaging, majority vote of reprints or blanket “all leads verified” conclusion is justified.
