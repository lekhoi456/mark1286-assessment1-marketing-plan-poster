# Green SM marketing and sales, 2023–September 2026

Green SM has marketed itself around three assets that conventional ride-hailing platforms cannot easily copy: a conspicuous cyan all-electric fleet, centrally controlled “five-star” service standards, and integration with VinFast/Vingroup. Its commercial model has evolved from company-operated taxis and employed drivers toward a hybrid system combining owned fleets, corporate transport, subscriptions, loyalty, tourism partnerships and independent VinFast owners on Green SM Platform.

**Evidence labels:** **[Company]** means Green SM, VinFast or Vingroup; **[Independent]** means media, market research, regulators or third-party databases. Company operating and environmental figures are not treated as independently audited.

### Competitive pricing

- A reliable, contemporaneous, independent Vietnam fare basket comparing identical routes, times, surge conditions and promotions across Green SM, Grab, Be, Mai Linh and Vinasun was **not found**.
- Rakuten Insight’s 2025 survey indicates that customers associated **Be**, rather than Green SM, most strongly with competitive prices and deep promotions; Green SM’s perceived strengths were driver service, vehicle cleanliness and comfort. **[Independent survey; 7,436 respondents]** Source, 20 May 2025: <https://insight.rakuten.com/2025-ride-hailing-app-landscape-in-vietnam/>. ## Criticism and risks

- **Asset intensity:** Company-owned vehicles and employed drivers provide consistent branding and service control but create high capital, charging, maintenance and fleet-utilization requirements compared with Grab’s predominantly asset-light model. Reuters-linked reporting identifies this as the central economic question in exporting the model. **[Independent analysis citing Reuters]** Source, 2 September 2026: <https://business-news-today.com/gsm-targets-1m-vinfast-cars-as-2028-hong-kong-ipo-tests-asset-heavy-ride-hailing-model/>. [business-news-today](https://business-news-today.com/gsm-targets-1m-vinfast-cars-as-2028-hong-kong-ipo-tests-asset-heavy-ride-hailing-model/)
- **VinFast dependence:** Green SM exclusively operates VinFast vehicles and has been a major related-party customer. VinFast reportedly delivered approximately 98,741 EVs and 51,679 electric scooters cumulatively to GSM and subsidiaries from March 2023 through 2025, making the two businesses commercially interdependent. **[Independent analysis citing VinFast filings/Reuters]** Source, 2 September 2026: <https://business-news-today.com/gsm-targets-1m-vinfast-cars-as-2028-hong-kong-ipo-tests-asset-heavy-ride-hailing-model/>. [business-news-today](https://business-news-today.com/gsm-targets-1m-vinfast-cars-as-2028-hong-kong-ipo-tests-asset-heavy-ride-hailing-model/)
- **Related-party concentration:** Approximately 72% of VinFast’s 2023 vehicle sales reportedly went to related parties, primarily GSM; VinFast’s 2025 filing reportedly reduced the related-party delivery proportion to 27.3%, with GSM still the majority of those deliveries. This supports scale but complicates assessment of arm’s-length vehicle demand and pricing. **[Independent analysis citing filings/Reuters]** Source, 2 September 2026: <https://business-news-today.com/gsm-targets-1m-vinfast-cars-as-2028-hong-kong-ipo-tests-asset-heavy-ride-hailing-model/>. ## Conflicting figures

1. **Vietnam market leadership:** Mordor-derived estimates show Green SM at 37.41% in Q4 2024 and 39.85% in Q1 2025, ahead of Grab. Rakuten’s 2025 consumer survey shows only 32% using Green SM versus 54%–55% using Grab. These are not necessarily contradictory: Mordor appears to estimate revenue/market value, while Rakuten measures respondent usage and allows consumers to use multiple apps. Sources: 17 March 2026, <https://www.vietdata.vn/vi/post/billion-dollar-race-in-vietnam-s-ride-hailing-market-xanh-sm-and-its-multinational-rival>; 20 May 2025, <https://insight.rakuten.com/2025-ride-hailing-app-landscape-in-vietnam/>. [vietdata](https://www.vietdata.vn/vi/post/billion-dollar-race-in-vietnam-s-ride-hailing-market-xanh-sm-and-its-multinational-rival)

2. **Preference versus market share:** Decision Lab reported 36% preference for Xanh SM and 62% for Grab in 2024, while Mordor later reported Green SM leading by revenue share. Preference, usage and revenue share must not be presented as interchangeable. Source: 26 November 2024, <https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2024/11/27/vietnams-richest-man-is-vying-to-dethrone-asias-ride-hailing-king-grab>. [aljazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2024/11/27/vietnams-richest-man-is-vying-to-dethrone-asias-ride-hailing-king-grab)

3. **India INR8/km fare:** Some automotive media described INR8/km as launch pricing, but Green SM told *The Hindu BusinessLine* before launch that circulating pricing and fleet numbers were speculative and unconfirmed. The official 5 June launch release published a 50% introductory discount but not a standard INR/km tariff. Sources: 2 June 2026, <https://caronphone.com/news/vinfast-cabs-are-coming-green-sm-electric-taxi-service-out-on-june-5/>; 22 May 2026, <https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/green-sms-cyan-ev-fleet-35000-driver-guarantee-signal-vingroups-big-india-bet/article>; 4 June 2026, <https://www.greensm.com/in-en/news/green-sm-launches-green-sm-limo-a-fully-electric-taxi-service-in-india-today>. 4. **“Unicorn” value versus US$20 billion:** VND35.5 trillion/US$1.35 billion is registered charter capital, while US$20 billion is a prospective IPO valuation discussed with advisers. Neither is a verified arm’s-length fundraising valuation. Sources: 11 January 2026, <https://news.tuoitre.vn/vietnamese-billionaire-pham-nhat-vuongs-gsm-reaches-billion-us-dollar-valuation-103260112122104312.htm>; 31 December 2025, <https://vnbusiness.vn/gsm-explores-overseas-ipo-options-with-valuation-discussions-around-us20-billion.html>. Sources: 29 December 2025, <https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-12-30/vietnam-s-gsm-weighs-overseas-ipo-eyes-20-billion-market-value>; 2–3 September 2026, <https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/vinfast-linked-taxi-firm-gsm-eyes-us-eu-expansion-ahead-hong/>. Articles that describe it as revenue “brought to Vingroup” should not be used as GSM turnover.
