# Green SM Market Evidence Base

Evidence quality varies sharply by country. Vietnam, Indonesia, India and the Netherlands have usable commercial evidence; Laos, Kazakhstan and Denmark lack transparent market-size data; and reliable, city-level market shares are largely unavailable outside Vietnam and India.

**Cut-off:** 27 September 2026. “Company claim” means a statement originating from Green SM/GSM or its distributed press release and not independently audited.

## Vietnam

### Market and competitors

- **Market size:** Mordor Intelligence estimates—not official statistics—value Vietnam’s ride-hailing market at **USD 1.06 billion in 2025**, rising to **USD 1.25 billion in 2026** and **USD 3.05 billion by 2031**, a forecast CAGR of **19.53% for 2026–2031**. Source: Mordor Intelligence/Research and Markets, 3 August 2026; URL: [https://www.marketresearch.com/Mordor-Intelligence-LLP-v4018/Vietnam-Ride-Hailing-Share-Trends-46079121/](https://www.marketresearch.com/Mordor-Intelligence-LLP-v4018/Vietnam-Ride-Hailing-Share-Trends-46079121/) [marketresearch](https://www.marketresearch.com/Mordor-Intelligence-LLP-v4018/Vietnam-Ride-Hailing-Share-Trends-46079121/)
- **Competition:** A Q1 2025 Mordor estimate reported Green SM at **40%**, Grab at **36%** and Be at **6%**; the open source does not disclose whether share was measured by revenue, bookings or trips, nor its sample or calculation method. Source: Vietnam.vn summarising Mordor Intelligence, 9 August 2025; URL: [https://www.vietnam.vn/en/grab-muon-thong-tri-dich-vu-goi-xe-o-dong-nam-a-viet-nam-indonesia-se-la-ngoai](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/grab-muon-thong-tri-dich-vu-goi-xe-o-dong-nam-a-viet-nam-indonesia-se-la-ngoai) [vietnam](https://www.vietnam.vn/en/grab-muon-thong-tri-dich-vu-goi-xe-o-dong-nam-a-viet-nam-indonesia-se-la-ngoai-le)
- Decision Lab’s Q4 2025 Connected Consumer survey found that more respondents had used Xanh SM Taxi than GrabCar during the preceding three months, but the publicly accessible summary provides neither percentages nor sample size. Source: Decision Lab, 23 March 2026; URL: [https://www.linkedin.com/posts/decision-lab_decision-lab-connected-consumer-report-q4-activity-7442108756638969857-DLQb](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/decision-lab_decision-lab-connected-consumer-report-q4-activity-7442108756638969857-DLQb) [linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/decision-lab_decision-lab-connected-consumer-report-q4-activity-7442108756638969857-DLQb)

### Consumer behaviour

- A 2024 TGM Research survey used **558 computer-assisted web interviews**, covering adults aged 18–55+ with age- and gender-representative quotas; nearly half identified competitive pricing as an app-choice factor, and nearly half regarded ride-hailing as safer, particularly for nights and events. Women placed particular weight on safety features, while older users valued quick pickup. Source: TGM Research, 7 April 2025; URL: [https://tgmresearch.com/press-tgm-ride-hailing-report-vietnam-2024.html](https://tgmresearch.com/press-tgm-ride-hailing-report-vietnam-2024.html) [tgmresearch](https://tgmresearch.com/press-tgm-ride-hailing-report-vietnam-2024.html)
- Country-specific survey evidence with disclosed samples on cash versus digital payment, motorcycle-versus-car preference and willingness to pay for electric rides: **not found**.

Source: Vietnam Law & Legal Forum, 6 March 2026; URL: [https://vietnamlawmagazine.vn/seeking-a-workable-safety-net-for-ride-hailing-drivers-79904.html](https://vietnamlawmagazine.vn/seeking-a-workable-safety-net-for-ride-hailing-drivers-79904.html) [vietnamlawmagazine](https://vietnamlawmagazine.vn/seeking-a-workable-safety-net-for-ride-hailing-drivers-79904.html)

## Conflicting figures

- **Vietnam market size:** Mordor’s newer release reports **USD 1.06 billion for 2025**, while another summary describes a **USD 1.7 billion market** without clearly identifying the reference year or consistent market boundary. URL: [https://b-company.jp/ride-hailing-market-in-vietnam-q1-2025-xanh-sm-leads-the-market/](https://b-company.jp/ride-hailing-market-in-vietnam-q1-2025-xanh-sm-leads-the-market/); published 26 May 2025. [b-company](https://b-company.jp/ride-hailing-market-in-vietnam-q1-2025-xanh-sm-leads-the-market/)
- **Vietnam shares:** one source reports Grab at **60% at end-2023** and Be at 9%, while the Q1 2025 Mordor summary reports Green SM 40%, Grab 36% and Be 6%. The rapid timing change may be real, but differing definitions and unpublished methodologies prevent a clean trend comparison. [b-company](https://b-company.jp/vietnam-ride-hailing-market-current-landscape-and-bolts-potential-entry/)
- **Vietnam arithmetic inconsistency:** one summary says Green SM held **40% of a USD 1.7 billion market while generating USD 400 million**; USD 400 million is not 40% of USD 1.7 billion, indicating mixed periods, definitions or an erroneous summary. URL: [https://b-company.jp/ride-hailing-market-in-vietnam-q1-2025-xanh-sm-leads-the-market/](https://b-company.jp/ride-hailing-market-in-vietnam-q1-2025-xanh-sm-leads-the-market/); 26 May 2025. [b-company](https://b-company.jp/ride-hailing-market-in-vietnam-q1-2025-xanh-sm-leads-the-market/)
- **India definitions:** PMR reports a **USD 23.7 billion broad taxi market** with ride-hailing at 68.4%, while another PMR page states **USD 18.5 billion for India’s ride-hailing-taxi market**. These are reasonably close but not interchangeable. [persistencemarketresearch](https://www.persistencemarketresearch.com/market-research/india-taxi-market.asp)
- **Philippine chargers:** an IEA-derived secondary table lists only **274 charging points in 2025**, while the Philippine DOE reported **nearly 1,100 stations by August 2025**. The likely explanation is points versus locations, reporting cut-offs or incomplete international coverage. [pna.gov](https://www.pna.gov.ph/articles/1257432)
- **Denmark charging network:** Mobility Denmark reports **30,439 public points at end-2024**, whereas a February 2026 article claims **50,000 public points**. A separate guide gives about 25,000, making definition and date verification essential before using the figure. [eletric-vehicles](https://eletric-vehicles.com/general/denmarks-public-ev-charging-network-reaches-50000-points/)
- **Denmark Uber availability:** a tourism guide published in June 2026 claims Uber had not returned, but Dantaxi’s own page confirms Uber acquired it in May 2025 and distributes licensed Dantaxi rides through the Uber app. Dantaxi is the more authoritative current source. [copenhagentourism](https://www.copenhagentourism.org/taxi-copenhagen-uber/)
- **Netherlands chargers:** Statbase gives **207,300 points in 2025**, while IEA reports **210,000 at year-end 2025**. This small difference is consistent with timing, rounding or database revisions. [iea](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-charging-chap-6-and-10)
- **Green SM Amsterdam fleet:** a local report says “several hundred” vehicles, while the company’s pilot release gives no fleet number. The local figure should remain provisional until Green SM or the licensing authority publishes an exact count. [bkan](https://www.bkan.nl/vinfast-probeert-het-opnieuw-als-taxidienst/)
- **India digital audience:** one compilation reports more than 500 million social users, while another reports 467 million. Different inclusion rules, dates and treatment of WhatsApp likely explain the difference. [theglobalstatistics](https://www.theglobalstatistics.com/india-social-media-statistics/)

## Biggest evidence gaps

1. **Comparable market economics.** There is no common, independently verified series for national and city-level gross bookings, trips, average fares, active riders and operator shares across all eight markets. Available consultancy estimates use materially different definitions.

2. **Green SM operating performance.** Country-level active fleet, active drivers, trips per vehicle, customer acquisition cost, repeat rate, cancellations, airport mix and business-account revenue are mostly undisclosed. These metrics are essential for sizing campaigns and judging whether promotions create repeat demand.

3. **Decision-grade consumer research.** Laos, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Denmark and the Netherlands lack recent public surveys with country or city sample sizes covering price elasticity, safety, payment preference, service switching, EV attitudes and willingness to pay. Green SM should commission a standardised city survey and app-choice experiment in every launch market.
