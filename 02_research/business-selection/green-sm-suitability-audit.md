## 4. The scope decision (Q-S9, for the group)

> Decided 27 September 2026 (D-023): the group chose one focus market, preferring Denmark or the Netherlands, to be confirmed by data; the poster states eight markets in total (D-024). The market choice is made in `focus-market-selection.md`. The options below are kept as the record of what was considered.

The brief asks "How did you segment and prioritize your target markets?" and the rubric gives 35% to budget, KPIs and the digital and sales plan together. The scope has to make those panels concrete.

| Option | What the poster covers | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Whole company, all eight markets | One plan for Green SM everywhere | Matches "not only Vietnam" fully | Eight competitive settings on one A0 sheet; budget and KPIs become generic; heaviest evidence load (eight markets x every panel) |
| B. The six Asian markets | The student's six; Europe mentioned as context | Matches the June 2026 footprint | Still six very different markets; has to explain why Europe is excluded; same depth problem as A |
| C. One market, footprint as context | Company background shows all eight markets; the plan is for one market | Deepest, most specific plan; realistic budget and KPIs | Loses the multi-country angle the group asked for, unless the choice of market is argued |
| **D. Portfolio screen, then a focus plan (recommended)** | Background shows all eight markets. Target Market Analysis compares and **prioritises markets** with explicit criteria (market size, competition, regulation, EV readiness, Green SM's position), then segments customers in the priority market. Digital, sales, budget and KPIs are detailed for the priority market (or a two-market cluster) over 12 months; Alignment shows how it feeds the company's expansion | Uses the brief's own prioritisation question; keeps the eight-market reality; keeps budget and KPIs specific | The prioritisation needs comparable data for all markets (prompt 01 is written for this) |

Agent's recommendation: **D**. Which market ends up as the priority is decided later by the group, after the market screen, not now.

Decisions that follow once the scope is set (proposals, not decisions):

1. Planning period: 12 months from the pitch month is a common default for a marketing plan.
2. Service focus: car taxi, bike, premium, business transport or delivery, or a combination; the choice should follow from the priority market's customers.
3. Business objective the plan serves: for example trips or market share, brand awareness after the rebrand, driver supply, or profitability of a market. Each implies different KPIs.
