# Evidence pack: the Netherlands (focus Amsterdam)

Prepared by the EvidenceNetherlands subagent, 27–28 September 2026, for the focus-market scoring (K1–K6, `02_research/business-selection/focus-market-selection.md`). This pack reports evidence only. It gives no scores. All PDFs are in `04_references/_staging/netherlands/`. None is registered yet. Keys and file names are proposals. The parent assigns same-year suffixes.

**Parent integration update, 28 September 2026:** the acquisition-stage registration status above is historical. Selected sources/claims are integrated into the 97-source registry and canonical `../business-selection/market-source-index.json`/evidence ledger; extra pack-only PDFs remain reserves. Use canonical keys: `ilt-nda` is **Marktbeeld Taxi**, `ilt-ndb` is **Taximonitor**; same-author/year suffixes below were provisional. The 2023 zero-emission-taxi proposal does not prove an enacted 2025 mandate; later implementation evidence/current-law limits govern. A national app share, unspecified contract-market share and city pilot coverage are different measures. The inspected product page establishes pricing conditions, not a numeric tariff or non-publication everywhere.

No greensm.com page was opened (D-025). Green SM's pilot terms come from the parent's saved copy of the release, `04_references/green-sm-2026a-netherlands-pilot.pdf`, which was read locally.

## 1. Scope

- **Focus area:** City of Amsterdam, including Weesp. Pilot districts: Centrum, West and Zuid. National data (the Netherlands) is used where no city figure exists, and this is marked.
- **Date range of evidence:** data from 2022 to July 2026. Sources are dated May 2023 to 26 September 2026 (see §2). All pages were accessed on 27–28 September 2026.

  - Web pages were saved with `python3 -B 06_workflow/scripts/save_web_pdf.py URL NAME.pdf --out-dir 04_references/_staging/netherlands --delay 5000` (8000 for slow pages), via the wrapper `/tmp/nl_save.sh`, which also ran `head -c 5`, `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext | wc -w`.
  - Direct PDFs (ACEA, O&S synthesis, Schiphol, Eurobarometer) were downloaded with `curl -L -A 'Mozilla/5.0' -o …`, and each has a `%PDF-` header.
  - Quote pages were located with a Python helper over `pdftotext` (raw and `-layout`).
  - Middle pages were rendered with `pdftoppm -f 2 -l 2 -r 40 -png` and viewed as three contact sheets (`montage`). The `/tmp` renders were deleted afterwards.
- **Failures and blocks.**
  - `opendata.cbs.nl`, `datasets.cbs.nl` and `statline.cbs.nl` did not respond (curl code 000), so no StatLine tables could be used. The CBS news page on www.cbs.nl worked.
  - `hogeraad.nl`: connection reset, and 403 to curl. The ruling was saved from rechtspraak.nl instead.
  - `iea.org`: Cloudflare challenge; the snapshot was deleted.
  - `amsterdam.nl`: 403 to curl. Headless Chrome returned "Pagina niet gevonden" for `/verkeer-vervoer/taxi/` and "Page not found" for `/en/news/emission-free-zones/`. Both snapshots were deleted.
  - `taxiregels.nl`: connection timed out (curl and Chrome).
  - `schiphol.nl`: 403 to curl. The traffic review PDF was taken from schipholannualtrafficreview.nl instead.
  - `parool.nl`: 403 to curl.
  - `uber.com/nl/nl/blog/taximarktnederland/` was saved, but it dates from 29 Nov 2017 and duplicates ILT, so it was deleted.
  - `tca.nl` is an unrelated IT firm. TCA taxi is at tcataxi.nl.
- **Eurobarometer.** The survey page is an Angular app, so it was rendered with headless Chrome `--dump-dom` to list deliverable IDs. The NL factsheet was found by probing IDs: 100743 is the Dutch-language NL factsheet. The English NL factsheet ID was not found. The Annex (99200) holds the country tables. The full report (98729/98730) with technical specifications was not saved.
- **Data limits.**
  - The Bolt snapshot prints only the first 4 of 6 categories in its carousel. "Electric" was seen in the live HTML only and is not claimed.
  - BKAN is paywalled after two paragraphs.
  - The gemeente-amsterdam-2023 snapshot is 50 pages because of embedded document viewers; only p. 1 is used.
- **Dates and naming.**
  - The DataReportal page is dated 5 November 2025, so the key is kemp-2025 although the title says "Digital 2026".
  - The Schiphol report shows no publication date (PDF metadata: 19 May 2026), so the key is royal-schiphol-group-nd.
  - The date rolled over during the work: bolt-nd and ploos-van-amstel-2026 were accessed on 2026-09-28; all others on 2026-09-27.
- **Independence notes.** Walther Ploos van Amstel signs the 2026 column "Verbonden aan TCA", a direct competitor, so his 2023 blog and 2026 column are not neutral. CleanTechnica repeats parts of the company release. VOV is Vietnamese state media.
- **Compliance.** No greensm.com page was opened. The parent's local file `04_references/green-sm-2026a-netherlands-pilot.pdf` was read with `pdftotext` only. No registry, evidence-log or decision-log files were edited. No builds or tests were run.
