## 1. Scope

- **Focus area:** Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC). Since the 2025 provincial reorganisation, official 2025 tables give HCMC on two bases: the old city (9.69 million) and the enlarged city that now includes the former Bình Dương and Bà Rịa–Vũng Tàu (13.80 million). The plan must state which one it uses.
- **Date range of evidence:** law from 2020 (repealed) and 2024–2026; consumer and market data mostly 2024 to Q4 2025; regulation and competition news up to 12 September 2026. All accessed 27 September 2026.
- **Where the data are national, not city-level:** TGM (national sample), Decision Lab (national online panel), DataReportal (national), IEA (national). Rakuten reports "key cities" (Hanoi and HCMC combined). Q&Me sampled HCMC, Hanoi, Bắc Ninh and Quảng Ninh.
