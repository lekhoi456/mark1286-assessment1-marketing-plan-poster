## 1. Scope

- **Focus area:** Delhi National Capital Region (NCR). Green SM's own release says it operates "in key areas of the Delhi National Capital Region" (`green-sm-2026b` p. 3, already saved as E-006). Independent reporting places operations in "Gurugram, central and south Delhi, and select parts of Noida" (`das-gupta-2026` p. 1). NCR spans three jurisdictions: NCT of Delhi, Haryana (Gurugram) and Uttar Pradesh (Noida). Only the Delhi (NCT) rules were retrieved.
- **National context** is used where no Delhi figure exists (digital use, EV sales, app usage, market size). Each row says which applies.
- **Date range:** sources dated November 2023 to 21 September 2026, plus a peer-reviewed survey fielded in 2021 (`gupta-sinha-2022`) and the Delhi taxi fare revision of January 2023. Population figures are official projections (base: Census 2011).
