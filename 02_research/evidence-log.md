## Claim types and entry rules

- **Fact:** observed or reported information supported by the saved source. Preserve attribution where the source is the business making a claim.
- **Interpretation:** the group's inference from specified evidence; identify its limits and plausible alternative explanation.
- **Calculation:** inputs each link to evidence IDs or assumption IDs; record formula, units, period and rounding in the marketing-plan model.
- **Assumption:** a planning input proposed by the group/agent, not an observed number. Assign A-001 onwards in the model and record approval before adoption.
- **Target:** a proposed outcome for a specified period, tied to an objective and measurement method. It is not historical performance.

Accepted status requires a saved PDF, complete source registry entry, a read locator and a claim that the passage actually supports. Metadata verification alone is insufficient. Use candidate / verified / qualified / rejected / superseded; retain rejected or superseded records with reasons.
