# Section 8 — approved compact content and layout

Prepared 5 October 2026. Compact content/layout approved under D-108; production authorised with an explicit stop at Section 8. Basis: D-105 targets and the v06 KPI content/tracking proposal. Fitted v01 artwork awaits student visual review.

## Visual proposal

Use five icon-led KPI cards with dominant numbers, a compact three-icon service strip, two month-review markers and a distinct Brand Upside area. Follow the cell's wheel-arch cut-out rather than shrinking an entire rectangular panel into it. Put the proposed tracking flow in the available lower-left area. No logo. Verified hand-drawn letters, flat broad colour regions and heading-width underline.

Consideration is a committed brand metric, not a paid conversion. Asterisks identify only the paid-campaign customer/trip/cost metrics. Paid acquisition means search/social media spend divided by distinct paid-attributed first paid riders, not full company CAC. Repeat includes only riders whose first-trip cohort has a complete 90-day observation window. The interim 35% threshold differs from the year-end 50% target.

Brand Upside describes wider ambition, not quantified extra customers or guaranteed causal branding lift. Organic/referral demand is measured separately and customer/trip IDs deduplicated. There is no invented multiplier. Consideration uses the same S1-in-area baseline/endline survey, separately from operational data.

The Big Data dashboard is a proposed Copenhagen integration, not a verified deployed feed. Corporate capability remains covered by E-207/v05 tracking notes. Daily service monitoring remains in full copy; the compact flow shows weekly actions/monthly cohort reviews. The full fare/area-clarity target >=80% remains in the earlier service specification and post-trip survey notes, but is omitted from this proposed compact display to prioritise commercial and service outcomes. The student accepts this compact omission under D-108. Exact observation volumes are not invented.
