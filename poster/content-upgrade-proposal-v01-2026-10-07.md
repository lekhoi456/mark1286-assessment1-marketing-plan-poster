## 5. Q&A answers to the marker's questions

1. **590 cars, only 2,500 rides?** The regulator calls the 590 potential capacity, not active taxis. The 2,500 counts only riders our paid campaigns acquire; organic and referral demand is tracked separately.
2. **Where does DKK600,000 come from?** It is a group-chosen market-entry envelope (rejected alternative: DKK240,000 learning pilot), built bottom-up: 460 h × DKK600, media envelopes, the published screen rate card and tools at DKK2,500 a month. Release is gated: DKK177,000 by M4, DKK265,000 by M6. At an assumed DKK68 contribution per ride, recovery needs about 8,800 rides, so year-one break-even is not expected.
3. **Price-conscious yet quality-positioned?** Published fares are at parity with Dantaxi and Drivr (DKK39 start), so price cannot differentiate. A clear fare and a first-ride voucher lower trial risk; the service standard drives repeat.
4. **Why a smaller voucher than the launch?** The launch offer ended on 30 September 2026. After launch we test the smallest incentive that converts and judge it on 90-day repeat, not on trial alone.
5. **Green SM versus Viggo?** Electric options are shared, as Section 3 says. The regulator's 2026 decision states that existing dispatch offices have no employed drivers; Viggo now runs as "Bolt" in Bolt's app. The difference is direct control of app, fleet and employed drivers.
6. **+10 pp from which baseline?** The same S1 survey runs in M1–2 and M12; about 400 respondents per wave detect a 10-point change.
7. **AI dispatch is operations?** In a service, reliability is the product. Dispatch delivers the *More reliable* promise and is measured by match time, fulfilment and cancellations, which drive repeat.
