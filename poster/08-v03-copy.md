# Section 8 v03 — proposed target dashboard

## 8. Success Dashboard
<!-- budget: 380 -->

**Proposed targets:** awareness → paid trial → repeat.

### O1 · Awareness

Consideration +10 percentage points from the M1–2 baseline by M12.

### O2 · Paid trial

378 first paid riders, M1–12. Count completed paid trips only. Remove refunds. Search conversion 3% and social 1.5%, each per all paid clicks. Search/social cost per first paid rider ≤DKK524.

### O3 · Repeat

Fully observed 90-day cohort → paid repeat ≥35%.

### O4 · Service integrity

- Completed / accepted bookings ≥95%.
- Service-caused cancellations / accepted bookings ≤2%.
- Referenced complaints answered within 24h ≥90%.
- Fare/area terms clear: ≥80% yes.

Installs are leads, not sales. DKK524 covers search/social only, not total acquisition cost. Advertise verified areas with usable offers.

### Gate A · M4

Completion ≥95%, service-caused cancellations ≤2%, referenced complaints answered within 24h ≥90%, search conversion ≥1.5%, social ≥0.8%. Agree minimum observed volume with operations. Pass → release M5–6. Fail → hold media, fix and re-test.

### Gate B · M6

Mature 90-day repeat ≥20% and first-rider cost ≤DKK1,829 → continue. Repeat ≥35% → release street screens. Fail either threshold → stop scale-up.

### M12 decision

Renew, revise or stop for year two.
