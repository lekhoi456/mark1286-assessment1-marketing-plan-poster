## 8. Success Dashboard


### Committed plan targets · M1–12

1,200 new first paid riders.

2,500 completed paid rides from this acquired customer group.

90-day repeat: ≥50% among first paid riders with a full observation window.

Paid search/social acquisition cost: ≤DKK165 per new paid-attributed rider.

These are plan targets, not achieved results or guaranteed forecasts. The numerical basis is the existing upside paid-acquisition scenario. Count unique riders and completed, settled trips. remove duplicates and refunds. Do not treat installs as sales or attribute organic demand to paid media to improve its acquisition cost.

### Brand consideration target

+10 percentage points among target riders from the M1–2 baseline by M12.

Use the same S1-in-area consideration survey definition at both waves. This is a separately measured marketing commitment. ad impressions do not demonstrate consideration lift.

### Wider brand growth · ambition, not committed volume

Brand-led demand, organic discovery and recommendations can add customers beyond the campaign cohort. Track their customers/trips separately from paid attribution and compare overall service-area results with the baseline. Do not add overlapping channel counts or call attributed demand incremental without a suitable comparison.

No numerical multiplier is set. A larger total-volume ambition requires a monthly demand/cohort scenario and service-capacity check. Branding spillover is not included in the paid-acquisition calculation.

### Budget-release reviews

M4: ≥200 unique first paid riders. completion ≥95%, service-caused cancellations ≤2%, referenced complaints answered within 24h ≥90%. Review permits M5–6 budget release only if service and the acquisition test support it.

M6: ≥450 unique first paid riders cumulatively. paid-media acquisition cost ≤DKK175. mature 90-day repeat ≥35% before street screens. Review the mature-cohort count. insufficient observation postpones the scale decision. No minimum cohort size is silently approved.

M12 repeat target is 50%. M6 35% is the interim scale floor. They are different periods and decisions.

### Tracking proposal

Ads + app/settled trips + dispatch/help → dashboard → weekly actions / monthly cohort review.

Verify Copenhagen data access and integration in M1–2. Consideration uses a separate baseline/endline survey. M12: renew, revise or stop.
