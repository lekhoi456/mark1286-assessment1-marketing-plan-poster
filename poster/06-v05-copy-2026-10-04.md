## 6. From Clicks to Rides

### Sales Strategy

### Awareness

Copenhagen visibility.

Installs: leads, not sales.

### Book

Green SM app → completed, paid trip = sale.

DKK30 first-ride discount.

400-rider cap, DKK12,000 reserve.

### Experience

Target app/driver/car/support after service checks.

### Feedback

Post-ride feedback.

Unpaid review after resolved/well-rated ride.

### Repeat

Consented app reminder.

Voucher + tailored monthly bundle pilots.

Only after observed frequency/unit economics test.

Fund separately.
