## Space allocation

Keep the large cyan Limo Green, upper-right information cloud, narrow safe margin, Copenhagen scenery and 4/4/2 order. Rebalance the internal dividers rather than enlarge the surrounding scenery or shrink the vehicle.

| Row | Target share of usable grid height | Sections and target width shares within that row | Reason |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Top | 32% | 1: 24% · 2: 25% · 3: 29% · 4: 22% | Introduction and segmentation stay compact; positioning needs room for its value and delivery mechanism; identity uses a small visual brand kit. |
| Middle | 40% | 5: 28% · 6: 18% · 7: 26% · 8: 28% | Channels, allocation and measurement need the most detail. The sales journey can use five short illustrated steps in two lines. |
| Bottom | 28% | 9: 55% · 10: 45% | Four innovation pilots need more height; business alignment uses a short outcome-and-growth sequence. |

These percentages are relative targets, not clipping paths. The sloping windows and wheel arches still constrain usable space. Keep divider gaps near 8–10 pixels at the current 1672-pixel canvas width and a small content inset; widen only where an outline needs separation. The current bottom cells are roughly 115 pixels high, against about 182 pixels above. Aim near 140 pixels below and 197 pixels in the middle; retain the vehicle silhouette. Do not force rectangular content across wheel arches.

Confidence: moderate for this allocation; actual fit needs each section reflowed in its final cell. No A0 print proof is claimed.

## Content and artwork synchronisation

1. Review the proposed divider proportions before changing the accepted background.
2. Fit each section into its cell using the current assembly copy as the lettering source. Change icon scale, line wrapping, diagram orientation and local spacing first.
3. Preserve facts, figures, meaning and important qualifiers. Removing assessed content or changing a target is a separate content proposal; an earlier panel acceptance does not accept that change automatically.
4. Keep the index's copy and artwork versions separate until the amended lettering is rendered. A new preview becomes latest only after its version, copy and source links are updated together.

The approved voucher amendment is now in Sections 5 v10, 6 v07 and 7 v05: a first-ride 10% versus 20% test, maximum DKK30, one first paid ride per rider, maximum 400 riders and DKK12,000 reserve ceiling. The reserve is a ceiling, not forecast redemption spend. The older images still need amended lettering.

For Section 8, propose one compact tracking strip: **Ads + app/trips + dispatch/help → dashboard → weekly actions / monthly review**. Consideration uses a separate baseline/endline survey. Corporate data capability is verified internally in E-207; Copenhagen feed access and connection remain proposed. ## Shared drawing direction

Use hand-drawn navy outlines, flat New Cyan and New Yellow accents, cream cells, simple pictograms and very few filled colour regions. Draw every logo in the same style; Green SM logos appear only in 1, 3 and 4. Use one controlled heading/body lettering system, consistent number badges, line weight and arrow style. Avoid gradients, textured fills and small decorative detail. Draw the Section 4 liquid-glass app cue simply without applying a glass effect across the whole poster.

The 16:9 illustration must be placed proportionately on the A0 landscape page. Resolve the remaining page area in the later assembly proof; do not stretch or crop the accepted car.
