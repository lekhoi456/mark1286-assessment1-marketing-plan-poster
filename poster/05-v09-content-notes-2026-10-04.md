## Brief fit

Section 5 addresses Digital Marketing Tactics. Make 5.3 the main visual: three acquisition branches (paid search, SEO and social content) join the Copenhagen information page, move into Green SM app booking and continue through the ride, help and consented repeat. Show a search specimen, a fare/booking FAQ specimen and a short social-video specimen, rather than channel names alone. The same verified fare, offer and help information should persist across touchpoints. “Electric taxi Copenhagen” is an illustrative search query, not measured search volume. Social platform selection and message performance remain to be tested with target riders.

The separate data loop is trip history → likely travel need → relevant reminder with consent → paid repeat. A likely need is a hypothesis, not a known intention. Begin a pilot with available, consented first-party records and simple rules. The named technology proposals have distinct roles:

- CDP: a persistent, unified customer record joining trip, app and consent data, accessible to relevant systems. Do not label a single trip spreadsheet a deployed CDP.
- Big Data: analyse joined activity patterns only when sufficient volume, quality and access justify it. Neither n=79 nor n=1,005 is itself Big Data.
- Marketing AI: suggest reminder timing and message variants for human review and testing. No local Green SM capability or uplift is asserted.

Use small consent, privacy and access icons beside this loop. Keep detailed checks here, not in a large poster GATE box. The technology proposals depend on data/system readiness. Measure paid repeats, not only opens or clicks.

The evidence does not establish willingness to buy a 30-day package, an optimal discount or preferred social platform.
