```plaintext
| Total | 600000 | 100.00 | Sum of allocations |

Allocation logic: local delivery and paid trial lead. Street screens wait for repeat evidence.

People: 460 company hours across research/service checks, local creative, campaign management and CRM/help-route handover. Assumed cost: DKK600/hour. 

Cars, drivers, charging and frontline support sit outside this marketing budget.

Search and social are proposed envelopes, not platform quotes. Analytics/CRM tools assume DKK2,500 monthly. Voucher reserve: 10% versus 20% first-ride test, maximum DKK30 per rider, 400-rider cap. DKK12,000 is the reserve ceiling.

Screens: 200,000 planned impressions ÷ 1,000 × published DKK191 CPM = DKK38,200, plus DKK4,995 setup. This uses a 2026 rate-card estimate, not a supplier quote. 

Five release stages. Before Gate A at M4: DKK177,000 exposed. Before Gate B at M6: DKK265,000 exposed. Fail: hold, fix, retest. DKK335,000 remains unreleased at M6, including the separate DKK40,805 contingency. Screens also require ≥35% mature 90-day repeat. Section 8 holds the full gate thresholds. 

Illustrative year one: 378 first riders, about 676 rides. At a DKK227 fare proxy and assumed 30% contribution, about DKK554,000 remains unrecovered. Break-even requires 8,811 rides. The illustrative year does not fund itself. No lifetime value is claimed.
```
