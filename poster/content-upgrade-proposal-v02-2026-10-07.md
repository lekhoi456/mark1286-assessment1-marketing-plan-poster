## 2. The thread (one insight, used in every cell)

**Insight.** Danes often choose green transport (41% regularly use walking, cycling, public transport or car-sharing instead of a private car; E-038), and app riders choose on price (57%; E-024). They take a taxi when the bike stays home, and they want that ride to be green and fairly priced.

**Position.** Same published fares as Dantaxi and Drivr (E-213), 100% electric (E-011), and one company runs app, car and driver (E-186). Riders pay no more for a better-run electric ride.

**Campaign.** *Når cyklen bliver hjemme* (When the bike stays home): rain, late-night and luggage moments, triggered by weather and time. Search catches *bestil taxa*; social shows the moment; the page says *Tjek prisen. Book i appen.*; the first ride is DKK15 or DKK30 off; repeat comes from service, reminders and referral.
