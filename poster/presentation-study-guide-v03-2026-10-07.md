## The argument to remember

**A 590-car fleet needs a city-wide entry → a focus segment that leads three others → one service standard at parity fares → Copenhagen, meet Green SM → paid trips and repeat → money released only through gates.**

The cyan arrows on the poster are the argument. Point along them when moving from one section to the next.

## Cue cards by section

### Overview

**Remember:** Nov 2026–Oct 2027 / 590 registered cars / DKK30M in stages / arrows / proposals, not results.

**Anchor:** We sized the plan to fill a fleet, not to run a pilot.

### 1. Company/Business Introduction

**Remember:** Vietnam / March 2023 / Vingroup / app–fleet–drivers / Denmark 30 July 2026 = 1st European market / 590 = potential capacity / regulator: significant marketing investment.

**Anchor:** That is why our budget is sized for the fleet, not for a test.

The 590 figure is registrations, not cars in service. The regulator's sentence is an assessment of what entry requires, not a budget figure.

### 2. Target Market Analysis

**Remember:** focus 25–44 (self-paying, taxi users, recurring trips) / 269,277 = 40.2% / ~100,000 regular riders = 38% of 25–44 / four segments: focus M1+, all adults M1+, business M4+, visitors + airport M6+ / 57% price / 41% green habit / trip control (hypothesis).

**Anchor:** One segment cannot fill the fleet.

269,277 is a pool, not a forecast. The 57% is a Capital Region subsample of 79 (±11 points), multiple answers allowed. The 41% is a national Danish figure for walking, cycling, public transport or car-sharing, not demand for electric taxis.

### 3. Positioning Strategy

**Remember:** electric rides, one managed experience / 100% electric / one company runs app, car + driver / Uber + Dantaxi 40–50% (revenue, Jan–May 2025) / partner operators / parity: booking, electric options, DKK39–42 fares / edge: one service standard.

**Anchor:** Ownership gives control; it does not by itself prove better service.

Do not claim rivals lack professional drivers or support. The Dantaxi sale is required, not completed. Bolt's fares are not in the published comparison.

### 4. Branding and Identity

**Remember:** existing identity / Copenhagen, meet Green SM = launch campaign / Go Green, For a Green Future / København, mød Green SM / Elektrisk taxa. Tjek prisen. Book i appen.

**Anchor:** The campaign invites the first ride; the service has to earn the second.

The group applies an existing identity; it did not design the logo or font. Both Danish lines need a native speaker's check before printing.

### 5. Digital Marketing Tactics

**Remember:** 70–90% have 0–1 taxi apps (n=1,005) / 57% price, 22% habit (n=79) / 4Ps / search → installs, Instagram + Facebook → CTR, screens + video → awareness, page → visits, CRM → repeat / DKK15 vs DKK30, one per rider / M1–3 test, M4/M6 gates, M7–12 scale.

**Anchor:** The evidence points to one battle: the app on the phone.

70–90% adds two published ranges (0 apps 30–40%, 1 app 40–50%); never quote a midpoint. Platforms are the group's choice; no registered source gives platform shares for Copenhagen.

### 6. Sales Strategies

**Remember:** Discover → First paid ride (now or 30 days ahead) → Experience → Feedback (unpaid reviews) → Repeat (reminders, referral, commuter pass) / app · B2B via LinkedIn M4+ · partners M6+.

**Anchor:** Success is a completed paid ride, not a download.

### 7. Budget and Resource Allocation

**Remember:** DKK30.0M / objective-and-task / 450 cars × 15 trips/day × DKK200 ≈ DKK493M → about 6% / bars 7.0 · 6.0 · 5.0 · 4.6 · 2.4 · 2.0 · 1.0 · 2.0 / caps DKK7.5M (M4), DKK13.5M (M6), DKK16.5M held.

**Anchor:** Our earlier DKK600,000 was about 0.1 per cent: a pilot, not a market entry.

Working: 450 × 15 × 365 = 2,463,750 trips × DKK200 = DKK492.8M; DKK30M ÷ DKK492.8M = 6.1%. Screens: 20M ÷ 1,000 × DKK191 = DKK3.82M (list rate; excludes the DKK4,995 setup fee and production). Incentives: 150,000 × DKK30 = DKK4.5M ≤ DKK4.6M. Held at M6: DKK14.5M scale release + DKK2.0M contingency = DKK16.5M; DKK13.5M + DKK16.5M = DKK30.0M. The base case is the group's assumption, not a Green SM figure.

### 8. Measurement and Evaluation

**Remember:** ≥50% aided awareness (n≈400 per wave) / 150,000 first paid riders / 2.5M paid trips (15/car/day average) / ≥50% 90-day repeat / media CAC ≤DKK100 / M4: 25,000 + CAC ≤DKK120 / M6: 55,000 + ≥10 trips/car/day + ≥35% repeat / ≥100 business accounts.

**Anchor:** Ten is a floor for releasing money, not the plan.

Media CAC = (search + social) ÷ first paid riders = DKK13M ÷ 150,000 = DKK86.7; all-in DKK30M ÷ 150,000 = DKK200. 2.5M trips ≈ 450 cars × 15 × 365. Riders: 2.46M trips ÷ 24 trips a year ≈ 102,700 regular riders; × 1.5 for churn ≈ 154,000 first riders, rounded to the 150,000 target. A repeat rate needs complete ninety-day follow-up.

### 9. Creativity and Innovation

**Remember:** weather + night triggers / dispatch vs local baseline / one trip ID + FAQ bot → human / pay-per-trip vs commuter pass / calm-ride vs fare-first test / unpriced.

**Anchor:** A faster match is worthless if service falls.

No brain-imaging research is claimed. Each pilot needs its own costing and data access.

### 10. Alignment with Business Objectives

**Remember:** stable operation (service gates) / local recognition (campaign + 50%) / green and smart (electric, app-booked) / sustainable growth (repeat + trips per car) / renew–revise–stop.

**Anchor:** Does Copenhagen come back for a second ride?

## Short defence answers for likely questions

| Question | Direct answer |
|---|---|
| Why DKK30 million, not DKK600,000? | Two methods agree: objective and task (about 150,000 first riders to fill the base case) and about 6% of base-case revenue. DKK600,000 would be about 0.1% of revenue, a pilot. The regulator says awareness needs significant marketing investment. |
| Is the base case realistic? | It is a labelled assumption. At 10 trips per car per day, revenue falls to about DKK329M and the budget becomes about 9% of it; the M4 and M6 gates hold money back if demand is lower. |
| Green SM plans 3,000 drivers; why not plan for that? | That ambition was reported in the press and publicly doubted; it is not registered evidence. The 590 registered cars are the documented capacity, and the regulator calls even those potential capacity. |
| Why still focus on 25–44? | It is the largest adult group and the lecturer asked for an age priority. The other three segments are added in stages, because one segment cannot fill the fleet. |
| Why do the M6 trips floor and the target differ? | Fifteen is the planned average; ten is the minimum for releasing more money. Below it, we hold the next release and diagnose. |
| Does DKK100 include all costs? | No. Media CAC counts search and social only, about DKK87 in the plan. The whole budget per first rider is about DKK200. |
| Price-conscious riders but a service position? | Fares are at parity, so price cannot differentiate. The offer lowers trial risk; the service standard earns repeat. |
| How is Green SM different from Viggo, which is also electric? | Electric options are shared. The regulator says existing dispatch offices have no employed drivers; Green SM runs app, fleet and drivers itself. |
| Can the budget fund the AI pilots? | No. They are unpriced and need separate costing and data access. |
| What if the month-six floors are missed? | Hold the scale release, diagnose the weak measure and revise. Incomplete ninety-day windows postpone the decision. |

These are preparation questions, not questions known to have been asked by the lecturer. The group has not yet assigned Q&A owners.

## How to learn and rehearse

1. Learn the one-sentence argument, then each anchor.
2. Walk the arrows on the poster and explain each link in one sentence.
3. Practise the budget working and the three money gates until they come without notes.
4. Run a timed group rehearsal with pointing and handovers. Record actual times before claiming the pitch fits fifteen minutes.
5. If a section runs long, cut repeated explanation; keep sample sizes, the base-case assumption and the CAC definition.
