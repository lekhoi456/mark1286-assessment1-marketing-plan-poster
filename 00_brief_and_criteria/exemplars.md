## 2. What to borrow — and what not to infer

### Clear hierarchy, not more decoration

Zara's ruled grid and Tinder's bounded panels show how a reader can locate topics without reading every line. Forte's whitespace offers another approach. For our A0 proof, the eleven exact brief headings remain the contract; we can vary panel layout without shortening or replacing them. “Presentation Quality” is a review dimension, not a twelfth content panel.

This is a design lesson, not evidence that one layout received a better mark. A0 gives more area than A1 as a paper standard, but the images do not establish the originals' size, so no sample-specific enlargement ratio or font size is inferred.

### Meaningful visualisation, not a logo in place of analysis

Tinder's lower-right pie chart is visibly a budget-style graphic; Zara's small positioning sketch is visibly a comparative visual; Forte and Travel Cubs use products/animals to convey business category. These are possible forms, not verified data or approved strategic choices. Our visuals must have legible labels, units, sources or assumption labels where applicable, and the numbers must agree with the approved plan.

Forte's short content labels illustrate a risk: a prompt to discuss the background is not the background itself. Likewise, Spotify's central topic/navigation labels help orientation but do not themselves supply marketing analysis.

### Business-specific identity, not a substitute for coherent strategy

The grooming, wildlife-tourism, dating and music motifs visibly match each poster's named subject. An A0 design can use a similarly coherent visual vocabulary once the group has chosen a business and approved the identity. This does not mean the photographed messaging, target segments, positioning or commercial claims are evidence-backed. Do not adopt them because they appear on a sample.

### Budgets need an allocation, not a financial headline

Zara's lower-centre budget lists percentage ranges, which do not by themselves establish a single reconciled allocation. Forte's lower-left budget lists percentage lines, but the photograph supplies no verified cost model. Spotify's bottom “Sales Budget” card reports a revenue figure and subscriber geography rather than a visible marketing spending plan. Our budget must state its total, currency and period, reconcile amounts and shares, and distinguish a sourced cost from a planning assumption. This is an operational response to criterion C4, not a grade judgement about these samples.

### Metrics need objectives and evaluation

The samples visibly label measurement/metrics areas; Forte lists items such as conversion, acquisition cost and retention, while Zara and Spotify refer to tracking/analytics. A metric label alone does not show a target, a method or a review decision. Our working tests add those operational details under C5 while recognising they are proposed implementation checks, not official grade-band wording.

## 3. A0 adaptation and verification

| Design/release question | Working rule | Proof required later |
|---|---|---|
| Can a marker locate every required element? | Use all eleven canonical heading names, with a deliberate reading route. | Compare actual A0 proof with `poster-headings.txt`; check from ordinary viewing distance. |
| Is larger paper being used for clearer information? | Allocate room to analysis, charts, identifiers and citations before ornamental borders or oversized logos. Do not simply double the text. | Inspect physical-size proof and photograph/export separately. |
| Do illustrations support the plan? | Use category/brand motifs only after the business and strategy are approved. | Trace each substantive graphic to an approved claim, calculation or labelled illustration. |
| Is the content legible in the submitted picture? | Avoid dependence on tiny handwriting, shadows, glare, curled paper or low-contrast colours. | Inspect the actual clear image intended for upload; no current image proves future legibility. |
| Is the budget a plan rather than a headline? | Show a reconciled visual allocation with costs/assumptions and resource fit. | Recalculate budget and compare every figure across Markdown and design. |
| Are references and identities included? | Reserve space for three exact names/numbers and confirmed contributions; resolve reverse/end reference-list delivery. | Inspect both the physical poster/reference side and the digital submission package. |
| Is the hand-drawn requirement met? | Keep source wording and production-route risk explicit. These photos visibly show handmade-looking lettering/drawings, but do not establish permission for AI-made equivalents. | Lecturer/policy clarification and truthful production record; visual resemblance alone is not approval. |
| Is the final text trustworthy? | Approve Markdown first; place it using a layout tool, never image-model text generation. | Exact text comparison against the approved version, including citations and numbers. |

## 4. Boundaries for use

The adopted working size is A0 under student-relayed lecturer guidance (D-007); the AI-made hand-drawn-style route remains a group decision with compliance unconfirmed (D-008). The samples provide no assurance on that issue.
