Welcome to the module
Dear Students,

A very warm welcome to the Marketing and Sales in the Future Economy module! 

Welcome to the exciting journey of exploring the dynamic world of sales and marketing in the future economy! This module has been designed to equip you, the next generation of business leaders, with the knowledge and skills necessary to thrive in an ever-evolving marketplace driven by technological innovation, shifting consumer behavior, and globalization.

In this 12-week module, we will embark on a comprehensive exploration of sales and marketing strategies tailored to the demands of the ever-changing economy. The module will cover an understanding of consumer behavior in the digital age to harness the power of emerging technologies, each week through (3 hours of engagement):

	•	1-hour pre-recorded lecture (available via video on Moodle)
	•	2-hour on-campus tutorial,

Besides self-study time, the module  will also offer valuable insights and practical tools to help you navigate the complexities of modern business landscapes with confidence and proficiency. This module is designed to provide you with a holistic understanding of the key principles, new trends, and strategies shaping the future of sales and marketing, empowering you to drive innovation and achieve sustainable business growth in a competitive environment.

Throughout the module, you will engage in a blend of theoretical discussions, real-world case studies, hands-on workshops, and interactive group activities. This experiential learning approach is aimed at fostering critical thinking, collaboration, and problem-solving skills essential for success in sales and marketing roles. Additionally, summative assessments will offer opportunities for self-reflection and feedback, enabling you to track your progress and deepen your understanding of the subject matter.

At the heart of this module lies a commitment to excellence and ethical conduct in sales and marketing practices. As future business leaders, it is incumbent upon us to uphold the highest standards of integrity, transparency, and social responsibility. Throughout our journey together, we will explore the ethical dimensions of sales and marketing, engaging in thoughtful discussions and exercises to cultivate a sense of ethical awareness and responsibility in our professional endeavors.

Moreover, this module is designed to be interactive and student-centered, with ample opportunities for collaboration, peer learning, and mentorship. I encourage you to actively participate in class discussions, share your insights and experiences, and leverage the diverse perspectives of your peers to enrich your learning experience.

In conclusion, I am thrilled to embark on this learning journey with you, as we unravel the mysteries of sales and marketing in the future economy. Together, let us embrace the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead, armed with knowledge, creativity, and a relentless pursuit of excellence. I am confident that with dedication, curiosity, and a growth mindset, you will emerge from this module as confident, competent, and ethical leaders ready to shape the future of sales and marketing.

Let the adventure begin!

Aims:
The module aims to prepare students to adapt to emerging trends and changes in the future economy of digitalization and sustainability and enhance their understanding of knowledge and skills needed to succeed as marketing and sales professionals in this rapidly evolving landscape.

Learning Outcomes:
On successful completion of this course a student will be able to: 
	•	Synthetize and interpret the impact of emerging trends, technologies, and customer behavior on marketing and sales in the future economy.
	•	Develop relevant skills in designing effective marketing and sales strategies that align with the globalisation trends.
	•	Analyze the leverage of digital marketing, e-commerce, omni-channel sales, customer experience, branding, and marketing analytics global context.
	•	Critically evaluate the role of AI, and emerging technologies in shaping future marketing and sales strategies.
	•	Apply concepts and principles to real-world business situations, gaining hands-on experience and developing practical skills.
Enquiry based learning and research-led teaching
Enquiry-Based Learning (EBL):
Defined as ‘an approach based on self-directed enquiry or investigation in which the student is actively engaged in the process of enquiry facilitated by a teacher. EBL uses real life scenarios (for example, from case studies, industry information, and project work) and students investigate topics of relevance that foster the skills of experimental design, data collection, critical analysis and problem-solving’. This module uses cases studies and industry databases where you will test your knowledge and develop your critical skills to become more employable. 

Research-Led Teaching (RLT):
An element of Enquiry Based Learning links to RLT, which involves faculty introducing students to their own research where it is relevant to the curriculum being taught as well as drawing on their own knowledge of research developments in the field, introducing them to the work of other researchers. RLT sees students as active participants in the research process, not just as an audience. This is achieved by discussing such developments in lectures and classes, and setting reading lists including recent research publications at the frontier of the field. The definition of a diverse assessment regime at the program level (incorporating an expectation of familiarity with, and use of, such publications in assignments) and the inclusion of projects at every level of the program is also fundamental to achieving these objectives.

The module team is research active and many examples will be used from their publications and projects. This will help you to better comprehend the link between theory and practice.
Employability skills gained
Undertaking this module on sales and marketing for the future economy will equip students with a diverse set of employability skills and competences essential for success in various roles within the business sector. Here are some of the key skills and competences that students can expect to develop:

	•	Strategic Thinking: Students will learn to analyze market trends, identify opportunities, and develop strategic marketing plans aligned with business goals and objectives.
	•	Digital Marketing Proficiency: Mastery of digital marketing channels and tools, including AI, social media, content marketing, SEO, and email marketing, enabling students to create and execute effective digital marketing campaigns.
	•	Data Analysis and Interpretation: Students will develop the ability to analyze marketing data, extract meaningful insights, and make data-driven decisions to optimize marketing strategies and campaigns.
	•	Consumer Behavior Understanding: Understanding of consumer behavior in the digital age, including purchasing patterns, decision-making processes, and the influence of technology on consumer preferences.
	•	Brand Management: Proficiency in building, managing, and enhancing brand equity through effective branding strategies, positioning, and storytelling techniques.
	•	Sales Skills: Development of essential sales skills, including prospecting, negotiation, relationship building, and objection handling, to drive revenue growth and client acquisition.
	•	Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Understanding of CRM systems and strategies to effectively manage customer relationships, enhance customer satisfaction, and drive customer loyalty.
	•	Omnichannel Marketing: Proficiency in designing and implementing omnichannel marketing strategies to deliver seamless and personalized customer experiences across multiple channels.
	•	Communication Skills: Strong verbal and written communication skills to articulate marketing strategies, engage with stakeholders, and convey brand messages effectively.
	•	Creativity and Innovation: Ability to think creatively and develop innovative marketing solutions to differentiate products/services, capture market share, and stay ahead of competitors.
	•	Ethical Decision-Making: Awareness of ethical considerations in sales and marketing practices, including privacy, transparency, and social responsibility, and the ability to make ethical decisions in complex situations.
	•	Adaptability and Agility: Agility to adapt to changing market dynamics, technological advancements, and emerging trends, and the ability to pivot marketing strategies accordingly.
	•	Collaboration and Teamwork: Collaboration skills to work effectively in cross-functional teams, leverage diverse perspectives, and achieve common goals in a dynamic business environment.
	•	Problem-Solving Skills: Analytical and critical thinking skills to identify marketing challenges, evaluate alternative solutions, and implement effective problem-solving strategies.
	•	Leadership Potential: Development of leadership qualities such as vision, influence, and resilience, preparing students for leadership roles in sales and marketing departments or as entrepreneurs.

Assessments tasks
The overall pass mark for this module is 50%.

Assessment 1: Marketing Plan Poster 
Group Poster Presentations (50% weighting)
 
For this assessment, during a 1–2-week Marketing Business Bootcamp, students will work in groups to develop a comprehensive marketing strategy for a real or hypothetical business. Each group will create a visually appealing poster summarizing their strategy, including key elements such as consumer analysis (target market analysis, positioning, branding), digital marketing tactics and sales strategies, budgeting and KPIs. The poster presentation will allow students to showcase their understanding of course concepts and their ability to apply them to real-world business scenarios.

All group members must actively participate in the presentation and submit the same poster on Moodle through their individual accounts. Failure to submit will result in a non-marked assignment, leading to automatic failure.

Assessment 2: Individual Vlog 
Video on Emerging Marketing Trends (50% weighting)

For this assessment, each student will create a 5–7-minute (maximum 8 mins) individual vlog discussing emerging trends and innovations in sales and marketing. Students will research and explore one of the topics such as AI-driven marketing, personalisation and customization of customer experiences, sustainable and ethical marketing practices, and the impact of technology (AI, blockchain etc) on marketing and sales strategies. In their vlog, students will analyse the real implications of these trends for businesses and share insights on how organisations can adapt their marketing campaign strategies to capitalise on these emerging opportunities.

Assessment 1: Details of Marketing Plan Poster (Group Presentation):

The presentation will be a pitch delivered in front of the class, lasting 15 minutes, followed by 5 minutes for questions (maximum 20 mins with Q/A). All group members must participate in the presentation and submit the poster (Hand-drawn paper chart). The group poster must be submitted on Moodle for grading. When submitting on Turnitin, include your tutorial room, date/time, and your tutor's name in the subject line. Make sure to include the names and student numbers of all group members on the poster and clearly indicate which part each member contributed (if applicable).

The poster must include at least the following (precise) information:

	•	For this assessment, students will participate in a 1–2-week Marketing Business Bootcamp, working in groups to develop a comprehensive marketing strategy for either a real (market) or hypothetical business from any country.

 1.     Student/Business Details:
Students should complete their information as per the University registration system, including the name of the company/business associated with their assignment.
2.     Company/Business Introduction:
What is the background of the company, e.g. could be from any industry? What are the locations or context of its operations e.g. could be from any country? What products and services does it offer?
3.     Target Market Analysis:
Have you clearly identified and described your target market(s)? Have you provided demographic, psychographic, and behavioural characteristics of your target audience? How did you segment and prioritize your target markets?
4.     Positioning Strategy:
Have you defined a clear positioning statement for the brand/product/service? How does your positioning differentiate your brand/product/service from competitors? Have you considered the unique value proposition (UVP) that resonates with your target audience?
5.     Branding and Identity:
How have you developed the brand identity, including logo, colour scheme, and brand messaging? Does your branding strategy align with the desired positioning and target market?
6.     Digital Marketing Tactics:
What digital marketing channels and tactics have you chosen to reach your target audience? How do you plan to leverage social media, content marketing, SEO, and other digital platforms to engage with customers?
7.     Sales Strategies:
What sales channels and techniques will you employ to drive revenue and customer acquisition? Have you outlined the sales process, including lead generation, prospecting, and conversion strategies?
8.     Budget and Resource Allocation:
Have you developed a realistic budget for implementing your marketing strategy? How do you plan to allocate resources across various marketing channels and activities?
9.     Measurement and Evaluation:
What metrics and KPIs will you use to measure the success of your marketing strategy? How will you track and analyse the performance of your marketing campaigns?
10.  Creativity and Innovation:
Have you demonstrated creativity and innovation in your marketing approach? How have you incorporated new and emerging trends in your marketing strategy including AI, personalization, omnichannel, neuro marketing, subscription and revenue model?
11.  Alignment with Business Objectives:
How does your marketing strategy align with the overall business goals and objectives? Have you considered the long-term sustainability and growth potential of your marketing initiatives?

	•	Presentation Quality:
Is the hand-drawn poster visually appealing, well-organized, and easy to understand? Have you effectively communicated your marketing strategy through visuals, graphics, and text?

If applicable - Cite all your sources in the poster. Use only reliable and credible references. Include a reference list at the back of your chart paper.
Incorporate/draw relevant graphs and tables to support your content - these will enhance the visual appeal and clarity of your work.
