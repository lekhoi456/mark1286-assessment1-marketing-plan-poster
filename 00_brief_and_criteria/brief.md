## 1. Handbook wording — verbatim

### Learning outcomes assessed (H §3; text lines 71–74)

On successful completion of this course a student will be able to:

- Synthetize and interpret the impact of emerging trends, technologies, and customer behavior on marketing and sales in the future economy.
- Develop relevant skills in designing effective marketing and sales strategies that align with the globalisation trends.

> Note: these are LO1 and LO2 in source order. The A1 assessment schedule maps to outcomes “1, 2”.

### Assessment task (H §7; PDF p. 7; text lines 207–214)

The overall pass mark for this module is 50%.

Assessment 1: Marketing Plan Poster

Group Poster Presentations (50% weighting)

For this assessment, during a 1–2-week Marketing Business Bootcamp, students will work in groups to develop a comprehensive marketing strategy for a real or hypothetical business. Each group will create a visually appealing poster summarizing their strategy, including key elements such as consumer analysis (target market analysis, positioning, branding), digital marketing tactics and sales strategies, budgeting and KPIs. The poster presentation will allow students to showcase their understanding of course concepts and their ability to apply them to real-world business scenarios.

All group members must actively participate in the presentation and submit the same poster on Moodle through their individual accounts. Failure to submit will result in a non-marked assignment, leading to automatic failure.

### Assessment 1: Details of Marketing Plan Poster (Group Presentation) (H §7; PDF pp. 8–9; text lines 256–291)

The presentation will be a pitch delivered in front of the class, lasting 15 minutes, followed by 5 minutes for questions (maximum 20 mins with Q/A). All group members must participate in the presentation and submit the poster (Hand-drawn paper chart). The group poster must be submitted on Moodle for grading. When submitting on Turnitin, include your tutorial room, date/time, and your tutor's name in the subject line. Make sure to include the names and student numbers of all group members on the poster and clearly indicate which part each member contributed (if applicable).

The poster must include at least the following (precise) information:

For this assessment, students will participate in a 1–2-week Marketing Business Bootcamp, working in groups to develop a comprehensive marketing strategy for either a real (market) or hypothetical business from any country.

#### 1. Student/Business Details:

Students should complete their information as per the University registration system, including the name of the company/business associated with their assignment.

#### 2. Company/Business Introduction:

What is the background of the company, e.g. could be from any industry? What are the locations or context of its operations e.g. could be from any country? What products and services does it offer?

#### 3. Target Market Analysis:

Have you clearly identified and described your target market(s)? Have you provided demographic, psychographic, and behavioural characteristics of your target audience? How did you segment and prioritize your target markets?

#### 4. Positioning Strategy:

Have you defined a clear positioning statement for the brand/product/service? How does your positioning differentiate your brand/product/service from competitors? Have you considered the unique value proposition (UVP) that resonates with your target audience?

#### 5. Branding and Identity:

How have you developed the brand identity, including logo, colour scheme, and brand messaging? Does your branding strategy align with the desired positioning and target market?

#### 6. Digital Marketing Tactics:

What digital marketing channels and tactics have you chosen to reach your target audience? How do you plan to leverage social media, content marketing, SEO, and other digital platforms to engage with customers?

#### 7. Sales Strategies:

What sales channels and techniques will you employ to drive revenue and customer acquisition? Have you outlined the sales process, including lead generation, prospecting, and conversion strategies?

#### 8. Budget and Resource Allocation:

Have you developed a realistic budget for implementing your marketing strategy? How do you plan to allocate resources across various marketing channels and activities?

#### 9. Measurement and Evaluation:

What metrics and KPIs will you use to measure the success of your marketing strategy? How will you track and analyse the performance of your marketing campaigns?

#### 10. Creativity and Innovation:

Have you demonstrated creativity and innovation in your marketing approach? How have you incorporated new and emerging trends in your marketing strategy including AI, personalization, omnichannel, neuro marketing, subscription and revenue model?

#### 11. Alignment with Business Objectives:

How does your marketing strategy align with the overall business goals and objectives? Have you considered the long-term sustainability and growth potential of your marketing initiatives?

#### Presentation Quality:

Is the hand-drawn poster visually appealing, well-organized, and easy to understand? Have you effectively communicated your marketing strategy through visuals, graphics, and text?

If applicable - Cite all your sources in the poster. Use only reliable and credible references. Include a reference list at the back of your chart paper.

Incorporate/draw relevant graphs and tables to support your content - these will enhance the visual appeal and clarity of your work.
