## Assessment metadata and review use

A1 weighs 50% of the module, assesses LO1–LO2, is non-anonymous and has an anticipated feedback return of 15 working days (H schedule). H gives the **module** pass mark as 50%; do not relabel that as a newly invented criterion threshold. Non-submission through a student's own account leads to non-marking/automatic failure in H's wording. All seven criterion weights are preserved and total 100% of A1 in `rubric.md`.

Review for the local marker and UK moderator using the same traceable evidence. There are no supplied official criterion-level band descriptors and no exemplar grades. Do not assign made-up “Exceptional” thresholds. Where a source or decision is ambiguous, retain the requirement and the unresolved status until actual clarification is recorded.
