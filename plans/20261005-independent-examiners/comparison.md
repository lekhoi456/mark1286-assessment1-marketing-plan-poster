## Convergent findings

1. Ten marketing panels plus the student/business cloud cover the required eleven groups. Budget totals, staffing hours, voucher ceiling and population percentage reconcile. All assessors award 14/20 for visual/presentation: strong visual identity and coherent diagrams, but small supporting text and extensive decorative area.
2. Segmentation is visible, but the population count does not establish why ages 25–44 should be prioritised. Digital channels need a concrete campaign example and an explicit acquisition rationale connecting activity, spend and proposed riders.
3. Budget and KPIs need a shared cost definition. DKK198,000 Search + Social / 1,200 first paid riders = DKK165; this is not automatically an arithmetic error. The visible KPI should clarify its numerator and cohort.
4. AI dispatch/chatbot/subscription pilots have mechanisms and honest proposal labels, but resourcing, priority, costing and success/stop rules remain insufficiently specified on the poster.
