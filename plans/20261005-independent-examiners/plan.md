# Independent poster examination

Date: 5 October 2026. Requested by the student. Status: complete. All three independent reports and comparison are saved.

Input: original Assessment 1 brief, official Assessment 1 rubric only, and saved poster-references-v02 A0 PDF/PNG. Each examiner starts with no conversation history and the same task; no internal checklist or earlier assessment is supplied. Inputs are copied into an isolated packet and checksummed in input-manifest.json. Each examiner owns one report; the poster stays unchanged.

Deliverable: three separate weighted criterion scores and totals, observed strengths/gaps, unobserved presentation elements and a comparison of conclusions. This is an independent formative evaluation, not an institutional awarded mark.
