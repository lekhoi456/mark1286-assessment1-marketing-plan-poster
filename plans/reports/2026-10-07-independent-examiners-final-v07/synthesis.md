## Agreed by all three

1. **No sales process for business accounts or partners** (C3). *B2B via LinkedIn (M4+) · partners (M6+)* is all that supports DKK2.0M, 2 FTE and ≥100 accounts. The examiners rank this first or second and estimate +1.0 to +1.5 for a four- to five-step pipeline line.
2. **The edge is operational, not a rider benefit; the message does not answer price** (C2). *Edge: Direct control → one service standard*; *Go Green* leads on electric, which Section 3 lists under parity; there is no formal positioning statement.
3. **Segments overlap.** *2 All adults · M1+* contains *1 Focus: 25–44 · M1+* and starts at the same time.
4. **590 registered vs 450 cars** is unexplained.
5. **The budget base and the timing.** *Objective-and-task · 6% of run-rate revenue* reads as two methods for one figure, and the run-rate is an M12 level. Denmark launched on *30/07/2026*, but the plan runs Nov 2026–Oct 2027 under a *Launch* title.
6. **Incentive headroom.** 150,000 × ≤DKK30 = DKK4.5M of the DKK4.6M line, so referral credit and the commuter pass test look unfunded if the DKK30 offer wins. The expected cost at the DKK22.50 average is DKK3.4M, but the poster does not say so.
7. **CAC label.** The formula says *(search + social)*, but the bar says *Search + app*.
8. **150,000 first riders** equals 56% of the 25–44 group; the split across segments is not shown.
9. **Unpriced pilots**, dense shorthand and small type; the survey figures cannot be traced to a reference.

Single-examiner points: *57%* is used both as a reason (Capital Region n=79) and as a segment trait (E1, E2). *40.2% of city* uses the all-ages base beside an 18+ chart (E3). There is no revenue, ROMI or CLV KPI (E2, E3). There is nothing beyond month 12 (E3).

Agreed strengths: the arithmetic reconciles throughout; the gated budget and decision rules; localised Danish tactics; evidence-anchored sizing with stated sample sizes; all brief elements in order; a strong car-as-canvas visual.

## Points that conflict with recorded lecturer guidance (not actioned)

Member contributions (all three; LG-005: not needed). Visible citations on the front (E2, E3; LG-004: not needed). A1 rather than A0 (all three; LG-001: A0). The examiners priced these at +0.5 to +1.5; the lecturer's guidance removes them from the real marking.

## Estimated effect of the remaining low-cost fixes

These are short text edits in existing space: a B2B pipeline line, the edge as a rider benefit plus *Other adults* for segment 2, *Search + app + social* in the CAC formula, a line reconciling 590/450 and the 30/07 launch, and the expected incentive cost. The examiners' own estimates for these sum to about +2 to +3, which would bring the mean to about 74–75. Confidence: low to moderate, because mock markers' estimated gains usually overstate the real change.
