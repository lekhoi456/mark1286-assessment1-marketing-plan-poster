### C1. Company Background and Country Context: 7/10
*Rubric: "Clearly and neatly presents the company's background, country of origin, and core products/services. Demonstrates a clear understanding of context."*

- **Strengths.** Cell 1 is neat and covers the essentials. The house icon marks Vietnam as home with "14/04/2023". The cell also states "Founded March 2023" and "Vingroup-backed", and shows the operating model "Owned fleet (590 registered) · Employed drivers". It names the core offer as "Green SM Taxi: app-booked electric taxi rides in Copenhagen". Eight flags show the company's international footprint, with Denmark highlighted ("30/07/2026") and the Netherlands marked as a "pilot". The VinFast-badged car links the company to its parent group.
- **Limits.** The Danish context is thin. Cell 1 says nothing about the Copenhagen taxi market, its regulation, EV or charging conditions, or why the group chose Copenhagen. The only context comes from the population figures in cell 2 and the competitor map in cell 3. "590 registered" does not say whether it means Denmark, Copenhagen or the whole group, and it is *unverified*: no reference on the back clearly supports it. Five of the country entries (Laos, Indonesia, Philippines, India, Kazakhstan) have no dates or status, so a reader cannot tell live operations from announced ones.
- These gaps keep the mark in the upper-Merit range: the background is clear, but the country context is shallow.

### C2. Target Market, Segmentation & Branding: 10/15
*Rubric: "Target market(s) are well-defined using demographic, psychographic, and behavioural characteristics. Branding elements (e.g., logo, message) are creative and aligned with the target audience."*

- **Demographic.** This part is good. A bar chart titled "Copenhagen residents aged 25–44" shows 18–24 at 72,396, 25–44 at 269,277, 45–64 at 143,668 and 65+ at 74,728. The 25–44 bar is highlighted as "40.2% of city", against "City population (all ages): 670,389" at "1 Jul 2026". The share checks (269,277 ÷ 670,389 = 40.2%), and Statistics Denmark (2026) appears on the back. The figures themselves are *unverified*.
- **Behavioural.** "Existing taxi users", "Recurring local trips", "Self-paying", "Recurring trips → repeat rides" and "Within the service area" are present but terse. "Self-paying" is not explained.
- **Psychographic.** The "Proposed rider profile" lists only three tags: "Price-conscious · Values trip control · Expects fair treatment". There are no lifestyle or attitude descriptors, and nothing about environmental attitudes, even though the brand message is "Go Green".
- **Segmentation and prioritisation.** Prioritisation is implied by "Later: visitors · business accounts", but the poster never says why 25–44 beats 45–64 (143,668). No segments are compared on size, need or accessibility.
- **Branding.** Cell 4 gives a full identity specification: the logo; "Green SM = Green and Smart Mobility"; the line "Go Green For a Green Future."; "New Cyan #28bdbf Pantone 319 C"; "New Yellow #e3bb42"; the typeface "Xanh Display 2.0"; "Modern Liquid Glass UI/UX · Layered · soft · clear"; and "Danish first · English second". However, this is the parent's existing corporate identity, and the poster adds no Copenhagen adaptation of its own. The rubric word "creative" is therefore only weakly met.
- **Alignment.** This is the largest gap. The target is "Price-conscious" and the survey bar shows "Lower price 57%". Yet cell 3 lists "Similar fares" as shared with competitors and claims the edge "Direct control → one service standard". Meanwhile cell 4's message is "Go Green For a Green Future." That is three different lead benefits (price, reliability and green), and the tagline is in English despite "Danish first".

### C4. Budget & Resource Allocation: 11/15
*Rubric: "Presents a visual budget (e.g., pie chart or table) that is well-organized. Demonstrates realistic allocation of resources and feasible budgeting."*

- **Strengths.**
  - Cell 7 presents a well-organised bar-and-table budget: "Proposed DKK600,000 · Nov 2026–Oct 2027 · excluding VAT". The lines are Team 276,000/46%, Search 108,000/18%, Social 90,000/15%, Screens 43,195/7.2%, Tools 30,000/5%, Vouchers 12,000/2% and Contingency 40,805/6.8%. I re-added the lines: they sum to exactly 600,000, and the percentages sum to 100.0.
  - The team line is costed by hours: "460h × DKK600/h*", made up of Checks 60h, Creative/localisation 120h, Campaigns 160h and CRM/help 120h, which comes to 460h and 276,000.
  - Vouchers reconcile with cell 5 at 400 riders × DKK30 = 12,000.
  - Funding is released in stages: "M4 review DKK177,000 → M6 review DKK265,000 … Held at M6 Remaining DKK335,000", and the cell shows "265,000 + 335,000 = 600,000".

- **Weaknesses.**
  - The CAC is narrow. DKK165 equals Search plus Social (198,000) divided by 1,200 first paid riders. It leaves out screens, vouchers and team time. Counting the whole DKK600,000, the cost is about DKK500 per first paid rider, which needs about 7.4 rides at DKK68 to repay.

  - Nearly half the budget goes on team hours at an assumed "DKK600/h* Planning allowance", and "DKK68 contribution" is also assumed with no fare basis.
  - The innovation pilots are "Unpriced … cost separately" (cell 9). As a result, the budget does not cover the full plan shown on the poster.
