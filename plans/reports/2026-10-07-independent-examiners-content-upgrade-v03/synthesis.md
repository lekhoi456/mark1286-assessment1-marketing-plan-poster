## Agreed by all three

1. **No formal positioning statement; positioning and brand do not answer the price-conscious target** (C2, C3). The Section 3 line *Similar fares (Dantaxi/Drivr)* is read as a concession beside *Price-conscious* and *Lower price 57%*, not as the reason to compete on service. *Go Green For a Green Future.* does not differentiate.
2. **Digital and sales tactics are named, not planned** (C3, the largest content weight). No social platform, content format, SEO keyword set or cadence; the app is the only sales channel; the *Screens* budget line has no tactic.
3. **Survey evidence cannot be traced** (brief: cite sources). Question wording and source are not shown, and none of the examiners linked the survey figures to *Ubers køb af Dantaxi* on the back.
4. **Shorthand and small type** (C7): *40–50% one app*, *30-day paid*, *Screens*, *Unpaid review: well-rated/resolved*, *M3–6 Test · M4/M6 checks*.
5. **Unit check flatters the economics** (C4): DKK165 counts search and social only; full plan cost is DKK500 per first paid rider (600,000 / 1,200); the targets imply about 2.1 rides per rider against the 2.4-ride payback.

Agreed strengths: reconciled, gated budget; measurable KPIs with M4/M6/M12 gates; data-based age segment; all eleven elements in brief order; honest labelling of proposals and pilots.
